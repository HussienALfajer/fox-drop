# packages/core_alerts

The native ringing layer of the restaurant app (architecture §11.2, §12.2; RA §4.3–4.5; slice 2.42) and of the captain app (architecture §14.2; CA §4.4–4.5; slice 2.43). Android only so far; Windows (tray, window, toasts, architecture §12.3) comes with its slice, and `NoAlerts` stands in there.

## Layout

- `lib/src/alerts.dart`: `Alerts` (the service, the ring, the chime, the device check's readings and settings pages), `AndroidAlerts` on the channel `fox_drop/core_alerts`, `NoAlerts`, `platformAlerts()`, `RingSound`, `DeviceReadings`, `DeviceSetting`. `AndroidAlerts.wake()` is for a push's background isolate.
- `lib/testing.dart` (tests only): `FakeAlerts`, which records the calls and answers `deviceReadings`.
- `android/src/main/kotlin/com/foxdrop/core_alerts/`: `CoreAlertsPlugin` (the channel; while a ring plays, the activity shows over the lock screen and turns the screen on), `ReceivingService` with `StartReceiver`, `Ringer`, `DeviceCheck`.
- `android/src/main/res/raw/`: the bundled sounds (the captain's offer `fd_offer` too), generated by `tool/placeholder_sounds.py` (owner decision 2026-11-20: placeholders until the design's sounds; generated here, so no licence). `keep.xml` keeps them through resource shrinking, since `Ringer` finds them by name.

## Rules

- **The service (RA §4.3):** `ReceivingService` is a foreground service of type `specialUse` (`dataSync` is limited to 6 hours a day on Android 15; check the Play policy at submission, RA §21 item 4) with the permanent notification «FOX DROP يستقبل طلباتك» and a partial wake lock, so the heartbeat and the ring work with the screen off once the device check turned battery optimisation off. `startReceiving` records that a signed-in device asked; `StartReceiver` starts it again after `BOOT_COMPLETED` and `MY_PACKAGE_REPLACED` only then; `stopReceiving` (sign-out) clears it. A start the system refuses (Android 12 and later restarting it from the background without an exemption) stops the service instead of crashing; `wake` starts it only when it is not running (`ReceivingService.running`), and never throws. The manifest's `<queries>` names the brands' auto-start packages, without which Android 11 and later hide them.
- **One Flutter engine:** `ReceivingService.engine(context)` creates the app's engine once (running `main`) and caches it as `fox_drop_main`; the app's `MainActivity` provides it (`provideFlutterEngine`) and never destroys it (`shouldDestroyEngineWithHost` false), so the board, its connection and its ring outlive the activity, and after a reboot the service runs the app with no activity. An app using this package must do the same.
- **The captain app (slice 2.43):** uses the ring, the chime and the readings, never the receiving service: its manifest removes `ReceivingService`, `StartReceiver` and `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE` (`tools:node="remove"`), since the background location's service of type `location` keeps its process; its `MainActivity` still provides the cached engine (`ReceivingService.engine`). A ring may vibrate while it plays (`vibrate`, 0.8 s on and 0.6 s off on the alarm usage) and stop by itself (`stopAfter`: the offer's end, for a ring an offer's push starts from the push's isolate with no app to stop it); `stopRing` cancels both. `chime(cancellation: true)` plays the cancellation sound once.
- **The ring (RA §4.4):** one `MediaPlayer` looping the sound on `USAGE_ALARM` (the media volume and silent mode do not mute it), a notification on the silent, high-importance channel `core_alerts_ring` with `CATEGORY_ALARM` and a full-screen intent that opens the app; Android 14 and later show it full screen only when the user allowed it (the device check's check 4), heads-up otherwise (owner decision 2026-11-20). A ring already playing changes only its sound or text. A screen wake lock turns the screen on for 30 s. The chime plays once on `USAGE_NOTIFICATION`, never over a ring.
- **The device check's readers (RA §4.5 checks 1–5):** notifications allowed, battery optimisation off (asked directly with `REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS`), full-screen allowed (always below Android 14), the alarm volume in percent, Do Not Disturb silencing alarms (total silence, or priority without alarms), and whether the brand has an auto-start page (Xiaomi, Huawei, Honor, Oppo, Realme, Vivo, Samsung), which no app can read: the user confirms it. `openSettings` opens each check's page, else the app's details.
- **Strings:** the service's and the ring channel's texts are system texts in `res/values/strings.xml` (apps/customer/CLAUDE.md «Arabic»); the ring's own text comes from the app.
- The Kotlin builds with the app (the `android-release` CI job); it has no unit tests. `test/alerts_test.dart` proves the channel's calls.
