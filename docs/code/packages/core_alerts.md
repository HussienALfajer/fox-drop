# packages/core_alerts

> Moved here from `packages/core_alerts/CLAUDE.md` on 2026-11-20 (`docs/claude-code-workflow.md`). In these notes, a folder's `CLAUDE.md` (or "its `CLAUDE.md`") means that folder's file under `docs/code/` when there is one, otherwise the folder's own `CLAUDE.md`; the folder's own `CLAUDE.md` now holds only the rules for working there.

The native ringing layer of the restaurant app (architecture §11.2, §12.2; RA §4.3–4.5; slice 2.42) and of the captain app (architecture §14.2; CA §4.4–4.5; slice 2.43; its iOS layer slice 2.46), and the restaurant app's Windows layer (architecture §12.3; RA §4.3–4.5; slice 2.44).

## Layout

- `lib/src/alerts.dart`: `Alerts` (the service, the ring, the chime, the device check's readings and settings pages), `AndroidAlerts` on the channel `fox_drop/core_alerts`, `NoAlerts`, `platformAlerts()`, `RingSound`, `DeviceReadings` (sealed: `AndroidReadings`, `WindowsReadings`), `DeviceSetting`.
  `AndroidAlerts.wake()` is for a push's background isolate.
- `lib/src/windows_alerts.dart`: `WindowsAlerts` (the tray, the window, the ring, the toasts, the readings) and `TrayTexts`; `windows/core_alerts_plugin.cpp`: its Win32 side on the same channel name.
- `ios/core_alerts/` (slice 2.46): a Swift package (`Package.swift`, no podspec: the captain app builds every plugin through Swift Package Manager) with `CoreAlertsPlugin.swift` on the same channel, and `IosAlerts` and `IosReadings` in `alerts.dart`;
  `ios/sounds/fd_offer_30s.wav`, the offer's loop written out for 29 seconds (`tool/placeholder_sounds.py`), for the notifications, which play a sound once and at most 30 seconds.
- `lib/testing.dart` (tests only): `FakeAlerts`, which records the calls and answers `deviceReadings`.
- `android/src/main/kotlin/com/foxdrop/core_alerts/`: `CoreAlertsPlugin` (the channel; while a ring plays, the activity shows over the lock screen and turns the screen on), `ReceivingService` with `StartReceiver`, `Ringer`, `DeviceCheck`.
- `android/src/main/res/raw/`: the bundled sounds (the captain's offer `fd_offer` too), generated by `tool/placeholder_sounds.py` (owner decision 2026-11-20: placeholders until the design's sounds; generated here, so no licence).
  `keep.xml` keeps them through resource shrinking, since `Ringer` finds them by name.

## Rules

- **The service (RA §4.3):** `ReceivingService` is a foreground service of type `specialUse` (`dataSync` is limited to 6 hours a day on Android 15; check the Play policy at submission, RA §21 item 4) with the permanent notification «FOX DROP يستقبل طلباتك» and a partial wake lock, so the heartbeat and the ring work with the screen off once the device check turned battery optimisation off.
  `startReceiving` records that a signed-in device asked;
  `StartReceiver` starts it again after `BOOT_COMPLETED` and `MY_PACKAGE_REPLACED` only then;
  `stopReceiving` (sign-out) clears it.
  A start the system refuses (Android 12 and later restarting it from the background without an exemption) stops the service instead of crashing;
  `wake` starts it only when it is not running (`ReceivingService.running`), and never throws.
  The manifest's `<queries>` names the brands' auto-start packages, without which Android 11 and later hide them.
- **One Flutter engine:** `ReceivingService.engine(context)` creates the app's engine once (running `main`) and caches it as `fox_drop_main`;
  the app's `MainActivity` provides it (`provideFlutterEngine`) and never destroys it (`shouldDestroyEngineWithHost` false), so the board, its connection and its ring outlive the activity, and after a reboot the service runs the app with no activity.
  An app using this package must do the same.
- **The captain app (slice 2.43):** uses the ring, the chime and the readings, never the receiving service: its manifest removes `ReceivingService`, `StartReceiver` and `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE` (`tools:node="remove"`), since the background location's service of type `location` keeps its process;
  its `MainActivity` still provides the cached engine (`ReceivingService.engine`).
  A ring may vibrate while it plays (`vibrate`, 0.8 s on and 0.6 s off on the alarm usage) and stop by itself (`stopAfter`: the offer's end, for a ring an offer's push starts from the push's isolate with no app to stop it);
  `stopRing` cancels both.
  `chime(cancellation: true)` plays the cancellation sound once.
- **The ring (RA §4.4):** one `MediaPlayer` looping the sound on `USAGE_ALARM` (the media volume and silent mode do not mute it), a notification on the silent, high-importance channel `core_alerts_ring` with `CATEGORY_ALARM` and a full-screen intent that opens the app;
  Android 14 and later show it full screen only when the user allowed it (the device check's check 4), heads-up otherwise (owner decision 2026-11-20).
  A ring already playing changes only its sound or text.
  A screen wake lock turns the screen on for 30 s.
  The chime plays once on `USAGE_NOTIFICATION`, never over a ring.
- **The device check's readers (RA §4.5 checks 1–5):** notifications allowed, battery optimisation off (asked directly with `REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS`), full-screen allowed (always below Android 14), the alarm volume in percent, Do Not Disturb silencing alarms (total silence, or priority without alarms), and whether the brand has an auto-start page (Xiaomi, Huawei, Honor, Oppo, Realme, Vivo, Samsung), which no app can read: the user confirms it.
  `openSettings` opens each check's page, else the app's details.
- **Strings:** the service's and the ring channel's texts are system texts in `res/values/strings.xml` (apps/customer/CLAUDE.md «Arabic»); the ring's own text comes from the app.
- The Kotlin builds with the app (the `android-release` CI job); it has no unit tests. `test/alerts_test.dart` proves the channel's calls.
- **Windows (slice 2.44; owner decisions 2026-11-20):** `WindowsAlerts.setUp` (from the app's `main`) makes closing the window hide it (`window_manager` 0.5.2's `setPreventClose`), and the tray icon (`tray_manager` 0.5.3, `assets/tray.ico`) opens it on a click and offers «فتح التطبيق» and «إغلاق التطبيق», which asks the app's question first (RA §4.3);
  `quitNow` quits without asking (the self-update).
  The 0.6 and later releases of both rebuilt them on `nativeapi`: stay on 0.5 until those settle.
  A ring loops the bundled WAV with `PlaySound` (the default output device, RA §4.4), brings the window to the front and flashes the taskbar button until it is focused (Windows keeps a background app from taking the focus), and shows one silent toast (`flutter_local_notifications_windows`, AUMID `windowsAppUserModelId`);
  `stopRing` ends all three.
  The sounds are the Android raw resources, declared as the package's assets (one copy; `soundPath`).
  The readings: Windows' toasts on for the user and the app (the registry), «Start with Windows» (the user's `Run` entry `FOX DROP` naming this executable, and not turned off in Task Manager's `StartupApproved`, which `startWithWindows` clears), the default output's mute and volume (`IAudioEndpointVolume`), the active power plan's sleep or hibernate timeout, on mains power or battery, whichever comes first (0 never).
  `openSettings`: notifications, sound and power open their `ms-settings:` pages;
  `autostart` turns the `Run` entry on itself (`startWithWindows`).
  `launch_at_startup` is not used: its `win32` 5 clashes with `package_info_plus`'s 6. No `ReceivingService` on Windows: the app keeps running in the tray.
  The C++ builds with the app (the `windows-release` CI job);
  it has no unit tests.

- **iOS (slice 2.46; CA §4.4–4.5, K15; owner decisions 2026-11-20):** no receiving service (`startReceiving`, `stopReceiving` and `wake` do nothing).
  A ring in the foreground loops `<sound>.wav` from the app's bundle with `AVAudioPlayer` on the `playback` audio category (the ring/silent switch does not mute it) and vibrates every 1.4 s;
  when the app leaves the foreground while it rings, or a ring starts outside it, it becomes one local notification `fd_ring` (once per sound and text, so the app's ring every 5 s alerts once; a background task covers the post): time-sensitive with the sound file (`fd_offer` uses `fd_offer_30s.wav`), or critical at volume 1 once the user allowed Critical Alerts;
  back in the foreground the notification is removed and the loop plays again;
  `stopRing` and `stopAfter` end both.
  The chime plays once on the `ambient` category, in the foreground only and never over a ring.
  The readings: notifications authorised, time-sensitive ones and Critical Alerts enabled, Background App Refresh available, Low Power Mode.
  `openSettings` opens the app's notification settings for `notifications` from iOS 16, else the app's page.
  The app bundles the sounds itself (Xcode resources referring to this package's files): a plugin's own resources are not in the main bundle, where iOS looks for a notification's sound.
  The Swift builds with the app (the `ios-release` CI job);
  it has no unit tests.
