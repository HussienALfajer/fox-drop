# marketing module

> Moved here from `apps/server/src/modules/marketing/CLAUDE.md` on 2026-11-20 (`docs/claude-code-workflow.md`). In these notes, a folder's `CLAUDE.md` (or "its `CLAUDE.md`") means that folder's file under `docs/code/`; the folder's own `CLAUDE.md` now holds only the rules for working there.

Promo codes «أكواد الخصم» (architecture §4.2; AP §9.11.1) and «تعويض الزبون» with its caps and approvals (AP §6.4, §9.3.2). The home sections (AP §9.11.3, slice 2.27b), the banners (AP §9.11.2, slice 2.27c) and the mass pushes (AP §9.11.4, slice 2.27d).

## Rules

- **What this module owns:** `marketing.promo_codes`, `marketing.promo_code_uses`, `marketing.idempotency_keys`, `marketing.compensations`, `marketing.late_codes`, `marketing.home_sections`, `marketing.banners` and `marketing.mass_pushes`. Cities, restaurants, zones, orders and accounts are plain ids.
- **A code (`promo-codes.ts`, the pure rules; slice 2.21a):** one row per code, upper-case Latin letters and digits, 3 to 20, unique in the city (`promo_codes_city_code`; a typed code is trimmed and upper-cased, `normalizeCode`). `kind` `percentage` (`percent_bp` 1 to 10000, an optional cap `max_discount`), `fixed_amount` (`amount`) or `free_delivery` (no value: the whole fee). The minimum order is on the items value before discounts. `valid_from` before `valid_to`; optional hours `start_minute`/`end_minute` on the city clock, both or neither, an end at or before the start running past midnight. `total_limit` and `per_customer_limit` (null: none, the second never above the first). `audience` `everyone` or `new_users`. `restaurant_ids` and `zone_ids` (none: all), checked against the city (`MerchantsService.restaurantIds`, `GeoService.zones`). `funding` `platform`, `restaurant` or `shared`, kept as `merchant_share_bp` (0, 10000, or the ratio 1 to 9999); a code a restaurant funds names its restaurants, since each pays its share on Saturday (AP §8.4; decided by us). `daily_budget` (null: none) counts the business day of the daily report (owner decision 2026-11-16). `visibility` `visible` or `secret`. `checkPromoFields` and the table's `CHECK`s say the same. Amounts are in the city's base when saved (`currency`), under `lockCityCurrencies` (`409 no_base_currency` without one); an edit of a code saved in an earlier base is `409 currency_changed` (a new code is made in the new base); registered with `FxService.useCityAmounts` while a code has not ended.
- **Status:** `active` or `paused`, set by OWN. What the list shows (`promoCodeState`): `expired` from `valid_to`, else `paused`, else `scheduled` before `valid_from`, else `active`. The daily budget's pause is worked out from the day's cost, never stored.
- **A code in an order (`promo-code-uses.service.ts`, `PromoCodeUsesService`, the public service ordering calls; slice 2.21b):** `check` (the quote, through the pool) and `hold` (placing, in its transaction under the code's advisory lock `marketing.code:<id>`, so two orders never take the last use or the rest of the day; `record` keeps the use once the order has its id) find the typed code in the city (`normalizeCode`) and answer the applied code (its id, its terms, `promoDiscount` and `fixedAmountAboveItems`) or the refusal of CU Appendix C (`codeRefusal`: `unknown_code`, `not_valid_now` for paused, not started, ended, outside its hours on the city clock or saved in another base, `not_for_restaurant` for the restaurants and zones, `below_minimum` with what is missing, `first_order_only`, `used_before`, `uses_ended`, `budget_reached`). The caller gives the cart in the base currency and the signed-in customer (null for a guest's quote, which leaves the audience and the per-customer limit to placing): the account, `accountIds` (identity's `phoneAccounts`: the number's accounts and the account's line, slice 2.25c, whose uses count toward the per-customer limit and «أكوادي»'s used codes, AP §8.8), identity's keyed hash of the number, the device id of the session, and `newUser` (no delivered order on any account of the number, ordering's to say). `promoDiscount` (AP §8.4): a percentage of the items value half up, capped at its cap; a fixed amount; both capped at the items value; free delivery the whole fee; the restaurant's part `merchant_share_bp` of it, half up. `promo_code_uses`: one row per order with the number's hash, the device, the business day (the cutoff that started it, `finance.business_day_cutoff`), the discount and the restaurant's part, `held` at placing, then `settle`d `spent` or `returned` when the order ends (again when an outcome changes). A use counts for the limits, the new-user rule (once per number and once per device, any account) and the day's cost while it is not `returned`. The cost of a use is the order's whole discount, the restaurant's share included (decided by us). The list's `uses` and `costToDate` are `totals` of the uses not given back.
- **«الأكواد المتاحة لك» and the suggestion (CU §10.4, U11, Q19; slice 2.21d, owner decision 2026-11-16):** `available` takes the cart without a code and answers the visible codes running in the city now (`active`, started, not ended, saved in the base) that `codeRefusal` lets through for this cart and customer, each with its value, cap, minimum, hours, end and discount; a code refused only by its minimum (checked as if the minimum were reached) is listed with `missing` and no discount; any other refusal leaves it out. Largest discount first, ties by code, then those below their minimum by what is missing. The counts are one grouped read of the uses (`counts`), shared with `check` and `hold`. `suggestedCode` (pure) is the listed code with the largest discount above the applied code's (0 without one), never the applied code itself; nobody applies it but the customer (U11).
- **«أكوادي» (CU §12.4; slice 2.21d):** `mine` answers the customer's codes in a city: «متاحة» (`ended` null), the codes running now (`runsNow`: visible, `active`, started, not ended, in the base) that `openToCustomer` lets through (the audience, the per-customer limit, the total; the cart's rules and the day's budget wait for checkout), by the nearest end; then «منتهية» newest first within `ENDED_CODES_DAYS` (30, fixed): a code the account used (a use not given back, within the 30 days) and can no longer use (`openToCustomer` false) is `used` at its latest use, a secret one included; a code they may still use but that is paused or secret is left out, and a visible code that ended without being used up is `expired` at its end, when its audience took the customer. The index `promo_code_uses_customer_held` serves the read of the account's uses. `usesLeft` is the per-customer limit less this account's uses. The customer's own personal codes are listed with the visible ones (`listedFor`; slice 2.22b): `runsNow` takes a visible or personal code, never a secret one.
- **The panel's customer profile (AP §9.8; slice 2.25b):** `usesOf(accountId, cityId, limit)` answers the customer's latest uses in the city with the code, its kind, the order, the discount and the use's status (held, spent, returned), newest first; the customers module imports `MarketingModule` for it.
- **The returned code's push (CU §10.4, §14 #23; owner decision 2026-11-16):** `settle` to `returned` that changes a use queues «أُعيد الكود {الكود} إلى أكوادك» (`customer.code_returned`, `data.target` `codes`) to the use's account in the same transaction, only when the code is back in that customer's «متاحة» by `mine`'s rule: `runsNow` in the city's base and `openToCustomer` on the uses that still count (whether the customer is still new is the placing's `new_user`: the order given back was not delivered). Source `marketing:customer.code_returned:<order>:<moment>`. The module imports `ContentModule` (`NotificationTexts`) and `NotificationsModule` (`PushQueue`) for it.
- **The code report (AP §9.11.1; slice 2.21d, owner decision 2026-11-16):** each use keeps `new_user` (the customer had no delivered order on any account of the number at placing, from `hold`) and `delivered` (`settle`'s last argument: the order ended delivered, set again whenever it is settled, so «تغيير النتيجة» follows; ordering passes the delivery's and an outcome's result, a waiting outcome's included); migration 0082 (with the index of «أكوادي»), uses before it read false. `report` counts the orders (uses not given back), the uses given back, the customers (accounts) behind the orders, the cost and the restaurants' part, and the new customers acquired: numbers with a use both `new_user` and `delivered`. `GET /v1/admin/promo-codes/{codeId}/report?cityId=` (the list's permissions; the cost and the restaurants' part null without `marketing.read_costs` or `marketing.manage`) answers the code, its report and `canManage`. From it OWN pauses, resumes (the list's routes) or extends: `POST …/{codeId}/extend` (`{ cityId, validTo }`, `marketing.manage`, no key: it sets a value) moves the end later than both the end it had and now (`400 invalid_promo_code` otherwise; `409 currency_changed` for a code saved in an earlier base), so an ended code runs again; audited `promo_code.extend` with the end before and after.
- **Personal codes (slice 2.22a):** `visibility` `personal` with `customer_account_id` (both or neither, `promo_codes_personal`): a compensation code or the late code (2.22c), made only by `createPersonalCode` (`personal-codes.ts`, which `CompensationsService` and `LateCodesService` share), never in «أكواد الخصم»: one use by its customer (`total_limit` and `per_customer_limit` 1), an amount or a percentage (a compensation's with its maximum, the late code's maybe without; `promo_codes_personal` no longer asks for one, migration 0086), `created_by` and `updated_by` the admin or null for the system (only a personal code, `promo_codes_admins`), funded by the platform, no minimum, restaurants, zones, hours, budget or batch, valid from its issue for `support.personal_code_valid_days` (30, owner decision 2026-11-17), a generated 8-character code (`generatedCode('')`) drawn under the city's lock `marketing.codes:<cityId>`. The list, the report and every code route leave them out (`unknown_promo_code`), and the routes' bodies take only `visible` or `secret`. They do not hold the city's base currency (`useCityAmounts` leaves them out): a code saved in an earlier base is refused as `not_valid_now` (to confirm with the owner). **In an order (slice 2.22b):** `check` and `hold` take a personal code only for its customer (its `customer_account_id`); anyone else, a guest included, gets `unknown_code`, so a code never tells another that it exists. «الأكواد المتاحة لك» (`available`) and «أكوادي» (`mine`) list the signed-in customer's own personal codes with the visible ones (`listedFor`). `CodeTerms.compensation` is true for a personal code: ordering keeps it in `promo_terms` and books the use's platform part to `platform.compensation_cost` instead of `platform.discount_cost` (docs/domain/11); a use given back returns as any use, with «أُعيد الكود…» while the code still runs.
- **«تعويض الزبون» (`compensations.service.ts`, AP §6.4, D4, §9.3.2; slice 2.22a, owner decisions 2026-11-17):** `marketing.compensations`, one row per compensation, linked to an order (its id, number and customer, plain values): the value (`fixed_amount` with `amount`, or `percentage` with `percent_bp` and the required `max_discount`), the city's base `currency` (`409 currency_changed` when the order is in another, `no_base_currency` without one), the reason chosen from content's `compensation` list (code and label copied, the note optional for every reason, 300 characters), `status` `issued` (with `code_id`), `waiting` or `rejected`, who asked and when, and OWN's decision (`decided_by`, `decided_at`; `CHECK`s). `request` (ordering's route passes the order's id, city, number, customer, customer total and currency) runs under the order's advisory lock `marketing.compensation:<orderId>`, then the city's codes lock `marketing.codes:<cityId>`, and no currencies lock (slice 2.22b: a compensation belongs to an order, so the city's base is locked for good by `fx.city_bases_keep_lock`, and the cancellation calls it holding the order's row before the captain's, which `lockCityCurrencies` would invert against `CaptainsService.setCustodyLimit`); `issue` reads its setting and the currencies through the transaction. `approve` takes the order's lock and the codes lock after the row's lock, then `lockCityCurrencies`, so a request on the order meanwhile counts the approved one: the cap (`capPct`) is null for an admin holding `compensation.approve_above_cap` (OWN), else the highest `support.compensation_cap_pct.<role>` of their roles among SUP and OPS (0 for any other role); what counts is the amount or a percentage's maximum (`countedValue`), summed over the order's `issued` compensations by anyone, and within the cap when that sum with the new one is at most the cap's percentage of the total (rounded down). Within it, `issue` creates the personal code, sets the row `issued` and queues «لك كود تعويض {القيمة}: {الكود}» (`customer.compensation_code`, CU §14 #16, `data.target` `codes`, source `marketing:customer.compensation_code:<id>`, expiring with the code; `{القيمة}` «25 ₺» or «20% بحد أقصى 50 ₺», as the panel says it, the wording ours) in one transaction; above it the row waits and raises «طلب موافقة تعويض» (`compensation_approval`, information, OWN, subject `compensation` with `details.orderNumber`). OWN's `approve` (with the value asked, or another) issues it then, and `reject` tells the customer nothing; both resolve the alert, and a decided request is `409 compensation_decided`. A waiting or rejected request counts against no cap. `request` is `prepare` (the reason and the cap, through the pool) then `requestIn` (the transaction's part), so ordering's cancellation runs it in its own transaction (slice 2.22b). `issuedCode` gives an order's latest issued code, which the customer's cancelled order shows (CU §11.9). The request takes `Idempotency-Key`, kept on its row for good (unique per admin): a repeat answers the row, another command with the key `422 idempotency_key_reused`. Audit: `compensation.issue`, `compensation.request`, `compensation.approve` (before and after), `compensation.reject`, target `compensation`.
- **The late code (`late-codes.service.ts`, AP §8.3, §6.4, docs/domain/03, CU §11.6, §14 #15; slice 2.22c, owner decisions 2026-11-17):** `LateCodesService.issue` runs in ordering's timer transaction under the order's lock (`OrderTimers.lateCode`, the ordering module's `CLAUDE.md`): once per order (`marketing.late_codes`, the order's id its key, with its number, customer, city, code and the promised time it was late against; the application role may not update it), a personal code of `order.late_compensation_value` in the order's currency (an amount, or a percentage of the next order's items value without a maximum), drawn under the city's codes lock (`lockCityCodes`), created by the system (`created_by` null), and the push «تأخر طلبك، وهذا كود خصم لطلبك القادم: {الكود}» (`customer.late_code`, source `marketing:customer.late_code:<orderId>`, `data` `{ target: 'order', orderId }`: CU §14 rows 1 to 15 open the order; expiring with the code). No code while the setting is unset («Before launch», owner decision 2026-11-17: the slice is built with it unset), at 0%, or for an amount in another currency than the order's (`issue` answers why: `unset`, `zero` for a percentage that rounds to nothing, `other_currency` for an order placed in an earlier base, `already_issued`). It is no compensation: no reason, no cap and never in the order's cumulative total (AP §6.4), and its use books to `platform.compensation_cost` as every personal code's (docs/domain/11). `forOrder` gives an order's late code with its value for the customer's order.
- **The wrong change's code (AP §6.4, §8.10; slice 2.23c):** support's ticket asks `prepare` with `'wrong_change'` instead of a reason: the fixed reason `WRONG_CHANGE_COMPENSATION` («مشكلة في المبلغ أو الباقي», outside Content's list), no cap, and `issuedSum` leaves it out of the order's cumulative total. A ticket's other codes are ordinary compensations with a reason of the list, asked in the resolution's transaction with `requestIn` (key `ticket:<ticket id>`).
- **The cost report's read (AP §9.10.6; slice 2.22d):** `CompensationsService.codeOrigins(codeIds)` says where each personal code came from: a compensation with its reason (code and label as chosen) and the order compensated, or the late code of an order.
- **Compensation routes:** ordering's `GET /v1/admin/orders/{orderId}/compensations` (`operations.read`) answers the order's compensations newest first, its currency, `capLeft` (what the session may still give without approval, null for no cap), `canCompensate` and the active reasons; `POST /v1/admin/orders/{orderId}/compensations` (`operations.compensate`: OWN, OPS, SUP; `Idempotency-Key`; `{ value, reason: { code, note } }`) answers the compensation (`400 invalid_value`, `unknown_reason`; `404 unknown_order`). «طلبات الموافقة» (`admin-compensations.controller.ts`, `compensation.approve_above_cap`): `GET /v1/admin/compensations?cityId=` (waiting, oldest first), `POST …/{compensationId}/approve` (`{ cityId, value }`, value null keeps the one asked) and `…/reject` (`{ cityId }`), no key: a repeat is `409 compensation_decided`. Each compensation names its requester and decider (`AdminSessionsService.names`); the module imports `IdentityModule` for it.
- **A batch:** `count` (2 to 500) codes of the prefix (up to 8 letters and digits) and 8 random characters that cannot be misread (`generatedCode`, no 0, O, 1, I or L), drawn again on a collision, sharing a `batch_id`, created in one transaction under the city's advisory lock `marketing.codes:<cityId>`, as a typed code is.
- **Keys (architecture §5.3):** creating one code or a batch takes `Idempotency-Key`: `idempotency_keys` per admin account with a hash of the command and the codes created, checked again under the key's advisory lock; a repeat within 24 hours answers those codes, another command with the key `422 idempotency_key_reused`. The edit, pause and resume set a value, so no key.
- **Audit:** `promo_code.create` (target the code), `promo_code.generate` (target the batch), `promo_code.edit` (before and after), `promo_code.pause`, `promo_code.resume`, `promo_code.extend` (slice 2.21d).
- **Admin routes (`admin-promo-codes.controller.ts`, AP §6):** `GET /v1/admin/promo-codes?cityId=` (`marketing.read`: OPS, `marketing.read_costs`: FIN, or `marketing.manage`: OWN) answers the codes newest first with the city's restaurants, zones and base currency, and `canManage`; `costToDate` is null without `marketing.read_costs` or `marketing.manage`. `POST /v1/admin/promo-codes` (one typed code), `POST …/batch`, `PUT …/{codeId}` (every field but the code), `POST …/{codeId}/pause` and `…/resume` (`marketing.manage`, each with `{ cityId }` in the body). `400 invalid_promo_code` with the rule broken, `404 unknown_city`, `unknown_promo_code`, `409 code_taken`, `no_base_currency`, `currency_changed`. Money travels as strings of minor units, the percentage and the ratio as basis points.

- **«أقسام الرئيسية» (AP §9.11.3, CU §8.1; slice 2.27b, owner decisions 2026-11-18; `home-sections.service.ts`, `admin-home-sections.controller.ts`):** `marketing.home_sections`, the customer home's sections per city: the six built-in kinds (`BUILT_IN_SECTIONS`: `banners`, `categories`, `nearest`, `most_ordered`, `new`, `offers`, one row per city and kind at most, the partial unique index `home_sections_built_in`) and `custom` ones (a title of 1 to 40 characters trimmed, 1 to `SECTION_RESTAURANTS_MAX` (30) of the city's restaurants each once, in the admin's order; at most `CUSTOM_SECTIONS_MAX` (20) not removed; both bounds technical, decided by us); `position`, `visible`, and `removed_at` for a custom section only (`home_sections_custom`: a built-in row has no title, restaurants or removal). A built-in section has no row until an edit of the city's sections stores them all (`store`, an upsert in the list's order); `sections(cityId)` reads the stored ones in order, then the built-in ones never stored in the default order, shown. A section's `key` is its kind when built in, its id when custom. Routes (`marketing.read` or `marketing.manage` to read, `marketing.manage`, OWN alone, to change: owner decision 2026-11-18, DATA has banners only): `GET /v1/admin/home-sections?cityId=`, `POST …/order` (`keys`, every section once; `409 sections_changed` otherwise; the same order writes nothing), `POST …/visibility` (`key`, `visible`), `POST /v1/admin/home-sections` (a custom section at the end, shown), `PUT …/{sectionId}` and `POST …/{sectionId}/remove` (a custom one; a built-in section is only hidden, decided by us); each answers the city's sections with its restaurants for the picker and `canManage`, takes the city's advisory lock `marketing.home_sections:<cityId>` and is audited `home_section.reorder`, `.visibility`, `.create`, `.update`, `.remove` (target `home_section`, the key). The add takes `Idempotency-Key`, kept with the section in `marketing.idempotency_keys` (its `code_ids` the section's id) under the key's advisory lock: a repeat within 24 hours answers the sections without adding one, another body under the key `422 idempotency_key_reused`; the others set a value or name their target, so take none. `400 invalid_section`, `404 unknown_city`, `unknown_section`. `offerRestaurants(cityId, zoneId, now)` gives «العروض» (owner decision 2026-11-18): the restaurants of the city's visible codes running now (active, started, not ended, in the base) limited to restaurants, working in the zone and with uses left of their total limit (`PromoCodeUsesService.totals`); their hours and day's budget are not read (decided by us, to confirm with the owner).
- **«البانرات» (AP §9.11.2, CU §8.1; slice 2.27c, owner decisions 2026-11-18; `banners.service.ts`, `admin-banners.controller.ts`):** `marketing.banners`, the customer home's banners per city: a title (trimmed, 1 to `BANNER_TITLE_MAX` (60) characters), the image's key (an upload of `banner_image`, cut to exactly 1200×500 in the panel and checked by the files module), a `target` (`restaurant`: one of the city's restaurants in `restaurant_ids`; `restaurants`: the banner's own list, 1 to 30 of the city's restaurants each once, in the admin's order, opened under its title; `category`: an active category; `code`: a code of the city running now, by `code_id`: visible, active, started, not ended and in the city's base, as «أكوادي» lists it, so the panel never publishes a code banner the home leaves out (a code paused later leaves the home until it runs again); the `banners_target` `CHECK` says the same), `zone_ids` (the city's zones, none for all), `starts_at` before `ends_at` and the end after now, `position`, who created and last changed it, and `removed_at` (removed for good, never deleted). At most `BANNERS_MAX` (30) not removed and not ended per city (technical, decided by us), counted by the create and by an edit that brings an ended banner back. Routes (`marketing.read`, `marketing.manage` or `marketing.banners` to read; `marketing.banners`, OWN and DATA, to change: owner decision 2026-11-18, DATA publishes alone): `GET /v1/admin/banners?cityId=` (the banners in order, ended ones included, with the city's restaurants, the active categories, the city's codes running now, the zones and `canManage`), `POST /v1/admin/banners` (with `uploadId`; `Idempotency-Key` kept in `marketing.idempotency_keys`, its `code_ids` the banner's id, with the hash of the body as sent: a repeat within 24 hours answers the banners before any check, so a banner or code ended since never refuses it, and another body is `422 idempotency_key_reused`), `PUT …/{bannerId}` (every field, `uploadId` null keeps the image; no key: it sets values), `POST …/order` (every id once, `409 banners_changed` otherwise) and `POST …/{bannerId}/remove` (a repeat is `404 unknown_banner`); each answers the city's banners, takes the city's advisory lock `marketing.banners:<cityId>` and is audited `banner.create`, `.update`, `.reorder`, `.remove` (target `banner`). The upload's `prepare` runs before the transaction and `link` in it (`409 upload_used` for an upload another record took); the files module's refusals keep their codes (`400 upload_wrong_size` for an image that is not 1200×500). `400 invalid_banner`, `404 unknown_city`. `running(cityId, zoneId, now)` gives the customer's home the banners started and not ended in the zone, in order, a code target only while its code runs now in the city's base (`runsNow`, as «أكوادي» lists it); the customers module drops the rest it cannot open and keeps `HOME_BANNERS_MAX` (10).
- **«الإشعارات الجماعية» (AP §9.11.4, D20, CU §14 row 22; slice 2.27d, owner decisions 2026-11-18; `mass-pushes.service.ts`, `admin-mass-pushes.controller.ts`):** `marketing.mass_pushes`, one row per push of a city: the title (trimmed, 1 to `MASS_PUSH_TITLE_MAX`, 50) and text (1 to `MASS_PUSH_BODY_MAX`, 200; both bounds decided by us), the audience (`all`, `zone` with an active zone of the city, `inactive` with 1 to 365 days, `never_ordered`), the target (`home`, `restaurant` of the city, `code` running now as a banner's, its code copied), `scheduled` and `send_at` (now, or ahead within `MASS_PUSH_SCHEDULE_DAYS`, 30), `recipients`, who sent it and who cancelled it (`CHECK`s keep them together; the application role may update only `cancelled_at` and `cancelled_by`, migration 0113). No daily limit (D20). **The audience** is the customers module's to say (`useAudience`, registered by its `MassPushAudienceSource`, since this module cannot import it): registered customers (a profile) whose account is not closed, never a guest; `all` every one whatever their city (decided by us: accounts have no city, V1 has one); `zone` those with a saved address in the zone (geo's `covered`); `inactive` those whose latest delivered order in the city (ordering's `useLastDeliveries` port on customers: test orders and free re-deliveries left out) is older than the days, an account without one left out; `never_ordered` those with none in the city (owner decisions: delivered orders only); deliveries count by the account's line (AP §8.8: identity's `lines`, so an account that continues a closed one with orders has ordered). **Sending:** `GET /v1/admin/mass-pushes/audience` counts it for the panel, and `POST /v1/admin/mass-pushes` takes that size (`audienceSize`): the audience is counted again, `409 audience_changed` when it moved (the size now in the message), `409 audience_empty` for none; then one transaction records the push, queues one push per customer through `PushQueue` (template `customer.mass_push`, its own `title`, level `default`, priority normal, `at` its time and `campaignId` its id, expiring 24 hours after its time, decided by us; source `marketing:customer.mass_push:<id>:<account>`; data `kind: mass_push` with `target` `home`, `restaurant` and `restaurantId`, or `codes` and `code`) and the key, and audits `mass_push.create`. So the audience is fixed at the confirmation, also for a scheduled push (decided by us, to confirm with the owner). `Idempotency-Key` as a banner's (`marketing.idempotency_keys`, `code_ids` the push's id). `POST …/{massPushId}/cancel` (`{ cityId }`, no key: it names its target) cancels a scheduled push before its time and gives up its pushes (`PushQueue.cancelCampaign`), audited `mass_push.cancel`; a push sent now, one whose time came or one cancelled is `409 not_cancellable`; no edit (owner decision). **The history:** `GET /v1/admin/mass-pushes?cityId=` answers the latest 100 newest first: status (`scheduled` before its time, `cancelled`, else `sent`), the sender's and canceller's names, the audience with the zone's name, the target with the restaurant's name or the code, `recipients`, `sent` (FCM accepted it for a device) and `delivered` (the app acknowledged it: notifications' `campaignCounts`), with the city's restaurants, codes running now, active zones and `canManage`. Permissions (owner decision 2026-11-18): `marketing.manage` (OWN) sends, counts and cancels; `marketing.read` (OPS) reads. `400 invalid_mass_push`, `404 unknown_city`, `unknown_mass_push`, `422 idempotency_key_reused`.
