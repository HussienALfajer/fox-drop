## Follow-ups

The owner's decisions of 2026-11-28 (AP §2, «Open items decided») leave these six:

- **A global daily cap on codes abroad** (D.11a security review; owner, 2026-11-28; T0): `otp.number_daily_max_abroad` limits one number, not a script that sends one code to each of many foreign numbers while `otp.app_check_required` is off. Add a city-wide daily cap on codes sent outside Syria and Turkey (a new setting in Appendix A); once reached, foreign numbers get a refusal until the next business day. Before the apps offer foreign numbers, that is with the rebuilt phone field.
- **A discount above the clearing account** (`test/money-properties`, PR 246; owner, 2026-11-28; T0): when a restaurant-funded discount exceeds what the order's clearing account holds, `outcomeEntries` throws («the order clearing account would not settle»). Post the shortfall to the restaurant's account as a debt, settled on Saturday (`docs/domain/` outcome rule), and drop the property test's skip of that case.
- **Erase personal data at account closure** (D19; owner, 2026-11-28; T0, a migration): closure keeps only the phone, devices, orders, ledger entries, blocks, strikes, dues and promo history; it erases the name, gender, birthday, saved addresses and favourites (`docs/domain/08`). Before the store submission, with the privacy policy (CU §18 item 4).
- **A zero line on the restaurant's statement** (reporting; owner, 2026-11-28; T1): every delivered order of a restaurant has a line on its statement, at 0 when no commission or restaurant-funded discount was posted; test orders stay out. Today the lines come from the account's entries alone.
- **A captain's cash hand-in punctuality** (reporting; owner, 2026-11-28; T1): the captains report gains the business days on which the captain still held cash due from a closed business day (D11's block or a grace), out of the days worked, from the cash desk's existing records.
