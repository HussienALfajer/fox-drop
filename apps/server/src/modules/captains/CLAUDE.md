# captains module

The captain record, its vehicle, status and cash limit, its availability and location, and the city's pay table (architecture §4.2, §7.3; AP §9.7, §9.12.5, §8.5.3–8.5.4; CA §4.3, §7).

## Rules

- **The record (AP §9.7.1, D10):** `createCaptain` adds an `active` captain with its full name, vehicle (`vehicles` of the settings catalogue), plate number (required for a motorbike or a car) and cash limit, audited as `captain.create`. The application pipeline, documents, contract, kit and deposit come with their slices; until then a captain is created activated, by the seed.
- **Status (AP §9.7.5):** `active`, `suspended`, `terminated`, changed by `setStatus` along `statusChanges` (active ↔ suspended; active or suspended → terminated; terminated is final), each with a reason (`reason_required`). A suspension keeps its reason for the app and an optional end; past its end the captain is active again without a write: `captain(id, now)`, the status changes and `availability.ts` all read it so. Suspending a suspended captain replaces the suspension (the next step of the ladder, AP §9.7.6). Terminating does not run the offboarding checklist yet (AP §9.7.5): the panel's route must not call it before the checklist exists. A suspension or termination ends the open online period at once (`end_reason`); with an active order it will be last-order mode (K18), with the ordering slices. Audited as `captain.status`.
- **Cash limit (AP §8.5.3, §9.7.5):** `custody_limit` in the city's base currency, above zero, changed by `setCustodyLimit` with a reason (`captain.custody_limit`). Checked under `lockCityCurrencies` like the merchants' amounts, and registered with `FxService.useCityAmounts` (terminated captains left out).
- **Custody (AP §8.5.3–8.5.4):** read through `LedgerService` only, never `ledger.*`. Usage is the sum of the positive `captain.custody` positions; the cash due from closed days is each currency's balance at the last `finance.business_day_cutoff` (`lastCutoff` from `geo`) plus the desk entries since (`custody.hand_in`, `reimburse`, `fx_convert`, `adjustment`), netted across currencies. Both convert to base at the current rate of every city currency, a disabled one included (`FxService.currentValue`), and so does a cash limit set in another of the city's currencies before the base locked; an entry's moment is its transaction's. `warn` is usage at `captain.custody_warn_pct` of the limit or more.
- **Availability (CA §7.1–7.2):** `availability.ts` holds the pure rules. `onlineBlocks` lists every block of going online at once: contract ended (alone), suspended, `closed_due` above `captain.remit_tolerance`, at the cash limit, the terms in force not accepted (`terms_not_accepted`, K7; it never stops an online captain, CA §7.2), the app below `app.captain.min_version`, a simulated location. `availability` gives the state: offline, `online_available`, `busy` with an active order (no offers, no stacking in V1, AP §9.12.6), `not_receiving_offers` with its reasons (the cash limit, `closed_due` past `captain.remit_online_grace_hours` after a cutoff the captain was online across, a simulated location), or suspended. Last-order mode comes with the delivery slices. Which captains have an active order (`accepted` to `arrived` with them as its captain) is ordering's to say: it registers `useActiveOrders` at start, so this module never reads its tables; going offline with an active order is refused (`409 active_order`, CA §15), under the captain's lock, which an assignment takes too. `hasActiveOrder` answers it for one captain (identity's sign-in refuses a second device then, CA §4.9).
- **Online periods:** a row per stretch online; the open one (no `ended_at`, one per captain, a partial unique index) is the captain online. `goOnline` locks the captain row, checks the blocks and opens it; already online answers the state. `goOffline` closes it, then, after the commit so a failed read never undoes it, answers the cash to hand in above the tolerance, for the reminder (CA §7.5). An app version not in the form `1.2.3` is below any minimum. Settings and the city's clock are read before the transaction (`cityRules`), which then reads the ledger with its own connection.
- **Location (CA §4.3, architecture §7.3):** `recordLocations` keeps every point recorded inside one of the captain's online periods in `captains.locations`, so a point queued without a signal and sent after going offline still completes the route. It is partitioned by the UTC month of `recorded_at` (last month, this month and the next, created by `captains.create_month_partitions()`, which the worker calls at start and every day through `CaptainsWorkerModule`; append-only, `UPDATE` revoked on the parent and every partition). A point sent again is one row (the key is the captain and the recorded time); one recorded offline or before last month is dropped; one recorded more than a minute after the server clock is refused (`invalid_location`). The points are written while the open online period is held `FOR SHARE`, so going offline waits for them. A captain offline with no point inside an online period gets `409 not_online`. The newest point of the open period decides `simulatedLocation` in the state.
- **Live position (`live-positions.ts`):** the newest real point of the open online period in Valkey, a geo set per city (`captains:online:<cityId>`) and a hash per captain (`captains:position:<id>`, expiring 10 minutes after its last point), written by a Lua step only when newer and only while that period has not ended. Going offline, a suspension or a termination removes it and marks the ended period (`captains:ended:<id>`), so a write still on its way is refused. A simulated point is never the live position. Nothing there is the only copy, so a failed Valkey write is logged and never fails the request; a reader (dispatch, the live map) checks the hash's `recordedAt` and the captain's state in the database, since the geo set has no expiry.
- **Eligible captains near a point (AP §9.12.6, §9.3.2):** `nearestEligible(cityId, restaurant, tripMetres, now)` reads the live positions within `dispatch.search_radius_km` nearest first (`LivePositions.near`, leaving out a point recorded more than 10 minutes ago, `LIVE_POSITION_MAX_AGE_MS`), or, when Valkey cannot answer, the newest real point of each open online period from `captains.locations` with the same rules (architecture §5.7); then, with the city's rules read once, each captain's record and state: online and receiving offers (`online_available`: active, under the cash limit, not blocked for unhanded cash), with `dispatch.max_distance_km.<vehicle>` at least the trip. `nearestEligible` answers the first eligible one with its vehicle and live position (for the search start of an accepted order), or null; `eligibleNear` whether there is one; a captain whose state cannot be read (such as a currency without a rate) is logged and left out, never failing the quote. A busy captain is not eligible; the maximum pickup amount is ignored, as the rule says. `candidates` gives the assignment picker every online captain of the city with a live position, nearest first by the straight line, each with its `IneligibleReason`s (`busy`, `cash_limit`, `closed_due`, `simulated_location`, `out_of_range` beyond the radius, `vehicle_not_allowed` for the trip), eligible with none; the owner decided (2026-11-06) that dispatch measures as the capacity check does: the radius by the straight line from the live position, the vehicle by the order's kept trip.
- **Captain app routes** (`captain-availability.controller.ts`, `@AppSignedIn()` with the captain token): `GET /v1/captain/availability` (the state and the cash), `POST /v1/captain/availability/online` (the app version as `1.2.3` and whether a simulated location is active; refused `409 online_blocked` with every block; `terms_not_accepted` while the captain's account has not accepted the captain terms in force, K7, read by the controller through content's `TermsService.pending`), `POST /v1/captain/availability/offline` (the cash to hand in, or null) and `POST /v1/captain/location` (1 to 360 points, every 10 seconds or queued). Money travels as `{ amount, currency }`, the amount as a string of minor units.
- **Pay table (AP §9.12.5):** `pay_tables` are versions per city, never changed (the application role cannot `UPDATE`), each with one `pay_rates` row per vehicle; `setPayTable` needs a rate for every vehicle and a reason, and takes the city's `pay-table:` advisory lock. A city's first table takes effect when published or later; every later one 7 days or more after it is published, from that moment by default (owner decision 2026-11-06). The seed asks `hasPayTable`, never whether one is in force, so a table scheduled on staging is never replaced. `payTable(cityId, at)` takes the latest start reached; of two with the same start, the later version. The maximum distance per vehicle stays the setting `dispatch.max_distance_km.<vehicle>`.
- **For the handover (slice 2.12b, CA §9.10.1, K17):** `latestPoint(captainId)` gives the newest real point of the captain's open online period whatever its age (where the first captain waits); `offlineAfterHandover(tx, captainId, now)` ends the open online period with `handed_over` in the receipt's transaction under the captain's lock, refused while the captain still has an active order, and returns what runs after the commit (the live position removed, the listeners told).
- **For dispatch:** `assignmentLock(cityId, now)` reads the city's rules, then gives the function that locks a captain row in dispatch's transaction and answers its state; `offlineForUnansweredOffers` ends the online period with `offers_expired` (C3), never with an active order; `onlineSince` gives the open period's start (C3 counts its offers only); `onWentOffline` listeners run after any online period ends and commits (going offline, a suspension, a termination, C3), so dispatch withdraws the captain's open offer, and a listener's failure is only logged; `orderPay(payTableId, vehicle, metres)` gives an order's pay by the pay table it kept, before waiting.
- **Pay of an order (`pay.ts`):** the base pay covers the included metres; each metre past them pays `per_extra_km / 1000`, rounded down to the minor unit; the base and distance pay are raised to the minimum pay, the top-up kept apart; waiting pays per whole minute past `captain.waiting_threshold_min` on top, never absorbed by the minimum (docs/domain/04; owner decision 2026-11-06). `orderPay` returns the parts (`PayParts`: base, distance, top-up, the order pay, waiting) for the weekly statement (AP §8.5.5) and the summary (CA §9.8); `payParts` reads them by the order's pay table, and the delivery posts `earn.order_pay` and `earn.waiting_pay` apart.
- **For the alerts (AP §9.1.3):** `cashLimits(cityId, now)` gives each captain not terminated and whether its usage reached 100% of its limit (null when its cash cannot be read); `lastFixes(captainIds, since)` the newest point of each since a moment, which `ordering` reads for «انقطع اتصال كابتن أثناء طلب».
- **Time:** services take `now`; the pay table's 7 days count from the database clock, as the commission's start does.
