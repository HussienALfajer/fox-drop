# captains module

The captain record, its vehicle, status and cash limit, its availability and location, and the city's pay table (architecture §4.2, §7.3; AP §9.7, §9.12.5, §8.5.3–8.5.4; CA §4.3, §7).

## Rules

- **The record (AP §9.7.1, D10):** `createCaptain` adds an `active` captain with its full name, vehicle (`vehicles` of the settings catalogue), plate number (required for a motorbike or a car) and cash limit, audited as `captain.create`. The application pipeline, documents, contract, kit and deposit come with their slices; until then a captain is created activated, by the seed.
- **Status (AP §9.7.5):** `active`, `suspended`, `terminated`, changed by `setStatus` along `statusChanges` (active ↔ suspended; active or suspended → terminated; terminated is final), each with a reason (`reason_required`). A suspension keeps its reason for the app and an optional end; past its end the captain is active again without a write: `captain(id, now)`, the status changes and `availability.ts` all read it so. Suspending a suspended captain replaces the suspension (the next step of the ladder, AP §9.7.6). Terminating does not run the offboarding checklist yet (AP §9.7.5): the panel's route must not call it before the checklist exists. A suspension or termination ends the open online period at once (`end_reason`); with an active order the captain is in last-order mode instead (K18, below), and reactivating during the order lifts the suspension's mode. Audited as `captain.status`.
- **Cash limit (AP §8.5.3, §9.7.5):** `custody_limit` in the city's base currency, above zero, changed by `setCustodyLimit` with a reason (`captain.custody_limit`). Checked under `lockCityCurrencies` like the merchants' amounts, and registered with `FxService.useCityAmounts` (terminated captains left out).
- **Custody (AP §8.5.3–8.5.4):** read through `LedgerService` only, never `ledger.*`. Usage is the sum of the positive `captain.custody` positions; the cash due from closed days is each currency's balance at the last `finance.business_day_cutoff` (`lastCutoff` from `geo`) plus the desk entries since (`custody.hand_in`, `reimburse`, `fx_convert`, `adjustment`), netted across currencies. Both convert to base at the current rate of every city currency, a disabled one included (`FxService.currentValue`), and so does a cash limit set in another of the city's currencies before the base locked; an entry's moment is its transaction's. `warn` is usage at `captain.custody_warn_pct` of the limit or more. `currencies` gives each custody currency's balance, its part due from closed days (`closedDue`), today's part and the balance in base, the base first (AP §9.10.3 step 2).
- **For the cash desk (AP §9.10.3, slice 2.17a):** `findByName(cityId, text, limit)` finds the city's captains by a part of their name, terminated ones included; `deskCustody(captainId, now)` answers the captain and their custody; `deskLock(captainId, now)` reads the city's rules, then gives the function that locks the captain row in the desk's transaction and answers the custody; `custodyChanged(tx, captain, now)` tells the availability listeners (`custody_changed`: the captain channel's broadcast, so the app reads its state again).
- **Availability (CA §7.1–7.2):** `availability.ts` holds the pure rules. `onlineBlocks` lists every block of going online at once: contract ended (alone), suspended, `closed_due` above `captain.remit_tolerance`, at the cash limit, the terms in force not accepted (`terms_not_accepted`, K7; it never stops an online captain, CA §7.2), the app below `app.captain.min_version`, a simulated location. `availability` gives the state: offline, `online_available`, `busy` with an active order (no offers, no stacking in V1, AP §9.12.6), `not_receiving_offers` with its reasons (the cash limit, `closed_due` past `captain.remit_online_grace_hours` after a cutoff the captain was online across, a simulated location), or suspended, or `last_order` (below). Which captains have an active order (`accepted` to `arrived` with them as its captain) is ordering's to say: it registers `useActiveOrders` at start, so this module never reads its tables; going offline with an active order is refused (`409 active_order`, CA §15), under the captain's lock, which an assignment takes too. `hasActiveOrder` answers it for one captain (identity's sign-in refuses a second device then, CA §4.9).
- **Online periods:** a row per stretch online; the open one (no `ended_at`, one per captain, a partial unique index) is the captain online. `goOnline` locks the captain row, checks the blocks and opens it; already online answers the state. `goOffline` closes it, then, after the commit so a failed read never undoes it, answers the cash to hand in above the tolerance, for the reminder (CA §7.5). An app version not in the form `1.2.3` is below any minimum. Settings and the city's clock are read before the transaction (`cityRules`), which then reads the ledger with its own connection.
- **Location (CA §4.3, architecture §7.3):** `recordLocations` keeps every point recorded inside one of the captain's online periods in `captains.locations`, so a point queued without a signal and sent after going offline still completes the route. It is partitioned by the UTC month of `recorded_at` (last month, this month and the next, created by `captains.create_month_partitions()`, which the worker calls at start and every day through `CaptainsWorkerModule`; append-only, `UPDATE` revoked on the parent and every partition). A point sent again is one row (the key is the captain and the recorded time); one recorded offline or before last month is dropped; one recorded more than a minute after the server clock is refused (`invalid_location`). The points are written while the open online period is held `FOR SHARE`, so going offline waits for them. A captain offline with no point inside an online period gets `409 not_online`. The newest point of the open period decides `simulatedLocation` in the state.
- **Live position (`live-positions.ts`):** the newest real point of the open online period in Valkey, a geo set per city (`captains:online:<cityId>`) and a hash per captain (`captains:position:<id>`, expiring 10 minutes after its last point), written by a Lua step only when newer and only while that period has not ended. Going offline, a suspension or a termination removes it and marks the ended period (`captains:ended:<id>`), so a write still on its way is refused. A simulated point is never the live position. Nothing there is the only copy, so a failed Valkey write is logged and never fails the request; a reader (dispatch, the live map) checks the hash's `recordedAt` and the captain's state in the database, since the geo set has no expiry.
- **Eligible captains near a point (AP §9.12.6, §9.3.2):** `nearestEligible(cityId, restaurant, tripMetres, now)` reads the live positions within `dispatch.search_radius_km` nearest first (`LivePositions.near`, leaving out a point recorded more than 10 minutes ago, `LIVE_POSITION_MAX_AGE_MS`), or, when Valkey cannot answer, the newest real point of each open online period from `captains.locations` with the same rules (architecture §5.7); then, with the city's rules read once, each captain's record and state: online and receiving offers (`online_available`: active, under the cash limit, not blocked for unhanded cash), with `dispatch.max_distance_km.<vehicle>` at least the trip. `nearestEligible` answers the first eligible one with its vehicle and live position (for the search start of an accepted order), or null; `eligibleNear` whether there is one; a captain whose state cannot be read (such as a currency without a rate) is logged and left out, never failing the quote. A busy captain is not eligible; the maximum pickup amount is ignored, as the rule says. `candidates` gives the assignment picker every online captain of the city with a live position, nearest first by the straight line, each with its `IneligibleReason`s (`busy`, `cash_limit`, `closed_due`, `simulated_location`, `out_of_range` beyond the radius, `vehicle_not_allowed` for the trip, `above_max_pickup` given the order's pickup payment), eligible with none; the owner decided (2026-11-06) that dispatch measures as the capacity check does: the radius by the straight line from the live position, the vehicle by the order's kept trip.
- **Captain app routes** (`captain-availability.controller.ts`, `@AppSignedIn()` with the captain token): `GET /v1/captain/availability` (the state and the cash), `POST /v1/captain/availability/online` (the app version as `1.2.3` and whether a simulated location is active; refused `409 online_blocked` with every block; `terms_not_accepted` while the captain's account has not accepted the captain terms in force, K7, read by the controller through content's `TermsService.pending`), `POST /v1/captain/availability/offline` (the cash to hand in, or null) and `POST /v1/captain/location` (1 to 360 points, every 10 seconds or queued). Money travels as `{ amount, currency }`, the amount as a string of minor units.
- **Last-order mode (CA §7.3, §7.6, K13, K18; slice 2.13a):** the open online period's `last_order_by` (`captain`, `forced`, `suspended`, `terminated`), `last_order_at` and `last_order_reason` (operations' reason, exactly when forced; a `CHECK`). `availability` answers `last_order` (with who set it and the reason shown: a force offline's, or the suspension's) while the captain is online in the mode with an active order, and still once the order left until the period ends (a suspended or terminated captain then shows as such); dispatch offers only `online_available`, so no order slips in, and the picker shows `last_order`. `setLastOrder(captainId, on)` («لا أريد طلبات جديدة», `POST /v1/captain/availability/last-order`): refused offline (`not_online`), on without an active order (`no_active_order`), off for a mode the captain did not set (`last_order_not_yours`); on while on keeps it. `forceOffline(captainId, change)` (AP §9.7.5 «فصل من الاتصال», a reason required, audited `captain.force_offline`): with an active order the mode `forced` (replacing the captain's own), otherwise the period ends `forced` at once; an offline captain stays so, unrecorded. `afterOrderLeft(tx, captainId, now)`, called by ordering in the transaction that takes an order from its captain, ends the period with the mode's reason (`last_order`, `forced`, `suspended`, `terminated`) once no active order is left, or lifts a suspension's mode whose suspension ended; it returns what runs after the commit. `onAvailabilityChanged` listeners run in the transaction of a force offline and of a period a last order ended (notifications queue the push and the broadcast). The state answers `remitTolerance` too, so the app shows the hand-in reminder after a last order (CA §7.5).
- **Admin routes (`admin-captains.controller.ts`, `captains.manage`):** `GET /v1/admin/captains/{captainId}/availability` (the state and who set a last-order mode) and `POST /v1/admin/captains/{captainId}/force-offline` (`reason`; answers the state after it; no `Idempotency-Key`, a repeat changes nothing more).
- **Carried currencies (CA §10.5, slice 2.14a):** `captains.carried_currencies` (text[], null until the captain sets them: the base only). `carriedCurrencies(captainId)` answers the base, what is carried of the city's enabled currencies now (in display order) and the choices with their Arabic names (a city without a base: none); `setCarriedCurrencies` takes the city's enabled currencies, once each, one at least (`invalid_carried_currencies`), stored in display order and not audited (the captain's own preference). Routes: `GET`/`PUT /v1/captain/settings/carried-currencies` (`@AppSignedIn()`, no key: it sets a value). `captain(id)` gives `carriedCurrencies` as stored; ordering's proposal keeps only those of the order's snapshot.
- **«أقصى مبلغ أحمله» (CA §10.5, C4, K2; slice 2.15a):** `captains.max_pickup_amount` and its currency (null: no limit, the default; a `CHECK` keeps them together and above zero) and `max_pickup_set_at`, when the captain last answered, a limit or none. `maxPickup(captainId)` answers the limit, `answered` (the app asks at going online until then, K2) and the city's base; `setMaxPickup(captainId, amount | null)` stores an amount in the city's base under `lockCityCurrencies` (`not_in_base_currency` while the city has none; `invalid_max_pickup` at zero or below; `active_order` during an order, CA §15), not audited (the captain's own preference). Registered with `FxService.useCityAmounts` (`captain.max_pickup_amount`), so the base never changes under a limit (on staging, a captain's limit blocks a base change until it is cleared; to confirm with the owner). Routes: `GET`/`PUT /v1/captain/settings/max-pickup-amount` (`{ amount }` minor units as a string, or null; no key: it sets a value). `candidates(..., pickupPayment)` adds `above_max_pickup` when the order's pickup payment is above the limit (same currency); `nearestEligible` and the capacity check ignore it, as AP §9.12.6 says. `captain(id)` gives `maxPickup`.
- **Pay table (AP §9.12.5):** `pay_tables` are versions per city, never changed (the application role cannot `UPDATE`), each with one `pay_rates` row per vehicle; `setPayTable` needs a rate for every vehicle and a reason, and takes the city's `pay-table:` advisory lock. A city's first table takes effect when published or later; every later one 7 days or more after it is published, from that moment by default (owner decision 2026-11-06). The seed asks `hasPayTable`, never whether one is in force, so a table scheduled on staging is never replaced. `payTable(cityId, at)` takes the latest start reached; of two with the same start, the later version. The maximum distance per vehicle stays the setting `dispatch.max_distance_km.<vehicle>`.
- **Peak bonus (AP §9.12.5, D15; slice 2.16, owner decisions 2026-11-12):** `peak-bonus.ts` holds the pure rules, `PeakBonusService` the versions. `peak_bonus_versions` (per city, never changed: the application role cannot `UPDATE`; `enabled`, the base currency when saved, `effective_from`, the editor) each with its `peak_bonus_windows` (ISO days, start and end minutes of the city's day, an end before the start running past midnight and belonging to the day it starts; the amount per delivered order above zero; geo's zone ids, none for every zone). Off until the owner first saves it. `setPeakBonus` (the city's `peak-bonus:` advisory lock, under `lockCityCurrencies`, the base only: `not_in_base_currency` without one; `invalid_peak_bonus` for a window or a zone not of the city; audited `peak_bonus.set`) takes effect by `takesEffect`: at once unless the new version pays a zone less than a window running now at any minute before that window's end (a lowering, a disabling, a shortened end, a zone dropped), then at the latest end among such windows; with no window running, at once. A save that both raises and lowers waits as a whole (AP §9.12.5 lets a raise apply at once; to confirm with the owner). The audit keeps the version in force (`versionId`) and the waiting version a save replaces (`replaces`); an unknown city is `unknown_city` (`404`). `versionInForce` takes the latest version whose start was reached, leaving out one still waiting when a newer one was saved (a newer save replaces a waiting change). `bonusAt` pays the largest amount among the running windows that cover the restaurant's zone (`GeoService.zoneAt`; outside every zone, only the windows for every zone), never summed. `bonusFor(cityId, restaurant, at)` answers it with the version, null when none applies or the version is in an earlier base; `activeNow` the windows running now with their end and zones (CA §12.5); `state` the version in force and the one waiting. Registered with `FxService.useCityAmounts` for the version in force and the latest while each is on, so the base never moves under a running window. What the captain sees (`captainWindows`, `activeNow`) is the version in force only while it pays: on and in the city's base. Routes (`admin-peak-bonus.controller.ts`, tested in `peak-bonus.routes.test.ts`): `GET /v1/admin/peak-bonus?cityId=` (`zones.read`: the versions, the city's zones and its base currency) and `PUT /v1/admin/peak-bonus` (`pricing.edit_pay_tables`, OWN; no key: it sets the value sent); `activeCaptainIds(cityId, now)` lists the captains its window's push goes to (CA §14 #23: neither terminated nor in a suspension still running); the captain app's `GET /v1/captain/peak-bonus` (`captain-peak-bonus.controller.ts`, `@AppSignedIn()`) answers the windows in force while it is on and those running now with their end (CA §12.5). The city clock is read with `Intl` (`localClock`); a window's end is counted in minutes of the city's clock, so a clock change inside a window would move it (Syria keeps UTC+3).
- **For the handover (slice 2.12b, CA §9.10.1, K17):** `latestPoint(captainId)` gives the newest real point of the captain's open online period whatever its age (where the first captain waits); `offlineAfterHandover(tx, captainId, now)` ends the open online period with `handed_over` in the receipt's transaction under the captain's lock, refused while the captain still has an active order, and returns what runs after the commit (the live position removed, the listeners told).
- **For «لا أستطيع إكمال الطلب» and «طوارئ» (slices 2.13b, 2.13c, K17):** `offlineAfterProblem(tx, captainId, reason, now)` ends the open online period with `cannot_complete` or `emergency` in ordering's transaction under the captain's lock, whatever last-order mode it had, refused while the captain still has an active order; the listeners hear `offline_after_order` (the app reads its state again) and the answer runs after the commit (the live position removed, `onWentOffline`).
- **For dispatch:** `assignmentLock(cityId, now)` reads the city's rules, then gives the function that locks a captain row in dispatch's transaction and answers its state; `offlineForUnansweredOffers(captainId, now, count)` ends the online period with `offers_expired` (C3), never with an active order, and in its transaction tells the availability listeners (`offers_expired` with the count: the notifications module's push «لم ترد على {العدد} عروض متتالية…», CA §7.4, §14 #3, and the captain's channel broadcast); while offline after it, the captain's availability answers `offersExpired` (`captain.offer_expiry_offline_count`; null otherwise and once a new period starts), so the app says why (slice 2.15b); `onlineSince` gives the open period's start (C3 counts its offers only); `onWentOffline` listeners run after any online period ends and commits (going offline, a suspension, a termination, C3), so dispatch withdraws the captain's open offer, and a listener's failure is only logged; `orderPay(payTableId, vehicle, metres)` gives an order's pay by the pay table it kept, before waiting.
- **Pay of an order (`pay.ts`):** the base pay covers the included metres; each metre past them pays `per_extra_km / 1000`, rounded down to the minor unit; the base and distance pay are raised to the minimum pay, the top-up kept apart; waiting pays per whole minute past `captain.waiting_threshold_min` on top, never absorbed by the minimum (docs/domain/04; owner decision 2026-11-06). `orderPay` returns the parts (`PayParts`: base, distance, top-up, the order pay, waiting) for the weekly statement (AP §8.5.5) and the summary (CA §9.8); `payParts` reads them by the order's pay table, and the delivery posts `earn.order_pay` and `earn.waiting_pay` apart.
- **For the alerts (AP §9.1.3):** `cashLimits(cityId, now)` gives each captain not terminated and whether its usage reached 100% of its limit (null when its cash cannot be read); `lastFixes(captainIds, since)` the newest point of each since a moment, which `ordering` reads for «انقطع اتصال كابتن أثناء طلب».
- **Time:** services take `now`; the pay table's 7 days count from the database clock, as the commission's start does.
