# captains module

The captain record, its vehicle, status and cash limit, its availability and location, and the city's pay table (architecture §4.2, §7.3; AP §9.7, §9.12.5, §8.5.3–8.5.4; CA §4.3, §7).

## Rules

- **The record (AP §9.7.1, D10):** `createCaptain` adds an `active` captain with its full name, vehicle (`vehicles` of the settings catalogue), plate number (required for a motorbike or a car) and cash limit, audited as `captain.create`: the seed's. «تفعيل» makes one from its application (below).
- **The application (CA §5.1–5.3, §15; slice 2.34a, migration 0127):** `captains.applications`, one per identity applicant account not rejected (`applications_open_account`; a rejected one stays and the next follows after 30 days, 2.34b), with the stage (`applicationStages`: `draft` until «إرسال الطلب», then the panel's stages and side stages, AP §9.7.1), step 1 (the full name of three words at least, 80 characters; the date of birth at least `captain.min_age` on the city's day; the neighbourhood, one of the active zones of the active cities, owner decision 2026-11-19, whose city is the application's), step 2 (the vehicle, the plate for a motorbike or a car only: `applications_plate`). `captains.application_documents`: one row per upload of a kind (`applicationDocumentKinds`; the signed contract is the office's, 2.35), its 1 or 2 files' keys in private-docs (the ID exactly 2), the ID's number (its digits, 5 to 20, typed with the photos: the duplicate check's, decided by us), the criminal record's issue date and expiry (`captain.criminal_record_validity_months` later, C18: never in the future, refused once expired), the status `pending` until the panel reviews it (2.34b); a new upload of a kind marks the one in force `replaced_at`, kept as the history. `applications.ts` holds the pure rules (`requiredDocuments`, `missing`, the normalising, the dates), `ApplicationsService` the steps: each step saves at once (the form's draft, CA §5.1) under the account's advisory lock, only while `draft` (`409 application_submitted`), and answers the application with `missing` (the vehicle, each required document in force, the captain terms accepted through content's `TermsService`, K7: the account keeps them as a captain); a captain's account is `409 already_captain`. «إرسال الطلب» needs nothing missing (`409 incomplete` with `missing`) and makes it `new`; a repeat answers it. The documents' photos are checked by `FilesService.prepare` before the transaction and linked in it (a photo linked to another document is `invalid_files`). Not audited: the applicant's own data (decided by us); the panel's actions will be. Routes (`captain-application.controller.ts`, `@AppSignedIn({ applicants: true })`, no `Idempotency-Key`: each sets its values, the submission answers a repeat): `GET /v1/captain/application`, `GET …/zones`, `PUT …/personal`, `PUT …/vehicle`, `POST …/documents/upload` (the purpose `captain_document`), `PUT …/documents/{kind}`, `POST …/submit`. Tested with a real Garage in `captain-application.test.ts` (CI only in a sandbox without IPv6).
- **The pipeline (AP §9.7.1, CA §5.3–5.4; slice 2.34b, migration 0128; owner decisions 2026-11-19):** `ApplicationPipelineService` (`application-pipeline.service.ts`), routes in `admin-captain-applications.controller.ts`: `GET /v1/admin/captain-applications?cityId=&stage=` (the counts by stage and one stage's list, oldest sent first) and `GET …/{applicationId}` (`captains.read`), every action `captains.manage` (OWN, OPS) and answering the review page. The review page: the data, the applicant's number (identity's `ApplicantLookups` registers it and the admins' names with `useIdentity`, since identity imports captains; in full for `captains.manage`, each such answer audited as a view `captain_application.phone_view` as the restaurants' accounts are, masked otherwise), every upload newest first with its files signed for 10 minutes, the open requests, the interview, the rejection, the duplicate check (another application with the same ID number, the account's other applications for the phone, since a number signs in to one account, another application or a captain with the same plate without spaces in lower case) and the history (`captains.application_events`, insert only, with the notes). `pipelineSteps` (`applications.ts`) says where each action is taken and where it leaves the application; the first action on «جديد» moves it to «قيد المراجعة» first. The actions: a document's review by the upload shown (`409 document_replaced` for a newer one; a rejection needs a reason), the office's scan (`checkedDocument` and `storeDocument`, shared with the applicant's; the files of the document in force sent again change nothing, so a retry needs no key), «طلب مستند» (`application_document_requests`, one open per document, `409 already_requested`; answered by the next upload of that document), a note (`Idempotency-Key`, kept on its event), «تحديد مقابلة» (time in the future, place and point typed each time; again with new values), training passed (from «مقابلة وتدريب» only), «رفض» with a reason (its open requests close with it), «قائمة انتظار» and back to «قيد المراجعة». A wrong stage is `409 wrong_stage` with the stage, so a repeat is refused; only the note takes a key. Each locks the row, writes its events, audits `captain_application.<action>` and calls the `onChanged` listeners in its transaction (notifications: the alert and the pushes): the change carries `notices`, the stage it moved to unless the action tells its own stage, then the action's own (a document asked for or rejected), one push each. A note's key is kept per application and admin. Migration 0128 gives the applications sent before it their `submitted` event; their alert is not raised. Once sent, the applicant uploads again only a document asked for or rejected while the application is open (`mayUploadAgain`), recorded as a `document_upload` event. A rejected applicant starts a new application 30 days after the rejection (K11, `reapplyFrom`; `409 reapply_wait` with `reapplyFrom`). The applicant's `GET /v1/captain/application` answers the requests, the interview, the rejection and the history for the status page. Tested in `application-pipeline.test.ts` with a real Garage.
- **The activation (AP §9.7.1–9.7.2, CA §5.5, D24; slice 2.35a, migration 0130; owner decisions 2026-11-19):** at «بانتظار العدة» the pipeline records, each again replacing the last until «تفعيل»: «تسليم العدة» (`PUT …/{applicationId}/kit`, `captains.manage`: items of the content list `kit_item` through `ContentService.choose`, kept as `kit_items` jsonb with their label as issued, the size typed, a count of 1 to 20; `400 invalid_kit`); the cash limit (`setCashLimit`, served by the desk's `PUT /v1/admin/cash-desk/deposits/{applicationId}/cash-limit`, `captains.cash_limit`: FIN, OWN, so FIN never reads the review page; in the base under the city's currencies lock, `CaptainsService.inBaseCurrency`; `not_in_base_currency`), prefilled from `captain.custody_limit_suggested.<vehicle>`; «تسجيل العقد» (`PUT …/contract`, `captains.manage`: the signing date not after the city's today, the start not before it, `400 invalid_contract`; its scan of 1 to `CONTRACT_PAGES_MAX` (6) pages stored as the document `signed_contract`, accepted as the office uploads it (`storeDocument`'s `acceptedBy`); `captain.probation_days` and `captain.min_commitment_months` copied when it is recorded; `probation_ends_on` computed; the commitment's end follows the confirmation, `contractDates`). The applicant never uploads `signed_contract`, nor does the office's scan route or «طلب مستند» take it (`document_not_needed`). The deposit's plan (`recordDeposit(tx, …)`, the desk's, in its transaction: the amount and the weekly instalment, null for none, in the base; `400 invalid_deposit`) and what is paid (`depositPaid`: the `captain.deposit` account of the captain to be). «تفعيل» (`POST …/activate`, `captains.manage`): `activationMissing` (`applications.ts`) lists each required document and the signed contract accepted and not expired, the kit, the deposit (its plan, with instalments or paid in full), the contract and the cash limit (`409 activation_incomplete` with `missing`); then, in one transaction, the captain row with the application's id (so the deposit paid before is already its account), the application's `captain_id` and `activated_at` (`applications_activated`), identity's `activate` (registered with `useIdentity`: the sign-in account gets its captain and city, so the open sessions read as the captain's at their next request), `captain.create` and `captain_application.activate` audited; notifications rebind the account's push devices to the captain and push `captain.application_active` «مرحباً بك في FOX DROP!» to it. Applications at «بانتظار العدة» with a cash limit, and the deposit plans at «بانتظار العدة» and «مفعّل», are registered with `FxService.useCityAmounts`. The review page answers `activation` (the kit, the cash limit and its suggestion, the contract, the deposit with `paid` and `recorded`, the setting `captain.kit_deposit_amount` as the suggestion, `missing`); the board and the page stay `captains.read` (FIN has the desk's list, `depositsAwaiting`, with the deposit and the cash limit). «رفض» is refused while the deposit holds cash (`409 deposit_paid`: no refund before 2.35b, decided by us). Tested in `captain-activation.test.ts`, which shares `application-pipeline.fixture.ts` with `application-pipeline.test.ts` (a real Garage).
- **The contract's phases (AP §9.7.2, CA §12.3–12.4, §14 #28; slice 2.35a, migration 0132):** `contractPhase` (`applications.ts`): «فترة تجربة» until the commitment starts, «فترة التزام» for `commitment_months` from its start, «مفتوح» after. `POST …/{applicationId}/confirm-probation` (`captains.manage`, at «مفعّل», once: `409 already_confirmed`; `409 captain_terminated`) records the day on the city's clock and who; the commitment starts on the probation's end, or on the confirmation's day once that has passed (`commitmentOf`), so an unreviewed captain stays in probation until someone decides. `probationReview` says `due` from `captain.probation_review_notice_days` before the end and `overdue` from it; `probationReviews(cityId, now)` lists them for the alerts sweep (`probation_ending`, information, then `probation_overdue`, warning, OWN and OPS, subject the captain with `applicationId`). `sweepContractPhases(cityId, now, notify)` (notifications' worker, every minute) tells each activated captain not terminated of a phase reached once (`phase_notified`, `probation` at activation), under the application's lock. The review page's contract answers the phase, the review, the confirmation and the commitment's dates (as confirmed, or as expected on time). `GET /v1/captain/contract` (`captain-contract.controller.ts`, `@AppSignedIn()`) answers the captain's contract, kit (labels as issued) and deposit from its latest activated application (`captainContract`; all empty for a captain the seed made). The contract's scan is not served to the app yet: nginx serves private-docs reads on the panel's host only.
- **A captain's documents (CA §12.2, AP §9.7.4, K6, C18; slice 2.35a):** the uploads of the captain's latest activated application (`captainUploads`, `captain-documents.ts`). `documentStates` (`applications.ts`): the newest accepted upload of a kind is in force until it expires (so a replacement waits beside it, CA §12.2), `expiring` from `captain.document_expiry_notice_days` before its expiry, `expired` on it, `missing` with none accepted. `goOnline` refuses an expired required document (`document_expired` with the kinds, `expiredRequired`; an optional one never blocks); captains the seed made have none. `GET /v1/captain/documents`, `POST …/documents/upload` (the purpose `captain_document`) and `PUT …/documents/{kind}` («رفع نسخة جديدة», `replaceDocument`: the checks of the application's documents, a `document_upload` event with no admin, never `signed_contract`; `409 document_not_needed`) (`captain-documents.controller.ts`, a captain's session) answer each document with its state, the one in force, the replacement waiting and the latest rejection. The panel reviews a replacement and scans a document at «مفعّل» too (`pipelineSteps`), but never rejects an accepted one there (`409 document_in_force`: it stays valid until it expires, so a missing required document cannot appear after activation; a problem with it is a suspension). `expiringDocuments(cityId, now)` lists each activated captain's documents in force expiring or expired, for notifications' reminder and alert.
- **The offboarding (AP §9.7.5, §9.7.2, §9.7.1, C17; slice 2.35b, migration 0133; owner decisions 2026-11-19):** kept on the contract, the activated application (`terminated_at`, `terminated_by`, `termination_reason`, `early_exit`, `kit_returned` with `kit_checked_at`, `deposit_settled` (`depositSettlements`: `refunded`, `withheld`) with `deposit_settled_at`, `ended_at`; `CHECK`s `applications_terminated` and `applications_offboarding`). «إنهاء التعاقد» (`POST …/{applicationId}/terminate`, `captains.manage`, `{ reason }`; `409 already_terminated`, `captain_terminated`) at «مفعّل», at the probation review or later: in one transaction `CaptainsService.setStatusIn` suspends the captain until further notice with the reason (offline at once, last-order mode with an order; `afterStatus` after the commit), so custody and earnings stop moving while the app still shows them (decided by us), and `earlyExit` (`applications.ts`) flags «خروج مبكر» only in «فترة التزام» (owner decision 2026-11-19: not in probation nor once open); a reactivation is refused while the contract is being ended (`offboarding_open`); `confirm-probation` is `409 already_terminated` then. The checklist (`offboardingMissing`): step 1 `PUT …/offboarding/kit` (`{ returned }`, `captains.manage`; a value, so no key; `409 not_terminated`, `contract_ended`, `deposit_settled` once the deposit followed it); steps 2 and 3 read from the ledger (every `captain.custody` and `captain.earnings` balance at zero: the desk's hand-in and the settlement, OWN's off-cycle payout); step 4 the deposit the platform holds at zero, settled by the desk (`lockForSettlement`, `recordSettlement` in its transaction: `depositSettlementOf`, refunded when the kit came back, withheld otherwise, all of it; `409 kit_not_checked`); step 5 `POST …/offboarding/close` (`captains.manage`; `409 offboarding_incomplete` with `missing`, `active_order`, `contract_ended` once closed): `setStatusIn` terminates the captain, identity's `release` (registered with `useIdentity`) ends the account's sessions (`account_closed`) and makes it an applicant's again (no captain, no city), `ended_at` is set, audited `captain_application.close`. Every step writes its event (`terminated`, `kit_returned`, `deposit_settled`, `contract_ended`) and is audited `captain_application.<action>`. The review page answers `offboarding` (each step, `depositHeld`, `missing`). `depositsToSettle(cityId)` lists the contracts being ended whose deposit is still held, for the desk. No deposit instalment is taken once the contract is being ended (`captainDeposit` answers none). A captain the seed made has no contract, so it is not terminated from the panel. An ended contract leaves the board and the sweeps (`activeContracts` skips a terminated contract), and the open-account and captain unique indexes leave it out (`applications_open_account`, `applications_captain` partial on `ended_at IS NULL`). **Applying again (C17):** the number signs in as an applicant (a code like any applicant's); `ApplicationsService` reads past an ended contract, and a new application keeps the latest ended contract's captain as `former_captain_id`, so `captainIdOf` gives the deposit paid before the activation to that captain's account. The review page answers `formerCaptain` (`earlyExit` when any contract of the account ended early) and each duplicate's `earlyExit`. «تفعيل» of a former captain sets its terminated row active again with the new name, vehicle, plate and cash limit (audited `captain.rehire`), so it keeps its id, ledger accounts and history; a flagged account needs `captains.activate_early_exit` (OWN, each time; `403 owner_approval_required`). Tested in `captain-offboarding.test.ts`.
- **Status (AP §9.7.5):** `active`, `suspended`, `terminated`, changed by `setStatus` along `statusChanges` (active ↔ suspended; active or suspended → terminated; terminated is final), each with a reason (`reason_required`). A suspension keeps its reason for the app and an optional end; past its end the captain is active again without a write: `captain(id, now)`, the status changes and `availability.ts` all read it so. Suspending a suspended captain replaces the suspension (the next step of the ladder, AP §9.7.6). «إغلاق الحساب» is the panel's only way to terminate (above); `setStatusIn` and `afterStatus` serve it. A suspension or termination ends the open online period at once (`end_reason`); with an active order the captain is in last-order mode instead (K18, below), and reactivating during the order lifts the suspension's mode. Audited as `captain.status`.
- **Cash limit (AP §8.5.3, §9.7.5):** `custody_limit` in the city's base currency, above zero, changed by `setCustodyLimit` with a reason (`captain.custody_limit`). Checked under `lockCityCurrencies` like the merchants' amounts, and registered with `FxService.useCityAmounts` (terminated captains left out).
- **Custody (AP §8.5.3–8.5.4):** read through `LedgerService` only, never `ledger.*`. Usage is the sum of the positive `captain.custody` positions; the cash due from closed days is each currency's balance at the last `finance.business_day_cutoff` (`lastCutoff` from `geo`) plus the desk entries since (`custody.hand_in`, `reimburse`, `fx_convert`, `adjustment`), netted across currencies. Both convert to base at the current rate of every city currency, a disabled one included (`FxService.currentValue`), and so does a cash limit set in another of the city's currencies before the base locked; an entry's moment is its transaction's. `warn` is usage at `captain.custody_warn_pct` of the limit or more. `currencies` gives each custody currency's balance, its part due from closed days (`closedDue`), today's part and the balance in base, the base first (AP §9.10.3 step 2).
- **For the cash desk (AP §9.10.3, slice 2.17a):** `findByName(cityId, text, limit)` finds the city's captains by a part of their name, terminated ones included; `deskCustody(captainId, now)` answers the captain and their custody; `deskLock(captainId, now)` reads the city's rules, then gives the function that locks the captain row in the desk's transaction and answers the custody; `custodyChanged(tx, captain, now)` tells the availability listeners (`custody_changed`: the captain channel's broadcast, so the app reads its state again).
- **Hand-in grace (AP §8.5.4, §9.10.3, §6.3; slice 2.17b):** `captains.hand_in_graces` (the application role may only insert): one captain's grace (`grantGrace`, `captains.hand_in_grace`: OWN, FIN) or, with no captain, the city's bulk grace for one business day (`grantBulkGrace`, `captains.bulk_hand_in_grace`: OWN), each with its reason and its admin, `until` the next `finance.business_day_cutoff` (`nextCutoff` from geo). The latest grace of the captain or their city still running (`graceOf`) is the state's `handInGrace` and the availability's `graceUntil`: while it runs, `closed_due` blocks neither going online nor offers (the cash limit still does), and the custody stays due. One captain's grace needs cash due above the tolerance (`409 nothing_due`); the bulk grace tells the captains blocked when it is granted (read before its transaction). A repeat to the same cutoff answers the grace in force without a new row or push, so the routes take no key. Audited `captain.hand_in_grace` and `captain.bulk_hand_in_grace` (target the city). In the grant's transaction the listeners hear `hand_in_grace` with the end and the city's time zone: notifications push «مُنحت مهلة حتى {الساعة}» silently (CA §14 #18, source `captain:captain.hand_in_grace:<grace>:<captain>`) and broadcast the captain channel. Routes (`admin-captains.controller.ts`): `POST /v1/admin/captains/{captainId}/hand-in-grace` and `POST /v1/admin/captains/hand-in-grace` (`{ cityId, reason }`). The availability answers the grace's end as `custody.handInGraceUntil`, for the captain app's home line «مُنحت مهلة حتى {الساعة}» (slice 2.17d).
- **The cash warning and reminders (CA §10.3–10.4, §14 #15–#17; slice 2.17d):** `captains.custody_warnings` keeps each time usage rose past `captain.custody_warn_pct`: one open row per captain (a partial unique index), cleared (`cleared_at`, the only column the application role may change) once usage is below it again, so the next rise warns again. `sweepCustodyWarnings(cityId, now)`, run by the worker every 30 seconds, reads each captain not terminated: at the threshold with no open row, it inserts one and the listeners hear `custody_warning` in that transaction (notifications push «اقتربت من حد العهدة. سلّم النقد قريباً.»); below it, it clears the open row. A sweep rather than the transactions that raise usage, so every way usage moves (a delivery, a correction, the desk, a new rate) is seen, within one sweep (decided by us). `remitOnlineUntil` (`availability.ts`) is the end of the offers for a captain online across the last cutoff with `closed_due` above the tolerance and no grace running (the cutoff plus `captain.remit_online_grace_hours`, while ahead); the state answers it as `remitBy` (`custody.remitBy` in the captain's availability) and `availability` stops the offers once it is past. `cashReminders(cityId, now)` gives each active captain's cash to hand in, whether some is from today, and `remitBy`, for the worker's reminders.
- **«أرصدة الكباتن» (AP §9.10.2):** `balances(cityId, now)` gives each captain of the city not terminated, and a terminated one still holding custody or earnings, with the custody, `blocks` (`closed_due` with no grace running, `cash_limit`), the grace and the `captain.earnings` balance in base; a captain whose cash cannot be read (a currency without a rate) is logged and left out, as `cashLimits` does; the cash desk adds its dates.
- **Availability (CA §7.1–7.2):** `availability.ts` holds the pure rules. `onlineBlocks` lists every block of going online at once: contract ended (alone), suspended, `closed_due` above `captain.remit_tolerance` with no hand-in grace running, at the cash limit, the terms in force not accepted (`terms_not_accepted`, K7; it never stops an online captain, CA §7.2), the app below `app.captain.min_version`, a simulated location. `availability` gives the state: offline, `online_available`, `busy` with an active order (no offers, no stacking in V1, AP §9.12.6), `not_receiving_offers` with its reasons (the cash limit, `closed_due` past `captain.remit_online_grace_hours` after a cutoff the captain was online across, a simulated location), or suspended, or `last_order` (below). Which captains have an active order (`accepted` to `arrived` with them as its captain) is ordering's to say: it registers `useActiveOrders` at start, so this module never reads its tables; going offline with an active order is refused (`409 active_order`, CA §15), under the captain's lock, which an assignment takes too. `hasActiveOrder` answers it for one captain (identity's sign-in refuses a second device then, CA §4.9).
- **The office (CA §7.5, C13; slice 2.17c):** `office(captainId, now)` answers the city's office from geo (`GeoService.office`: the point, the hours, the clock and whether the desk is open now, until when or when it opens next). `GET /v1/captain/availability`, going online, last-order mode and going offline answer it as `office` (null until the owner sets it), for the hand-in reminder and «عهدتي». `custodyEntries(captainId, limit)` gives the newest entries of the captain's custody accounts (CA §10.2) for the cash desk's route.
- **For «أرباحي» (CA §10.6, slice 2.18b):** `earnings(captainId, after)` answers the `captain.earnings` balance in the city's base and that account's entries posted after a moment (`LedgerService.periodEntries`, each with its journal's reason); `onlineMinutes(captainId, from, to)` the whole minutes online in a window, the open period counted up to `to`.
- **Online periods:** a row per stretch online; the open one (no `ended_at`, one per captain, a partial unique index) is the captain online. `goOnline` locks the captain row, checks the blocks and opens it; already online answers the state. `goOffline` closes it, then, after the commit so a failed read never undoes it, answers the cash to hand in above the tolerance, for the reminder (CA §7.5). An app version not in the form `1.2.3` is below any minimum. Settings and the city's clock are read before the transaction (`cityRules`), which then reads the ledger with its own connection.
- **Location (CA §4.3, architecture §7.3):** `recordLocations` keeps every point recorded inside one of the captain's online periods in `captains.locations`, so a point queued without a signal and sent after going offline still completes the route. It is partitioned by the UTC month of `recorded_at` (last month, this month and the next, created by `captains.create_month_partitions()`, which the worker calls at start and every day through `CaptainsWorkerModule`; append-only, `UPDATE` revoked on the parent and every partition). A point sent again is one row (the key is the captain and the recorded time); one recorded offline or before last month is dropped; one recorded more than a minute after the server clock is refused (`invalid_location`). The points are written while the open online period is held `FOR SHARE`, so going offline waits for them. A captain offline with no point inside an online period gets `409 not_online`. The newest point of the open period decides `simulatedLocation` in the state.
- **Live position (`live-positions.ts`):** the newest real point of the open online period in Valkey, a geo set per city (`captains:online:<cityId>`) and a hash per captain (`captains:position:<id>`, expiring 10 minutes after its last point), written by a Lua step only when newer and only while that period has not ended. Going offline, a suspension or a termination removes it and marks the ended period (`captains:ended:<id>`), so a write still on its way is refused. A simulated point is never the live position. Nothing there is the only copy, so a failed Valkey write is logged and never fails the request; a reader (dispatch, the live map) checks the hash's `recordedAt` and the captain's state in the database, since the geo set has no expiry.
- **Live position listeners (slice 2.29b):** `onLivePosition(listener)` is told after `recordLocations` writes a new live position, or when Valkey failed (the point is still the newest of its batch), never for a point older than the live one (`LivePositions.set` answers true, false or null); the request does not wait for the listeners, and a listener's failure is logged. Ordering publishes it to the order's channel after the pickup.
- **Eligible captains near a point (AP §9.12.6, §9.3.2):** `nearestEligible(cityId, restaurant, tripMetres, now)` reads the live positions within `dispatch.search_radius_km` nearest first (`LivePositions.near`, leaving out a point recorded more than 10 minutes ago, `LIVE_POSITION_MAX_AGE_MS`), or, when Valkey cannot answer, the newest real point of each open online period from `captains.locations` with the same rules (architecture §5.7); then, with the city's rules read once, each captain's record and state: online and receiving offers (`online_available`: active, under the cash limit, not blocked for unhanded cash), with `dispatch.max_distance_km.<vehicle>` at least the trip. `nearestEligible` answers the first eligible one with its vehicle and live position (for the search start of an accepted order), or null; `eligibleNear` whether there is one; a captain whose state cannot be read (such as a currency without a rate) is logged and left out, never failing the quote. A busy captain is not eligible; the maximum pickup amount is ignored, as the rule says. `candidates` gives the assignment picker every online captain of the city with a live position, nearest first by the straight line, each with its `IneligibleReason`s (`busy`, `cash_limit`, `closed_due`, `simulated_location`, `out_of_range` beyond the radius, `vehicle_not_allowed` for the trip, `above_max_pickup` given the order's pickup payment), eligible with none; the owner decided (2026-11-06) that dispatch measures as the capacity check does: the radius by the straight line from the live position, the vehicle by the order's kept trip.
- **Captain app routes** (`captain-availability.controller.ts`, `@AppSignedIn()` with the captain token): `GET /v1/captain/availability` (the state and the cash), `POST /v1/captain/availability/online` (the app version as `1.2.3` and whether a simulated location is active; refused `409 online_blocked` with every block; `terms_not_accepted` while the captain's account has not accepted the captain terms in force, K7, read by the controller through content's `TermsService.pending`), `POST /v1/captain/availability/offline` (the cash to hand in, or null) and `POST /v1/captain/location` (1 to 360 points, every 10 seconds or queued). Money travels as `{ amount, currency }`, the amount as a string of minor units.
- **Last-order mode (CA §7.3, §7.6, K13, K18; slice 2.13a):** the open online period's `last_order_by` (`captain`, `forced`, `suspended`, `terminated`), `last_order_at` and `last_order_reason` (operations' reason, exactly when forced; a `CHECK`). `availability` answers `last_order` (with who set it and the reason shown: a force offline's, or the suspension's) while the captain is online in the mode with an active order, and still once the order left until the period ends (a suspended or terminated captain then shows as such); dispatch offers only `online_available`, so no order slips in, and the picker shows `last_order`. `setLastOrder(captainId, on)` («لا أريد طلبات جديدة», `POST /v1/captain/availability/last-order`): refused offline (`not_online`), on without an active order (`no_active_order`), off for a mode the captain did not set (`last_order_not_yours`); on while on keeps it. `forceOffline(captainId, change)` (AP §9.7.5 «فصل من الاتصال», a reason required, audited `captain.force_offline`): with an active order the mode `forced` (replacing the captain's own), otherwise the period ends `forced` at once; an offline captain stays so, unrecorded. `afterOrderLeft(tx, captainId, now)`, called by ordering in the transaction that takes an order from its captain, ends the period with the mode's reason (`last_order`, `forced`, `suspended`, `terminated`) once no active order is left, or lifts a suspension's mode whose suspension ended; it returns what runs after the commit. `onAvailabilityChanged` listeners run in the transaction of a force offline and of a period a last order ended (notifications queue the push and the broadcast). The state answers `remitTolerance` too, so the app shows the hand-in reminder after a last order (CA §7.5).
- **Admin routes (`admin-captains.controller.ts`, `captains.manage`):** `GET /v1/admin/captains/{captainId}/availability` (the state and who set a last-order mode) and `POST /v1/admin/captains/{captainId}/force-offline` (`reason`; answers the state after it; no `Idempotency-Key`, a repeat changes nothing more).
- **Carried currencies (CA §10.5, slice 2.14a):** `captains.carried_currencies` (text[], null until the captain sets them: the base only). `carriedCurrencies(captainId)` answers the base, what is carried of the city's enabled currencies now (in display order) and the choices with their Arabic names (a city without a base: none); `setCarriedCurrencies` takes the city's enabled currencies, once each, one at least (`invalid_carried_currencies`), stored in display order and not audited (the captain's own preference). Routes: `GET`/`PUT /v1/captain/settings/carried-currencies` (`@AppSignedIn()`, no key: it sets a value). `captain(id)` gives `carriedCurrencies` as stored; ordering's proposal keeps only those of the order's snapshot.
- **«أقصى مبلغ أحمله» (CA §10.5, C4, K2; slice 2.15a):** `captains.max_pickup_amount` and its currency (null: no limit, the default; a `CHECK` keeps them together and above zero) and `max_pickup_set_at`, when the captain last answered, a limit or none. `maxPickup(captainId)` answers the limit, `answered` (the app asks at going online until then, K2) and the city's base; `setMaxPickup(captainId, amount | null)` stores an amount in the city's base under `lockCityCurrencies` (`not_in_base_currency` while the city has none; `invalid_max_pickup` at zero or below; `active_order` during an order, CA §15), not audited (the captain's own preference). Registered with `FxService.useCityAmounts` (`captain.max_pickup_amount`), so the base never changes under a limit (on staging, a captain's limit blocks a base change until it is cleared; to confirm with the owner). Routes: `GET`/`PUT /v1/captain/settings/max-pickup-amount` (`{ amount }` minor units as a string, or null; no key: it sets a value). `candidates(..., pickupPayment)` adds `above_max_pickup` when the order's pickup payment is above the limit (same currency); `nearestEligible` and the capacity check ignore it, as AP §9.12.6 says. `captain(id)` gives `maxPickup`.
- **Pay table (AP §9.12.5):** `pay_tables` are versions per city, never changed (the application role cannot `UPDATE`), each with one `pay_rates` row per vehicle; `setPayTable` needs a rate for every vehicle and a reason, and takes the city's `pay-table:` advisory lock. A city's first table takes effect when published or later; every later one 7 days or more after it is published, from that moment by default (owner decision 2026-11-06). The seed asks `hasPayTable`, never whether one is in force, so a table scheduled on staging is never replaced. `payTable(cityId, at)` takes the latest start reached; of two with the same start, the later version. The maximum distance per vehicle stays the setting `dispatch.max_distance_km.<vehicle>`.
- **Peak bonus (AP §9.12.5, D15; slice 2.16, owner decisions 2026-11-12):** `peak-bonus.ts` holds the pure rules, `PeakBonusService` the versions. `peak_bonus_versions` (per city, never changed: the application role cannot `UPDATE`; `enabled`, the base currency when saved, `effective_from`, the editor) each with its `peak_bonus_windows` (ISO days, start and end minutes of the city's day, an end before the start running past midnight and belonging to the day it starts; the amount per delivered order above zero; geo's zone ids, none for every zone). Off until the owner first saves it. `setPeakBonus` (the city's `peak-bonus:` advisory lock, under `lockCityCurrencies`, the base only: `not_in_base_currency` without one; `invalid_peak_bonus` for a window or a zone not of the city; audited `peak_bonus.set`) takes effect by `takesEffect`: at once unless the new version pays a zone less than a window running now at any minute before that window's end (a lowering, a disabling, a shortened end, a zone dropped), then at the latest end among such windows; with no window running, at once. A save that both raises and lowers waits as a whole (AP §9.12.5 lets a raise apply at once; to confirm with the owner). The audit keeps the version in force (`versionId`) and the waiting version a save replaces (`replaces`); an unknown city is `unknown_city` (`404`). `versionInForce` takes the latest version whose start was reached, leaving out one still waiting when a newer one was saved (a newer save replaces a waiting change). `bonusAt` pays the largest amount among the running windows that cover the restaurant's zone (`GeoService.zoneAt`; outside every zone, only the windows for every zone), never summed. `bonusFor(cityId, restaurant, at)` answers it with the version, null when none applies or the version is in an earlier base; `activeNow` the windows running now with their end and zones (CA §12.5); `state` the version in force and the one waiting. Registered with `FxService.useCityAmounts` for the version in force and the latest while each is on, so the base never moves under a running window. What the captain sees (`captainWindows`, `activeNow`) is the version in force only while it pays: on and in the city's base. Routes (`admin-peak-bonus.controller.ts`, tested in `peak-bonus.routes.test.ts`): `GET /v1/admin/peak-bonus?cityId=` (`zones.read`: the versions, the city's zones and its base currency) and `PUT /v1/admin/peak-bonus` (`pricing.edit_pay_tables`, OWN; no key: it sets the value sent); `activeCaptainIds(cityId, now)` lists the captains its window's push goes to (CA §14 #23: neither terminated nor in a suspension still running); the captain app's `GET /v1/captain/peak-bonus` (`captain-peak-bonus.controller.ts`, `@AppSignedIn()`) answers the windows in force while it is on and those running now with their end (CA §12.5). The city clock is read with `Intl` (`localClock`); a window's end is counted in minutes of the city's clock, so a clock change inside a window would move it (Syria keeps UTC+3).
- **For the handover (slice 2.12b, CA §9.10.1, K17):** `latestPoint(captainId)` gives the newest real point of the captain's open online period whatever its age (where the first captain waits); `offlineAfterHandover(tx, captainId, now)` ends the open online period with `handed_over` in the receipt's transaction under the captain's lock, refused while the captain still has an active order, and returns what runs after the commit (the live position removed, the listeners told).
- **For «لا أستطيع إكمال الطلب» and «طوارئ» (slices 2.13b, 2.13c, K17):** `offlineAfterProblem(tx, captainId, reason, now)` ends the open online period with `cannot_complete` or `emergency` in ordering's transaction under the captain's lock, whatever last-order mode it had, refused while the captain still has an active order; the listeners hear `offline_after_order` (the app reads its state again) and the answer runs after the commit (the live position removed, `onWentOffline`).
- **For dispatch:** `assignmentLock(cityId, now)` reads the city's rules, then gives the function that locks a captain row in dispatch's transaction and answers its state; `offlineForUnansweredOffers(captainId, now, count)` ends the online period with `offers_expired` (C3), never with an active order, and in its transaction tells the availability listeners (`offers_expired` with the count: the notifications module's push «لم ترد على {العدد} عروض متتالية…», CA §7.4, §14 #3, and the captain's channel broadcast); while offline after it, the captain's availability answers `offersExpired` (`captain.offer_expiry_offline_count`; null otherwise and once a new period starts), so the app says why (slice 2.15b); `onlineSince` gives the open period's start (C3 counts its offers only); `onWentOffline` listeners run after any online period ends and commits (going offline, a suspension, a termination, C3), so dispatch withdraws the captain's open offer, and a listener's failure is only logged; `orderPay(payTableId, vehicle, metres)` gives an order's pay by the pay table it kept, before waiting.
- **Pay of an order (`pay.ts`):** the base pay covers the included metres; each metre past them pays `per_extra_km / 1000`, rounded down to the minor unit; the base and distance pay are raised to the minimum pay, the top-up kept apart; waiting pays per whole minute past `captain.waiting_threshold_min` on top, never absorbed by the minimum (docs/domain/04; owner decision 2026-11-06). `orderPay` returns the parts (`PayParts`: base, distance, top-up, the order pay, waiting) for the weekly statement (AP §8.5.5) and the summary (CA §9.8); `payParts` reads them by the order's pay table, and the delivery posts `earn.order_pay` and `earn.waiting_pay` apart.
- **For the alerts (AP §9.1.3):** `cashLimits(cityId, now)` gives each captain not terminated and whether its usage reached 100% of its limit (null when its cash cannot be read); `lastFixes(captainIds, since)` the newest point of each since a moment, which `ordering` reads for «انقطع اتصال كابتن أثناء طلب».
- **Time:** services take `now`; the pay table's 7 days count from the database clock, as the commission's start does.
