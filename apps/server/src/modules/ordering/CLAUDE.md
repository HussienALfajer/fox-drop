# ordering module

The quote, the order, its state machine, edits and outcomes (architecture §4.2). So far the versioned quote of a cart (CU §10.1–10.6; AP §8.4, §8.6), placing the order (CU §10.7; AP §8.1, §4.8; RA §7.4; D23) and the restaurant's acceptance and «الطلب جاهز» (RA §8.5–8.8, §18), relaying its events to the apps (architecture §5.5, §6.2), and the reads the apps fetch for the full state (CU §11, RA §8).

## Rules

- **The quote is computed, never stored:** `POST /v1/customer/quote` (open to guests, who see the full quote, CU §5.1) takes the restaurant, the delivery point, the cart lines, «سأدفع بـ» and the app version, and answers the priced lines, the money, the equivalents, the estimate, the minimum-order and free-delivery status, the rate versions, every blocking problem and the version. The app asks again whenever the cart, the address or the code changes, and on opening checkout. A restaurant customers do not see, or one suspended while the quote is computed, answers `404 unknown_restaurant`. «سأدفع بـ» is not binding (CU §10.5): a currency the city no longer accepts gives `payIn: null`, never a refusal.
- **Lines (`priceLines` in `quote.ts`):** each line at the menu's current prices through `MerchantsService.menu(id, now)`. Hidden rows are `item_not_found`; an unavailable item `item_unavailable`; an option not on the item `option_not_found`; a repeated choice or a group's count outside its minimum and maximum `options_invalid`; an unavailable choice `option_unavailable`; a price outside the base currency `price_not_in_base_currency` (not checked while the city has no base: the quote shows `rates_unavailable`). Such a line has no price and stays out of the money. Every line carries the `knownUnitPrice` the app last showed; a line whose unit price differs is priced and flagged `price_changed` (CU §10.1), so the customer sees «تغيّر سعر…» before confirming. Quantities are 1 to 20 and item notes up to 100 characters without a run of 7 or more digits (Q25), checked by the route's schema.
- **Money (`quoteMoney`, AP §8.4):** items value + delivery fee − discount, never below 0, rounded to the base currency's unit and mode from the rate snapshot (`rounding`, 0 without a snapshot). The discount is 0 until codes arrive (Phase 2, owner decision 2026-11-06). The fee is `GeoService.deliveryFee` for the items value; the equivalents and «ستدفع حوالي» are `equivalent` from fx, each currency by its own rounding.
- **Thresholds (CU §10.2):** the minimum order and the free-delivery threshold compare the items value before discounts; below the minimum is `below_minimum_order` with what is missing. The threshold is the one of the fee rule the fee was priced with (`DeliveryFee.freeDeliveryFrom`), so the two never disagree. A minimum order in another currency than the base (allowed until the first order locks it) is `minimum_not_in_base_currency`.
- **Checks (CU §10.6; owner decision 2026-11-06):** built: the app version (`app.customer.min_version` compared part by part, `versionBelow`, `update_required`) and maintenance, the platform hours (`platform.hours` on the city clock, `platform_closed` with the next opening), the zone (`outside_zones`) and the restaurant's coverage (`not_covered`), the restaurant's state (`restaurant_closed`, with the state in the answer, `busy` at its maximum active orders), the lines, the minimum order, a fee rule in the base currency (`delivery_unpriced`), a rate snapshot (`rates_unavailable`) and a captain pay table in force (`captain_pay_unset`, AP §9.12.5: without one no order could be delivered). Every problem blocks ordering. The route is `@AppSignedInOrGuest()`: a guest gets the quote without a token; a signed-in customer's quote also checks their active orders (D23, CU §5.5, `ActiveOrders` in `active-orders.ts`: `placed` to `arrived`, against `customer.new_max_active_orders`, or `customer.trusted_max_active_orders` after `customer.trusted_after_delivered_orders` delivered), `one_order_at_a_time` for a new account and `active_order_limit` for a trusted one, the two texts of CU §5.5, and the customer terms in force (`terms_pending` while the account has not accepted them, CU §4.7; `TermsService.pending` from content), and the city's emergency stop (`emergency_stop`, with the stop's browsing message in the problem's `message`, AP §9.1.6). The capacity check (AP §9.12.6) runs while `dispatch.capacity_check` is on: `CaptainsService.eligibleNear` for the restaurant and the trip's distance, `no_captain_nearby` («الضغط عالٍ الآن، جرّب بعد دقائق») without an eligible captain. It costs a few reads per nearby captain, so it runs only when no other problem blocks the order. Tests that are not about it turn it off. Passing until their slices: account states and codes.
- **The version (`quoteVersion`):** a hash of the lines' prices, the money, the equivalents, the rate versions and the blocking problems, so a new estimate alone never changes it (CU §10.6). The order command carries it and compares it. A new fee rule that charges the same keeps the version; the order keeps the rule it was priced with.
- **Placing the order (`orders.service.ts`, CU §10.7):** `POST /v1/customer/orders` (`@AppSignedIn()`) takes the cart as the quote does, the quote version, the address (`addressId` of a saved one, or `unsavedAddress` with the fields of CU §7.4: exactly one), the note (up to 200 characters, no run of 7 or more digits, Q25) and «سأدفع بـ», and answers `201` with the order as placed. Steps:
  1. A repeat of the key (below) answers its order before anything else, and again before any refusal is answered: a repeat sent with the first can be refused by what the first changed.
  2. `CustomersService.orderAddress` copies the address with «الحي» from the zone at the pin now (Q6): `404 unknown_address`, `409 outside_zones`.
  3. `QuoteService.price` re-runs the quote for the customer at the pin through the pool, before the transaction, so a placing never holds a connection while it waits for another. Any problem: `409 order_blocked` with the quote; another version: `409 quote_changed` with the quote (Q10).
  4. One transaction: the customer's advisory lock, then the restaurant's (always in that order); the customer's active orders (D23); the restaurant's (`placed`, `accepted`, `ready`) against its maximum (RA §7.4); `FxService.snapshotForOrder`, which locks the base and reads through the transaction, compared with the quote's rates and rounding; the city's number; the order, its lines, its first event with its `order.placed` outbox row, the automatic cancellation job and the key. A check failed here (a limit reached, an `FxRefusedError`, other rates) rolls back and re-runs the quote, which shows it: `409 order_blocked` with its problem (the restaurant `busy`, the customer at the limit), or else `409 quote_changed`.
- **The restaurant's commands (`order-commands.ts`, RA §8.5–8.8, §18, §4.7):** `POST /v1/merchant/orders/{orderId}/accept` (`{ prepMin }`) and `POST /v1/merchant/orders/{orderId}/ready`, `@AppSignedIn()` for owners and cashiers alike, with `Idempotency-Key`; a terminated restaurant gets `403 restaurant_terminated`, another restaurant's order `404 unknown_order`. `OrderCommands.run` replays the key, reads the order without a lock and checks its state, reads whatever is slow before the transaction (the settings, the nearest captain and its distance), then in one transaction locks the order, replays the key again (a repeat sent with the first answers its order, also when it read the order after the first committed), checks the state again, writes the change, `recordTransition` (actor `restaurant` with the account and the app installation, `actor_device`), its timers and the key. A state the command no longer allows answers `409 order_state_changed` with the order as it is now (accepted from another device with its device name, cancelled first), which the app explains (RA §4.7). Both answer the board's order. Keys live in `idempotency_keys` per account, whichever party's (`idempotency-keys.ts`).
- **Accept (RA §8.5, docs/domain/03, AP §8.3):** a `placed` order becomes `accepted` with the prep time, any whole minute from `merchant.prep_time_floor_min` to `merchant.prep_time_ceiling_min` (`400 invalid_prep_time`), not only the chips. It sets together (a `CHECK`): `accepted_at`, `prep_min`, `promised_at` (acceptance + prep + the trip kept at placing + `order.eta_buffer_min`, rounded up to the next 5 minutes), `search_start_at` and `prep_late_alert_at` (`order.prep_late_ops_alert_min` past the prep time). The search start (owner decision 2026-11-06) is computed once: the nearest eligible captain (`CaptainsService.nearestEligible`, for the trip's metres) and its distance to the restaurant by its vehicle (`GeoService.distance`) at `order.eta_speed_kmh` give its ETA; the search starts at acceptance + prep − ETA − `dispatch.search_start_buffer_min`, at once when that has passed or without a captain. Its job (`QUEUES.searchStart`) is sent in the transaction with the moment it was set for and `dispatch.no_captain_alert_min` then; the worker's `OrderTimers.searchStart` locks the order and acts only while it is `accepted` or `ready` with no captain, its search not started and its `search_start_at` still the job's (a moved moment leaves the old job without effect): the search starts (`search_started_at`, event `order.search_started`), the captain track becomes `searching` unless an offer is already open, and operations' «طلب بلا كابتن» moment is `search_alert_at`. Manual mode (D6) is the only one built, so every order then needs assignment, «بحاجة لتعيين», while its track is `searching`. The board's `prepTime` gives the chooser: the chips, the step, the floor, the ceiling and the preselected `defaultMin` (the default prep time plus the busy-mode minutes while the restaurant is open in busy mode, as in the quote's estimate, within the limits).
- **Reject (RA §8.6, R3, S2; slice 2.7a):** `POST /v1/merchant/orders/{orderId}/reject` (`reason`, `items`, `busyExtraMin`, `pauseMin`; owners and cashiers, `Idempotency-Key`) runs through `OrderCommands.run` as accept does. `rejectionProblem` is the pure rule: a code of `REJECTION_REASONS` (Content's closed `merchant_rejection` list, `400 unknown_reason`, also for a code deactivated in Content); «صنف غير متوفر» needs one or more of the order's items, each once, each «حتى الغد» or «حتى أعيده»; «ضغط طلبات» takes busy mode (one of `merchant.busy_extra_options_min`) or «إيقاف 30 د», not both; anything else is `400 invalid_rejection`. A `placed` order becomes `rejected` and keeps `rejected_at`, the code, its label, the customer's text copied from Content with «{الأصناف}» filled with the items' names (CU Appendix C) and the items' ids (`reject_item_ids`, empty for the other reasons), all together (a `CHECK`). In the same transaction, after the order's lock (the order then the restaurant, as the auto-pause): the items switched off (`MerchantsService.switchOffForRejection`, the end from `switchedOffUntil` read before the transaction; an item deleted since is left alone), or `RestaurantStatusService.afterRejection` for the pause «حتى أعيد الفتح» of «المطعم مغلق» or the choice of «ضغط طلبات», each set only when the restaurant could set it now (open or busy, no operations or auto pause), else nothing and the rejection stands. The event `order.rejected` publishes the code as its reason. The restaurant's board drops the order (it lists `placed` to `arrived`); the customer's order gives `rejection` (the code, the text and `unavailableItemIds` for «اطلب بدون الأصناف غير المتوفرة», CU §11.9).
- **On the restaurant's behalf (AP §9.3.2, RA §8.15; slice 2.7a):** `POST /v1/admin/orders/{orderId}/accept` (`prepMin`, `reason`) and `.../reject` (`rejection` the code, `items`, `busyExtraMin`, `pauseMin`, `reason`), `operations.manage`, `Idempotency-Key`, answer the card. They are `OrderCommands.acceptFor` and `rejectFor`, the restaurant's commands on any order with the actor `admin`: the event row keeps operations' note and publishes only the code (or null for an acceptance), the side effects and the audit (`order.accept`, `order.reject`, with the note) share the transaction, and the side effects are audited with the admin. Every outbox payload carries `byOperations` (the actor is an admin; absent, so false, before 2.7a), which the relay publishes to the restaurant, the panel and the captain, so the restaurant's devices know operations acted; the board's `acceptance.byOperations` gives «قبلته العمليات», and operations' detail gives `rejection` (the code, label, customer text, items and `byOperations`, from the rejection's event). While placed, the detail also gives `onBehalf` (`OrderCommands.onBehalfChoices`, slice 2.7b): the restaurant's prep-time chooser, Content's active `merchant_rejection` reasons and `merchant.busy_extra_options_min`, which the panel's dialogs offer; each detail line carries its `itemId` for the items of «صنف غير متوفر». Pushes: `order.rejected` apologises to the customer with the restaurant's name and the reason's customer text (CU §14 #7) and, by operations, tells the restaurant the label (RA §16 #6); `order.accepted` by operations tells the restaurant the prep time (RA §16 #5).
- **Ready (RA §8.8, AP §8.3):** an `accepted` order becomes `ready`, early or late, with no undo; it sets `ready_at` and `no_captain_alert_at` (`dispatch.no_captain_after_ready_min` after it), since no captain is assigned yet. A ready order has no prep time left, so a search start still ahead moves to now, with a new job (AP §9.12.6). The `orders_ready` `CHECK` asks only that a `ready` order has its `ready_at`, so a later pickup straight from `accepted` (AP §8.1) can record it alone. Operations' alerts at `prep_late_alert_at` and `no_captain_alert_at` are moments the operations board shows once passed (owner decision 2026-11-06); the latter only while no captain is assigned.
- **What is atomic (owner decision 2026-11-06, CU §10.7 item 6):** the two limits, the rate snapshot and the number are checked under the locks; the quote's other checks (menu, prices, restaurant state, zone) run just before the transaction, as their reads would take no lock inside it under READ COMMITTED. A pause or suspension committed in that window (the distance call takes up to 1 s) still lets the order in; the restaurant's acceptance and the automatic cancellation are the last safeguard. Never call the distance or another slow service inside the order's transaction.
- **The order (`schema.ts`):** `orders`, `order_lines` and `order_events`, partitioned by the UTC month of placing (this month's and the next, created by `ordering.create_month_partitions()`, which the worker calls at start and every day through `OrderingWorkerModule`); the lines and events carry the order's `placed_at`. The order keeps the money as the quote priced it (a `CHECK` that the total adds up), the rate snapshot (its bigints as strings), the fee rule, the commission and the city's pay table version in force, the address snapshot (another contact number stays encrypted with identity's key) and the estimate shown. `version` starts at 1 and every transition increments it with an `order_events` row (append-only, `UPDATE` revoked on every partition).
- **Transitions (architecture §5.5, `order-events.ts`):** each one locks the order (`lockOrder`, by its id and `placed_at`), checks its state and version, writes the new state and version, then `recordTransition` writes the `order_events` row, the `outbox` row and a relay job (`QUEUES.outboxRelay`) in the same transaction. A change of the captain track alone (AP §8.2: `order.search_started`, `order.offered`, `order.offer_closed`, `order.assigned`, `order.captain_arrived`, and the pickup's `order.pickup_requested`, `order.pickup_rejected`, `order.pickup_disputed`) is a transition too, from a status to the same status: it increments the version, and its event row keeps its `type` and the captain it is about (`captain_id`); the outbox payload carries the track and the captain.
- **The outbox (E4):** `ordering.outbox`, one row per transition (`order.placed`, `order.accepted`, `order.ready`, `order.cancelled`, and the captain track's `order.search_started`, `order.offered`, `order.offer_closed`, `order.assigned`, `order.captain_arrived`, the pickup's `order.pickup_requested`, `order.pickup_rejected`, `order.picked_up`, `order.pickup_disputed`, and `order.arrived_at_customer` and `order.delivered`) with the order version and a payload of version 1 (`schema: 1`, the order, its number, restaurant, customer, status, version, reason, time). The application role inserts rows and may change only `published_at`.
- **The relay (`outbox-relay.ts`, architecture §5.5):** `OutboxRelay.drain` takes the rows with no `published_at` in id order, 20 per transaction, `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, publishes each with `RealtimePublisher.broadcast` to `merchant:{restaurant_id}`, the panel's `ops:city.{city_id}` and the captain's channel (`publication`: the event's `type` with the payload's order, number, restaurant, status, version, reason, time and captain track, without the ids only the server needs; idempotency key `outbox:<id>`), then to `order:{order_id}` without the captain track and without the captain's own steps (`CUSTOMER_SKIPS`: the search, offers and the pickup request's steps; CU §11.2; key `outbox:<id>:order`), and marks them in that transaction: at least once, never twice at once. Ids are taken at insert, not at commit, so it keeps no «last published id»; clients order events by the order version and ignore one not newer than the one they show (RA §4.3), since a row whose mark was lost is published again. Centrifugo out of reach stops the run; a row it refuses is logged and skipped for that run, so it never holds back the others; either way the run throws, so the queue tries again with its backoff and reports it. In the worker (`OutboxRelayWorker`) every transition's job wakes a run (polling every 0.5 s, 50 jobs per call), one run at start takes the rows written while no worker ran, and one a minute those left by runs that gave up (the queue's retries end after about an hour). While real time is off (no `CENTRIFUGO_API_URL` and `CENTRIFUGO_API_KEY`), the worker logs it and the rows wait; in production it does not start. Proved by `outbox-relay.test.ts` and, through Centrifugo to a client, `order-realtime.test.ts`.
- **Pushes (`order-pushes.ts`, `outbox-pushes.ts`; architecture §8.1, CU §14, RA §16, CA §14; slice 2.3a, owner decision 2026-11-08: the rows whose events exist):** `orderPushes(type, reason, facts)` is the pure rule of which push each event sends to whom, with its template and values: `order.placed` rings the restaurant (RA #1); `order.accepted` tells the customer the promised time (CU #1); `order.ready` the assigned captain (CA #5); `order.cancelled` apologises to the customer with the reason's customer text (CU #8, AP §8.3) and, for `merchant_auto_cancel`, tells the restaurant its unanswered orders of that cancellation's business day up to it and `merchant.auto_pause_unanswered_count` (RA #2); `order.offered` offers the captain the order pay and the restaurant (CA #1; dispatch passes the offer's pay as `offerPay` in the outbox payload); `order.assigned` names the captain to the customer (CU #2) and tells the captain only when operations assigned it without an offer (CA #4, the event's actor `admin`); `order.captain_arrived` and `order.pickup_requested` (with the pickup payment) the restaurant (RA #9, #13); `order.pickup_rejected` the captain with the restaurant's answer (CA #8, CA §9.4); `order.picked_up` the customer, the restaurant (RA #14, or #15 when operations confirmed) and the captain (CA #7); `order.pickup_disputed` the captain (CA #9); `order.arrived_at_customer` the customer with the total, or the recipient's text (CU #4, §11.7); `order.delivered` the customer (CU #6). The search and an offer closing are silent. The worker's `OutboxPushes` (every `OUTBOX_PUSH_MS`, 1 s, apart from the real-time relay, so neither holds the other back) reads the rows with no `push_queued_at` and what their pushes say (the order, its event's actor, the captain the event was about, the pickup request opened by the event's time, the names, the city's clock and the currencies) through the pool; a row whose pushes cannot be read is logged and reported once and left for the next run, never holding back the rows after it; then in one transaction locks those still unqueued (`SKIP LOCKED`), queues each push rendered with `NotificationTexts` through `PushQueue` (source `outbox:<id>:<party>:<template>`, at the event's time so its expiry counts from it; an offer's ends with the offer, `offerExpiresAt` in the outbox payload), and sets `push_queued_at`; the application role may change only `published_at` and `push_queued_at`. Migration 0034 marked every earlier event queued. Money shows as the apps show it (`formatMoney`: «120 ₺», «10.50 $») and times on the city's 12-hour clock (`formatTime`: «9:45 م»).
- **The emergency stop (AP §9.1.6, D7; `emergency-stop.ts`, slice 2.4c):** `ordering.emergency_stops`, one row per stop, kept after it ends; a partial unique index keeps one open per city, and starting, resuming and the automatic resume of a city take turns on an advisory lock. The application role may change only its end columns (`ended_at`, `ended_by`, `end_kind`, `service_back_message`), with `CHECK`s that a manual end names its admin and an automatic one none, and that only a manual end carries «الخدمة عادت». `POST /v1/admin/emergency-stop` (`emergency_stop.toggle`: OWN, OPS) takes the dialog: the internal reason (the audit's reason), the browsing message, the delay notice with `{السبب}`, the customer-facing reason and the optional `resumeAt` (`400 resume_at_past`; `404 unknown_city`; `409 already_stopped`). **New orders refused at once:** placing reads the stop again inside its transaction, under the shared side of the advisory lock a start takes (`stopsOrders`, after the restaurant's lock), so an order commits before the stop (and its customer gets the delay notice) or is refused (`Requote`, then `order_blocked`). One transaction (E3, E4): the stop, the `QUEUES.emergencyResume` job for `resumeAt`, the delay push to every customer with an active order in the city (`placed` to `arrived`; one per customer, opening their latest active order, its text the notice with `{السبب}` filled, CU §14 #18, template key `customer.emergency_delay`), the platform broadcast (`PlatformBroadcasts` from notifications, with the active orders' ids, so their `order:` channels hear it too; the end's broadcast names the orders active then) and the audit `emergency_stop.start`. `POST /v1/admin/emergency-stop/resume` («استئناف», same permission) names the stop the panel shows (`404 unknown_stop`, `409 stop_ended`, so it never ends a newer one) and an optional `serviceBack` text: «الخدمة عادت» (CU §14 #19, `customer.service_back`) to each customer who opened checkout during the stop; audited `emergency_stop.resume`. The worker's `EmergencyStopWorker` runs the automatic resume: it ends the stop if still open, with no push (AP §9.1.6), audited with the actor `system`. Neither command takes an `Idempotency-Key`: a repeat is refused (`already_stopped`, `stop_ended`). `GET /v1/admin/emergency-stop?cityId=` (`@SignedIn()`: every panel user sees the bar) answers the open stop with its attempts; the dialog's prefills from `platform.emergency_default_message_*`, the internal reason and who started it only to `emergency_stop.toggle` (null for the others: those settings are OWN's, owner decision 2026-11-05, and AP §9.1.6 gives OPS the dialog). **Attempts (CU §10.6):** the quote the app asks on opening checkout (`openedCheckout: true`; the cart's quotes leave it false) of a signed-in customer during the stop writes `ordering.emergency_stop_attempts` (one row per stop and customer, never changed); a guest is not counted, having no account (to confirm with the owner). **The apps' read:** `GET /v1/customer/emergency-stop?latitude=&longitude=` (public; the app knows no city, so `GeoService.locate`) answers `active`, `startedAt`, the browsing message and the delay notice filled; `GET /v1/merchant/emergency-stop` and `GET /v1/captain/emergency-stop` (`@AppSignedIn()`) answer `active` and `startedAt` for the session's restaurant's or captain's city. The apps read it on connecting and on each `platform.emergency_stop` publication.
- **«إعادة إرسال الإشعار» (AP §9.3.2, `resend-notification.ts`):** `POST /v1/admin/orders/{orderId}/resend-notification` (`{ to: customer | merchant | captain }`; OWN, OPS and SUP: `operations.manage` or `operations.compensate`, `@RequireAnyPermission`, checked by the guard before the body) queues again the order's latest push to that party among the templates that tell its current state (`currentTemplates`: the restaurant hears «طلب جديد» again only while the order is placed, the captain never an offer once assigned) (`PushQueue.resend`) while the order is active (`placed` to `arrived`; the captain's once assigned), audited as `order.resend_notification`; `409 order_state_changed` or `nothing_to_resend`, `404 unknown_order`. A repeat sends again, which is what the button asks, so it takes no `Idempotency-Key`.
- **The reads (`order-views.ts`, CU §4.3, §11; RA §4.3, §8.2–8.4):** what an app fetches on opening, after every reconnect before it shows anything, and every 30 seconds while the customer's socket is down. `GET /v1/customer/orders/active` (the active-order bar, newest first) and `GET /v1/customer/orders/{orderId}` (`404 unknown_order` for another's) answer the order as the placing command does: its version, its cancellation, the restaurant's names and the total's equivalents by the order's own snapshot. `GET /v1/merchant/orders` answers the board (with the captain line of RA §8.1, §8.9: the captain track, and from assignment the captain's first name and vehicle, never the phone), every order from `placed` to `arrived` and a delivered one within its pickup dispute window, oldest first, with the time it turns late (`accept_alert_at`) and the automatic cancellation's time (`auto_cancel_at`), both set at placing from the settings then in force, the acceptance (time, prep time, `prepDueAt` for the prep countdown and lateness of RA §8.7, the device's name) and the ready time, and only what RA §8.4 lets the restaurant see: the customer's first name and «الحي», the items and the items value, never the delivery fee, the total, the address, a phone, the promised time or operations' moments (R11, principle 6); the customer's order adds `promisedAt` («يصل حوالي {الساعة}», null while placed) and, from assignment, the captain card (first name, vehicle and `atRestaurant` for the subtitles of CU §11.2, never the phone or the captain track); a terminated restaurant's owners get `403 restaurant_terminated`. `readOrders` reads the orders and their lines in two queries, and the names come in one read per party (`MerchantsService.names`, `CustomersService.firstNames`); each route picks its fields, so a new field is never shown to a party by accident.
- **The orders' side of dispatch (`order-assignments.ts`, AP §8.2, §9.3.2):** `OrderAssignments` (exported with `OrderAssignmentsModule`, which the API and the worker import) gives the `dispatch` module what it reads of an order (`find`, and `lock` in its transaction), whether it can take a captain (`assignable`: `accepted` or `ready`, no captain, no open offer), and writes each change of the captain track with its event and outbox row (`change`: `order.offered`; `order.offer_closed`, back to `searching` once the search started or to no track before; `order.assigned` with `captain_id` and `assigned_at`). It registers with `CaptainsService.useActiveOrders` which captains have an active order (`accepted` to `arrived` as its captain), so such a captain is busy in every process, and counts each captain's orders assigned since a moment for the picker.
- **The captain's side (`captain-orders.ts`, CA §9, §15):** `GET /v1/captain/orders/active` answers the order assigned to the signed-in captain from `accepted` to `arrived`, or null (no stacking, AP §9.12.6): the restaurant (name, address, phone for «اتصال بالمطعم», location), the preparation (ready, or `prepDueAt`), the arrival, the lines with their options and notes for the item check (C7), the item count, the note, «تدفع للمطعم» and «تقبض من الزبون», and the customer block of CA §9.5 (C2: from assignment): the recipient's name when the order is for someone else or the customer's first name, the number «اتصال بالزبون» dials (the address's contact number, else the account's own through `CustomerAccountsService.phone`; the app shows it nowhere else), the pin, the address (no label: CA §9.5 lists the building, floor, landmark and directions), «سأدفع بـ», the total's equivalents, the promised time and the arrival. C10: the number is given only while the order is this captain's and active, so a command replayed after the order ended or left the captain answers none. The app fetches it on opening, after every reconnect and when its channel says the order was assigned. Captain commands (`CaptainOrders`) run as the restaurant's do: replay the key (the captain's app account), read the order without a lock (another captain's is `404 unknown_order`), the slow reads, then one transaction that locks the order, replays again, checks the track again and writes the change, its event (actor `captain` with the app installation) and the key; a state the command no longer allows is `409 order_state_changed` with the captain's active order now. Every captain command carries the fix (`location`: the point, its accuracy, its time, whether simulated), recorded with `CaptainsService.recordLocations` before the transaction (CA §15); a command's hash leaves the fix out, so a retry with a newer fix replays.
- **Arrival (CA §9.2, C6, K3; RA §8.9):** `POST /v1/captain/orders/{orderId}/arrive` needs the order `accepted` or `ready` with the track `assigned` and this captain; the fix within `captain.merchant_arrival_radius_m` of the restaurant by the straight line (`409 too_far_from_restaurant` with `distanceMetres`), recorded within the last minute (`FIX_MAX_AGE_MS`, `409 stale_location`: C6 uses the latest fix), its accuracy 100 m or better (`WEAK_GPS_METRES`, `409 weak_location`: «إشارة الموقع ضعيفة — اتصل بالعمليات لتسجيل وصولك») and not simulated (`409 simulated_location`; a mock location proves nothing). It sets the track `at_merchant`, `arrived_at_merchant_at` and `captain_waiting_alert_at` (`captain.waiting_threshold_min` later; a `CHECK` keeps them together and present at the restaurant), event `order.captain_arrived`. Operations' «تسجيل وصول الكابتن» (`POST /v1/admin/orders/{orderId}/record-arrival`, `operations.manage`, a reason, `Idempotency-Key`) does the same for the assigned captain, actor `admin` with the reason, audited as `order.record_arrival`, and answers the board's card. An operations note stays in the event row and the audit: `recordTransition`'s `publicReason` publishes null on the apps' channels (AP §10, RA §8.4). The waiting alert is a moment the reads show (owner decision 2026-11-06): the restaurant's board has `captainArrival` (`at`, `waitingAlertAt`, the app chimes «الكابتن ينتظر الطلب…» at it) while the track is `at_merchant` or `pickup_pending`, and operations' card has `captainArrivedAt`, `captainWaitingAlertAt` and the alert `captain_waiting` («كابتن ينتظر عند المطعم») once passed while the captain is still at the restaurant before pickup.
- **Arrival at the customer (CA §9.6, K4; owner decision 2026-11-06):** `POST /v1/captain/orders/{orderId}/arrive-at-customer` (the fix and `away`) needs the order `picked_up` with the track `picked_up` and this captain; the fix follows the restaurant arrival's rules (`checkFix`: not simulated, within `FIX_MAX_AGE_MS`, `WEAK_GPS_METRES`). Within `captain.customer_radius_m` of the pin by the straight line it needs nothing; beyond it, `away` (a code of Content's `captain_away` list with the text «أخرى» needs, checked by `ContentService.choose` before the transaction: `400 unknown_reason`, `reason_note_required`, `reason_note_not_allowed`; the order keeps the code, a copy of the label in `away_reason_label` and the note, owner decision 2026-11-07), else `409 too_far_from_customer` with `distanceMetres`. A shortfall's reason is checked the same way against the `shortfall` list and kept in `collections` with `shortfall_reason_label`. The rows' `CHECK`s only ask that a label or note goes with a reason; a row written by a version before Content has no label, and the views show its code. A reason given within the radius is not kept. The order becomes `arrived` (track `at_customer`, `arrived_at_customer_at`, `away_reason`, `away_note`; a `CHECK`), event `order.arrived_at_customer` with the reason in its row and `publicReason: null`, so the customer sees the status «الكابتن عند الباب» only. The reason covers the delivery too (CA §9.8). Operations' card raises `away_from_address` («تسليم بعيد عن العنوان», information) while the order is active (`UNTIL_ENDED`), its time in the status counts from the arrival (and a `picked_up` order's from the pickup), and the detail gives `customer.arrival` with the reason.
- **The door payment (`door-payment.ts`, AP §8.6, CA §9.7; owner decision 2026-11-06: every accepted currency of the order's snapshot):** `checkDoorPayment` takes the received lines (one per currency, above zero, at least one) and the change lines (one per currency, above zero, none allowed), converts them with the order's snapshot (`toBase`) and nets them; a gap from the amount due within half the largest rounding unit among the currencies of all the lines is rounding (`roundingGap`), a net above it beyond that is `overpaid` («سجّل الباقي الذي أعدته للزبون»), below it a `shortfall`. A currency the snapshot did not accept is `currency_not_accepted`. `waitingMinutes` counts whole minutes from the later of the arrival at the restaurant and the prep due time, plus the threshold kept with the arrival (`captain_waiting_alert_at` − `arrived_at_merchant_at`), until the pickup (docs/domain/04). `commission` is `commission_bp` on the items value, half up to the minor unit (no codes, so no restaurant-funded discount, before Phase 2).
- **The delivery (`captain-orders.ts`, CA §9.7–9.8, C9, K4, K16):** `POST /v1/captain/orders/{orderId}/deliver` (`received`, `change`, `amountDue`, `shortfall`, `away` and the fix) needs the order `arrived` with the track `at_customer` and this captain (AP §8.1: the captain delivers from arrived only). The fix follows `checkFix`; beyond `captain.customer_radius_m` it needs `away` now unless one was given at the arrival, which is kept (`409 too_far_from_customer`). `amountDue` must be the order total the screen showed, in the order's currency (`409 amount_due_changed` with the order; operations' edits will change it), each line at most 15 digits, a shortfall needs its reason (Appendix C: `customer_short`, `counting_error`, `other` with its text; `409 shortfall_reason_required` with `shortfall`), and a reason without a shortfall is not kept. Everything slow runs before the transaction (`prepareDelivery`: the captain's vehicle, `CaptainsService.payParts` by the order's pay table and trip, the restaurant's status). The transaction locks the order, checks it again and calls `DeliveryBooking.complete`; the answer is the summary of CA §9.8 (`pay` in its parts, `custodyChange` the net collected minus the pickup payment, `handInToday` the captain's custody in every currency at current rates, in the base, below zero when the desk owes the captain, CA §10.2), read back from the collection, so a repeat of the key answers it again. A delivered order leaves the captain's active orders, so the captain is free at once.
- **The atomic delivery (`delivery-booking.ts`, AP §8.1, §8.4–8.6, §8.9):** in one transaction, the order `delivered` (track `done`, `delivered_at`, `outcome` `normal`; a `CHECK` that a delivered order has both and a finished one an outcome), a `collections` row (one per order: the lines, the amount due, the net, the shortfall and its reason, the pay parts and waiting minutes, who recorded it), the journals, and `order.delivered` with `publicReason: null`. The journals, all with the order as their source: `custody.collect` per currency received and `custody.change_given` per currency of change (the captain's custody against `platform.order_clearing` in that currency); one `platform.fx_rounding` journal bringing each other currency's net into the base at the order's rates (the clearing account in that currency back to zero, `platform.fx_rounding` holding the currency position); `platform.revenue_delivery` for the fee; `platform.fx_rounding` for the total's rounding less the door's gap within rounding; `merchant.commission` against `platform.revenue_commission` (none on a test order: `test_order`, set at placing from the restaurant's `test` status, AP §9.6.3); `earn.order_pay` and `earn.waiting_pay` apart against `platform.captain_pay` (`platform.test_cost` on a test order). A zero amount posts nothing; an order with a discount is refused until codes book it (Phase 2). The order's clearing account then sums to zero, or to the shortfall, which waits for operations (AP §8.6). Proved against AP §8.4's example by `delivery.test.ts` (without its platform code: codes come in Phase 2).
- **Operations' delivery (AP §9.3.2):** `POST /v1/admin/orders/{orderId}/record-delivery` («تسجيل التسليم نيابة عن الكابتن», `operations.manage`, the door payment and a reason, `Idempotency-Key`) delivers a `picked_up` or `arrived` order with the same checks and journals, without a location, audited as `order.record_delivery`. Operations' detail gives `delivery` (the lines, the change, the net, the shortfall, who recorded it); the customer's order gives `delivery` with the lines received and given back (CU §11.8, Q23), never the pay or the commission. `GET /v1/admin/orders/shortfalls?cityId=` (`operations.read`) lists «نقص في التحصيل», every collection of the city short beyond rounding, newest first (all open until resolution is built).
- **The restaurant's board after the delivery (RA §8.10 item 8; owner decision 2026-11-06):** `restaurantActiveOrders` also lists a delivered order until `order.pickup_dispute_window_min` after its pickup, so «المبلغ غير صحيح» keeps its place.
- **The pickup request (`captain-orders.ts`, CA §9.4, RA §8.10 items 1-3):** `POST /v1/captain/orders/{orderId}/pickup-request` (`lines` and the fix) needs the order `accepted` or `ready`, the track `at_merchant` and this captain (one open request at a time: a partial unique index, and the track is then `pickup_pending`). `checkLines` (`pickup-booking.ts`): one line per currency, each above zero (`400 invalid_lines`); the base currency only in Phase 1 (owner decision 2026-11-05, `409 currency_not_accepted`; RA §12.7's other currencies wait); the lines' total by the order's snapshot within half the largest rounding unit among their currencies of the pickup payment, the items value (`409 amount_mismatch`, «المبلغ لا يطابق المطلوب»). It writes a `pickup_requests` row (the pickup payment set by the server, the lines, `confirm_by`), the track `pickup_pending` (event `order.pickup_requested`) and the automatic confirmation job (`QUEUES.pickupAutoConfirm`) for `order.pickup_confirm_timeout_sec` later, in one transaction. The item check (C7) is the app's. The captain's read adds `pickup`: the proposed lines (CA §10.5: the whole amount in the base currency), the latest request with its outcome and the lines the restaurant received, the pickup time and the dispute.
- **The atomic pickup (`PickupBooking.complete`, AP §8.1):** under the order's lock and then its open request's: the order `picked_up` (track `picked_up`, `picked_up_at`; `ready_at` set then with `ready_by_pickup` true when ready was not pressed, «لم يُضغط جاهز», S8; a `CHECK` keeps a picked-up order's `picked_up_at` and `ready_at`), the request `confirmed` with its outcome (`restaurant`, `automatic`, `operations`) and the lines booked, `custody.pay_merchant` per line (the captain's custody down, `platform.order_clearing` up), a `platform.fx_rounding` journal for the gap between the lines and the items value so the clearing account holds the items value (RA §12.7), and `order.picked_up` with the outcome as its reason (AP §8.1: the customer sees the status only, so the pickup's events publish no reason, `publicReason: null`; the restaurant and the captain read the outcome from their order). The ledger takes no captain lock: the amount comes from the request, not a balance. Three ways in: the restaurant's `POST /v1/merchant/orders/{orderId}/pickup/confirm` (actor the user and the device; the first answer wins, a later one is `409 order_state_changed`); the worker's `PickupTimersWorker` (polling every second) runs `PickupBooking.autoConfirm`, which acts only on a request still open and due by its `confirm_by` on the worker's clock or the database's (pg-boss starts jobs by the latter); one early by both is sent again for its moment; operations' `POST /v1/admin/orders/{orderId}/confirm-pickup` (`operations.manage`, the agreed lines checked as the captain's, a reason, audited as `order.confirm_pickup`), which confirms the open request or writes a confirmed one, needing the captain at the restaurant (track `at_merchant` or `pickup_pending`); the reason stays in the request and the audit.
- **The restaurant's refusals (`pickup-commands.ts`, RA §8.10 items 6-7):** `POST .../pickup/incomplete` («الطلب غير مكتمل») and `POST .../pickup/dispute` (`receivedLines`, «المبلغ غير صحيح») on an open request reject it (`outcome` `incomplete` or `amount_incorrect` with the lines received), the track back to `at_merchant` (event `order.pickup_rejected` with the outcome); the captain pays the difference or rechecks, then sends a new request. Operations' alert `pickup_disputed` («خلاف على مبلغ الاستلام») shows once any request was rejected for its amount while the order is still accepted or ready, so it resolves itself with the pickup or the order's end (RA §8.10 item 6.4), and the card has the badge `pickupPending` while a request waits.
- **The dispute after the pickup (RA §8.10 item 8, owner decision 2026-11-06):** `POST .../pickup/dispute` within `order.pickup_dispute_window_min` of `picked_up_at` and without an open request records one `pickup_disputes` row per order (the confirmed request, the user, the device, the lines received; `409 pickup_already_disputed`) and `order.pickup_disputed`, which the relay publishes to the captain's channel too; past the window it is `409 order_state_changed`. The order continues normally; the ticket and its resolution (AP §9.9.3: `custody.pickup_correction` with `merchant.credit` or `merchant.debit`, or a rejection) come with the support module. The board's `pickup` gives the latest request (lines, base equivalent, `confirmBy` for the countdown, the outcome, and the user and device that answered for «أكّد {اسم المستخدم} الاستلام»), `pickedUpAt`, `readyNotPressed`, `disputeUntil` and `disputedAt`; operations' detail gives the dispute with the user, the device and the lines received. `currency_not_accepted` and `amount_mismatch` answer `409` on every route, `invalid_lines` `400`. The reads take the requests by the order's id and `placed_at`, so they stay in the order's partition.
- **Operations' reads (`operations-views.ts`, `admin-orders.controller.ts`, AP §9.2, §9.3.1):** `GET /v1/admin/orders?cityId=` (the board: every order of the city from `placed` to `arrived`, oldest first) and `GET /v1/admin/orders/{orderId}` (the detail, in any state), with `operations.read`. A card has the restaurant, «الحي», the time in the status (`statusSince`) and its column's limit (`limitAt`: the acceptance alert, the prep time, the no-captain-after-ready moment; null for the columns whose limit is not built), the captain track and captain, the total, the badges built («بحاجة لتعيين», «ملاحظة», «زبون جديد», «بانتظار تأكيد الاستلام») and the alerts' moments and the order's alerts open now by their conditions (`orderAlerts`; «تسليم بعيد عن العنوان» only while the order is active). An assigned order shows no no-captain alert. The detail adds the promised time, the acceptance, the customer's name and address (the masked phone comes with its slice), the lines, the money with the pickup payment, the pickup (its time, «لم يُضغط جاهز», every request with its lines and answer, whose last lines prefill «تأكيد الاستلام نيابة عن المطعم»), and the timeline from `order_events`, each offer and its answer included.
- **The order's alerts (`order-alerts.ts`, AP §7.3, §9.1.3):** `orderAlerts` is the pure rule of every order alert (`ORDER_ALERT_CODES`: «مطعم لم يقبل», «طلب بلا كابتن», «جاهز بلا كابتن», «انقطع اتصال كابتن أثناء طلب», «خلاف على مبلغ الاستلام», «تحضير متأخر», «كابتن ينتظر عند المطعم», «توصيل متأخر» at `promised_at` + `order.delivery_late_alert_min`, «نقص في التحصيل», «تسليم بعيد عن العنوان»), which the board and the records share; it answers the open ones and the next moment one may open. `recordTransition` sends a `QUEUES.orderAlerts` job in every transition's transaction; the worker's `OrderAlerts.sync` reads the settings and the captain's newest point first, then locks the order, syncs its alerts through `AlertsService.sync` and sends the job of the next moment (its id derived from the order and the moment, `timerId`, so many syncs leave one timer), all in one transaction. The job carries no version: the sync recomputes from the order as it is, so a late or repeated one changes nothing. «انقطع اتصال كابتن أثناء طلب» (`captain.gps_stale_alert_min` without a point, from the later of the newest point and the assignment, while a captain is on an active order) moves with every point, so it has no timer: `OrderAlertsWorker` syncs every order with a captain every `GPS_SWEEP_MS` (30 s) without timers, and every live order once at start with them (a deploy, a sync whose retries ran out). «نقص في التحصيل» stays open until operations resolve the shortfall (not built).
- **The order's channel:** `OrderChannelAccess` registers the `order` namespace's check with `RealtimeAccess`: a customer subscribes only to their own order.
- **Timers (AP §8.3, `order-timers.ts`):** the automatic cancellation is a pg-boss job (`QUEUES.merchantAutoCancel`) sent in the placing transaction for `order.merchant_auto_cancel_min` after placing, with the order, its `placed_at` and version 1. The queue retries a failed run with a growing delay for about an hour, and each failure is logged and reported. The worker (`OrderTimersWorker` in `OrderingWorkerModule`) runs `OrderTimers.merchantAutoCancel`: a `placed` order still at that version becomes `cancelled` (version 2, `cancel_reason` `merchant_auto_cancel`, «المطعم لم يستجب», `food_prepared` false, actor `system`), which also counts it as unanswered by the restaurant (RA §11; `unansweredInBusinessDay` in `unanswered.ts`, which the push of RA §16 #2 shares) and, at `merchant.auto_pause_unanswered_count` in the business day, auto-pauses the restaurant in the same transaction (`RestaurantStatusService.autoPauseIfDue`, which locks the restaurant before it counts; RA §8.3, AP §9.6.5): the code is the system's alone, so an operations cancellation with the same text never counts; anything else is left alone, so a late or repeated job is harmless. A cancelled order keeps when, why and whether food was prepared (a `CHECK`). The alert at `order.merchant_accept_alert_min` is raised by `order-alerts.ts`; the apology push leaves through `OutboxPushes`.
- **Order numbers (AP §4.8):** `order_numbers` holds each city's last number, from 10001 (owner decision 2026-11-06), taken last in the order's transaction, so a refused order leaves no gap. The partitions cannot keep `(city_id, number)` unique; this row does.
- **Idempotency (architecture §5.3):** the command needs `Idempotency-Key` (`@Idempotent()` and `@IdempotencyKey()` from `src/http/idempotency.ts`, `400 idempotency_key_required`). `idempotency_keys` keeps the key per account with a hash of the command and the order, written in the order's transaction, so a refused command keeps no key. A repeat within 24 hours answers that order (its money, lines and address never change; its status may have moved on); with another command `422 idempotency_key_reused`; an older key places a new order. Two repeats sent together wait on the customer's lock, and the second answers the first's order, even when the first's order fills the restaurant.
- **The restaurant's active orders:** `OrdersService` registers its count with `MerchantsService.countActiveOrders`, so the restaurant's state is `busy` at its maximum everywhere (the restaurant app, the browsing, the quote) without `merchants` reading `ordering`.
- **The estimate (docs/domain/03, CU §8.4):** `estimateRange` and `tripMinutes` from `geo`, which the customers' browsing uses too (in `ordering` they made an import cycle once `ordering` imported `customers`). The trip is the motorbike's distance, as the fee's is; a distance-band fee's distance is reused. The order keeps the trip of the estimate shown (`trip_seconds`, rounded up, and `trip_metres`), which the promised time and the search start use; an order placed before slice 1.10 has none and counts it as 0.
