# ordering module

The quote, the order, its state machine, edits and outcomes (architecture §4.2). So far the versioned quote of a cart (CU §10.1–10.6; AP §8.4, §8.6), placing the order (CU §10.7; AP §8.1, §4.8; RA §7.4; D23) and relaying its events to the apps (architecture §5.5, §6.2).

## Rules

- **The quote is computed, never stored:** `POST /v1/customer/quote` (open to guests, who see the full quote, CU §5.1) takes the restaurant, the delivery point, the cart lines, «سأدفع بـ» and the app version, and answers the priced lines, the money, the equivalents, the estimate, the minimum-order and free-delivery status, the rate versions, every blocking problem and the version. The app asks again whenever the cart, the address or the code changes, and on opening checkout. A restaurant customers do not see, or one suspended while the quote is computed, answers `404 unknown_restaurant`. «سأدفع بـ» is not binding (CU §10.5): a currency the city no longer accepts gives `payIn: null`, never a refusal.
- **Lines (`priceLines` in `quote.ts`):** each line at the menu's current prices through `MerchantsService.menu(id, now)`. Hidden rows are `item_not_found`; an unavailable item `item_unavailable`; an option not on the item `option_not_found`; a repeated choice or a group's count outside its minimum and maximum `options_invalid`; an unavailable choice `option_unavailable`; a price outside the base currency `price_not_in_base_currency` (not checked while the city has no base: the quote shows `rates_unavailable`). Such a line has no price and stays out of the money. Every line carries the `knownUnitPrice` the app last showed; a line whose unit price differs is priced and flagged `price_changed` (CU §10.1), so the customer sees «تغيّر سعر…» before confirming. Quantities are 1 to 20 and item notes up to 100 characters without a run of 7 or more digits (Q25), checked by the route's schema.
- **Money (`quoteMoney`, AP §8.4):** items value + delivery fee − discount, never below 0, rounded to the base currency's unit and mode from the rate snapshot (`rounding`, 0 without a snapshot). The discount is 0 until codes arrive (Phase 2, owner decision 2026-11-06). The fee is `GeoService.deliveryFee` for the items value; the equivalents and «ستدفع حوالي» are `equivalent` from fx, each currency by its own rounding.
- **Thresholds (CU §10.2):** the minimum order and the free-delivery threshold compare the items value before discounts; below the minimum is `below_minimum_order` with what is missing. The threshold is the one of the fee rule the fee was priced with (`DeliveryFee.freeDeliveryFrom`), so the two never disagree. A minimum order in another currency than the base (allowed until the first order locks it) is `minimum_not_in_base_currency`.
- **Checks (CU §10.6; owner decision 2026-11-06):** built: the app version (`app.customer.min_version` compared part by part, `versionBelow`, `update_required`) and maintenance, the platform hours (`platform.hours` on the city clock, `platform_closed` with the next opening), the zone (`outside_zones`) and the restaurant's coverage (`not_covered`), the restaurant's state (`restaurant_closed`, with the state in the answer, `busy` at its maximum active orders), the lines, the minimum order, a fee rule in the base currency (`delivery_unpriced`) and a rate snapshot (`rates_unavailable`). Every problem blocks ordering. The route is `@AppSignedInOrGuest()`: a guest gets the quote without a token; a signed-in customer's quote also checks their active orders (D23, CU §5.5, `ActiveOrders` in `active-orders.ts`: `placed` to `arrived`, against `customer.new_max_active_orders`, or `customer.trusted_max_active_orders` after `customer.trusted_after_delivered_orders` delivered), `one_order_at_a_time` for a new account and `active_order_limit` for a trusted one, the two texts of CU §5.5. The capacity check (AP §9.12.6) runs while `dispatch.capacity_check` is on: `CaptainsService.eligibleNear` for the restaurant and the trip's distance, `no_captain_nearby` («الضغط عالٍ الآن، جرّب بعد دقائق») without an eligible captain. It costs a few reads per nearby captain, so it runs only when no other problem blocks the order. Tests that are not about it turn it off. Passing until their slices: account states, the terms version, the emergency stop and codes.
- **The version (`quoteVersion`):** a hash of the lines' prices, the money, the equivalents, the rate versions and the blocking problems, so a new estimate alone never changes it (CU §10.6). The order command carries it and compares it. A new fee rule that charges the same keeps the version; the order keeps the rule it was priced with.
- **Placing the order (`orders.service.ts`, CU §10.7):** `POST /v1/customer/orders` (`@AppSignedIn()`) takes the cart as the quote does, the quote version, the address (`addressId` of a saved one, or `unsavedAddress` with the fields of CU §7.4: exactly one), the note (up to 200 characters, no run of 7 or more digits, Q25) and «سأدفع بـ», and answers `201` with the order as placed. Steps:
  1. A repeat of the key (below) answers its order before anything else, and again before any refusal is answered: a repeat sent with the first can be refused by what the first changed.
  2. `CustomersService.orderAddress` copies the address with «الحي» from the zone at the pin now (Q6): `404 unknown_address`, `409 outside_zones`.
  3. `QuoteService.price` re-runs the quote for the customer at the pin through the pool, before the transaction, so a placing never holds a connection while it waits for another. Any problem: `409 order_blocked` with the quote; another version: `409 quote_changed` with the quote (Q10).
  4. One transaction: the customer's advisory lock, then the restaurant's (always in that order); the customer's active orders (D23); the restaurant's (`placed`, `accepted`, `ready`) against its maximum (RA §7.4); `FxService.snapshotForOrder`, which locks the base and reads through the transaction, compared with the quote's rates and rounding; the city's number; the order, its lines, its first event with its `order.placed` outbox row, the automatic cancellation job and the key. A check failed here (a limit reached, an `FxRefusedError`, other rates) rolls back and re-runs the quote, which shows it: `409 order_blocked` with its problem (the restaurant `busy`, the customer at the limit), or else `409 quote_changed`.
- **What is atomic (owner decision 2026-11-06, CU §10.7 item 6):** the two limits, the rate snapshot and the number are checked under the locks; the quote's other checks (menu, prices, restaurant state, zone) run just before the transaction, as their reads would take no lock inside it under READ COMMITTED. A pause or suspension committed in that window (the distance call takes up to 1 s) still lets the order in; the restaurant's acceptance and the automatic cancellation are the last safeguard. Never call the distance or another slow service inside the order's transaction.
- **The order (`schema.ts`):** `orders`, `order_lines` and `order_events`, partitioned by the UTC month of placing (this month's and the next, created by `ordering.create_month_partitions()`, which the worker calls at start and every day through `OrderingWorkerModule`); the lines and events carry the order's `placed_at`. The order keeps the money as the quote priced it (a `CHECK` that the total adds up), the rate snapshot (its bigints as strings), the fee rule, the commission and the city's pay table version in force, the address snapshot (another contact number stays encrypted with identity's key) and the estimate shown. `version` starts at 1 and every transition increments it with an `order_events` row (append-only, `UPDATE` revoked on every partition).
- **Transitions (architecture §5.5, `order-events.ts`):** each one locks the order (`lockOrder`, by its id and `placed_at`), checks its state and version, writes the new state and version, then `recordTransition` writes the `order_events` row, the `outbox` row and a relay job (`QUEUES.outboxRelay`) in the same transaction.
- **The outbox (E4):** `ordering.outbox`, one row per transition (`order.placed`, `order.cancelled`) with the order version and a payload of version 1 (`schema: 1`, the order, its number, restaurant, customer, status, version, reason, time). The application role inserts rows and may change only `published_at`.
- **The relay (`outbox-relay.ts`, architecture §5.5):** `OutboxRelay.drain` takes the rows with no `published_at` in id order, 50 per transaction, `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, publishes each with `RealtimePublisher.broadcast` to `merchant:{restaurant_id}` and `order:{order_id}` (the payload as written, idempotency key `outbox:<id>`), and marks them in that transaction: at least once, never twice at once. Ids are taken at insert, not at commit, so it keeps no «last published id»; clients order events by the order version and ignore an older one (RA §4.3). Centrifugo out of reach stops the run; a row it refuses is logged and skipped for that run, so it never holds back the others; either way the run throws, so the queue tries again with its backoff and reports it. In the worker (`OutboxRelayWorker`) every transition's job wakes a run (polling every 0.5 s, 50 jobs per call), and one run at start takes the rows written while no worker ran. While real time is off (no `CENTRIFUGO_*`), the worker logs it and the rows wait. Proved by `outbox-relay.test.ts` and, through Centrifugo to a client, `order-realtime.test.ts`.
- **The order's channel:** `OrderChannelAccess` registers the `order` namespace's check with `RealtimeAccess`: a customer subscribes only to their own order.
- **Timers (AP §8.3, `order-timers.ts`):** the automatic cancellation is a pg-boss job (`QUEUES.merchantAutoCancel`) sent in the placing transaction for `order.merchant_auto_cancel_min` after placing, with the order, its `placed_at` and version 1. The queue retries a failed run with a growing delay for about an hour, and each failure is logged and reported. The worker (`OrderTimersWorker` in `OrderingWorkerModule`) runs `OrderTimers.merchantAutoCancel`: a `placed` order still at that version becomes `cancelled` (version 2, `cancel_reason` `merchant_auto_cancel`, «المطعم لم يستجب», `food_prepared` false, actor `system`), which also counts it as unanswered by the restaurant (RA §11): the code is the system's alone, so an operations cancellation with the same text never counts; anything else is left alone, so a late or repeated job is harmless. A cancelled order keeps when, why and whether food was prepared (a `CHECK`). The alert at `order.merchant_accept_alert_min` waits for the operations board (slice 1.11); the apology push will leave through the relay with push.
- **Order numbers (AP §4.8):** `order_numbers` holds each city's last number, from 10001 (owner decision 2026-11-06), taken last in the order's transaction, so a refused order leaves no gap. The partitions cannot keep `(city_id, number)` unique; this row does.
- **Idempotency (architecture §5.3):** the command needs `Idempotency-Key` (`@Idempotent()` and `@IdempotencyKey()` from `src/http/idempotency.ts`, `400 idempotency_key_required`). `idempotency_keys` keeps the key per account with a hash of the command and the order, written in the order's transaction, so a refused command keeps no key. A repeat within 24 hours answers that order (its money, lines and address never change; its status may have moved on); with another command `422 idempotency_key_reused`; an older key places a new order. Two repeats sent together wait on the customer's lock, and the second answers the first's order, even when the first's order fills the restaurant.
- **The restaurant's active orders:** `OrdersService` registers its count with `MerchantsService.countActiveOrders`, so the restaurant's state is `busy` at its maximum everywhere (the restaurant app, the browsing, the quote) without `merchants` reading `ordering`.
- **The estimate (docs/domain/03, CU §8.4):** `estimateRange` and `tripMinutes` from `geo`, which the customers' browsing uses too (in `ordering` they made an import cycle once `ordering` imported `customers`). The trip is the motorbike's distance, as the fee's is; a distance-band fee's distance is reused. The promised time at acceptance joins with slice 1.10.
