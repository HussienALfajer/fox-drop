# ordering module

The quote, the order, its state machine, edits and outcomes (architecture §4.2). So far the versioned quote of a cart (CU §10.1–10.6; AP §8.4, §8.6) and the delivery estimate (docs/domain/03).

## Rules

- **The quote is computed, never stored:** `POST /v1/customer/quote` (public, so a guest sees the full quote, CU §5.1) takes the restaurant, the delivery point, the cart lines, «سأدفع بـ» and the app version, and answers the priced lines, the money, the equivalents, the estimate, the minimum-order and free-delivery status, the rate versions, every blocking problem and the version. The app asks again whenever the cart, the address or the code changes, and on opening checkout. A restaurant customers do not see, or one suspended while the quote is computed, answers `404 unknown_restaurant`. «سأدفع بـ» is not binding (CU §10.5): a currency the city no longer accepts gives `payIn: null`, never a refusal.
- **Lines (`priceLines` in `quote.ts`):** each line at the menu's current prices through `MerchantsService.menu(id, now)`. Hidden rows are `item_not_found`; an unavailable item `item_unavailable`; an option not on the item `option_not_found`; a repeated choice or a group's count outside its minimum and maximum `options_invalid`; an unavailable choice `option_unavailable`; a price outside the base currency `price_not_in_base_currency` (not checked while the city has no base: the quote shows `rates_unavailable`). Such a line has no price and stays out of the money. Every line carries the `knownUnitPrice` the app last showed; a line whose unit price differs is priced and flagged `price_changed` (CU §10.1), so the customer sees «تغيّر سعر…» before confirming. Quantities are 1 to 20 and item notes up to 100 characters without a run of 7 or more digits (Q25), checked by the route's schema.
- **Money (`quoteMoney`, AP §8.4):** items value + delivery fee − discount, never below 0, rounded to the base currency's unit and mode from the rate snapshot (`rounding`, 0 without a snapshot). The discount is 0 until codes arrive (Phase 2, owner decision 2026-11-06). The fee is `GeoService.deliveryFee` for the items value; the equivalents and «ستدفع حوالي» are `equivalent` from fx, each currency by its own rounding.
- **Thresholds (CU §10.2):** the minimum order and the free-delivery threshold compare the items value before discounts; below the minimum is `below_minimum_order` with what is missing. The threshold is the one of the fee rule the fee was priced with (`DeliveryFee.freeDeliveryFrom`), so the two never disagree. A minimum order in another currency than the base (allowed until the first order locks it) is `minimum_not_in_base_currency`.
- **Checks (CU §10.6; owner decision 2026-11-06):** built: the app version (`app.customer.min_version` compared part by part, `versionBelow`, `update_required`) and maintenance, the platform hours (`platform.hours` on the city clock, `platform_closed` with the next opening), the zone (`outside_zones`) and the restaurant's coverage (`not_covered`), the restaurant's state (`restaurant_closed`, with the state in the answer), the lines, the minimum order, a fee rule in the base currency (`delivery_unpriced`) and a rate snapshot (`rates_unavailable`). Every problem blocks ordering. Passing until their slices: the capacity check (dispatch), the active-order limit (slice 1.8), account states, the terms version, the emergency stop and codes.
- **The version (`quoteVersion`):** a hash of the lines' prices, the money, the equivalents, the rate versions and the blocking problems, so a new estimate alone never changes it (CU §10.6). The order command carries it and placing the order compares it (slice 1.8).
- **The estimate (`delivery-estimate.ts`, docs/domain/03, CU §8.4):** `estimateRange` and `tripMinutes`, used by the quote and by the customers' browsing: default prep + busy-mode minutes + the trip at `order.eta_speed_kmh` + `order.eta_buffer_min`, from a multiple of 5 to 10 minutes later. The trip is the motorbike's distance, as the fee's is; a distance-band fee's distance is reused. The promised time at acceptance joins here (slice 1.10).
