# merchants module

Restaurants, their account status, hours, operating state, coverage, minimum order, commission and menu (architecture §4.2; AP §9.6, RA §7, §9, §10).

## Rules

- **The record (AP §9.6.3):** `createRestaurant` adds a visible `draft` with its pin, coverage, minimum order, first commission version and default prep time (within `merchant.prep_time_floor_min` and `merchant.prep_time_ceiling_min`, RA §10), audited as `restaurant.create`. Every change takes the restaurant's row lock (`FOR UPDATE`) and is audited in its transaction (`restaurant.status`, `restaurant.hours`, `restaurant.commission`, `menu.*.create`).
- **Account status (AP §9.6.1):** `draft`, `test`, `active`, `suspended`, `terminated`, changed by `setAccountStatus` along `statusChanges` in `merchants.service.ts` (draft → test or active; test → active or terminated; active ↔ suspended; active or suspended → terminated; terminated is final). Suspending, reactivating and terminating need a reason (AP §9.6.5, `reason_required`); a suspension keeps it for the restaurant app (RA §5.5). The activation checklist and the zero balance before termination join with the onboarding wizard and the finance slices.
- **Operating state (RA §7.1):** `operatingState(input, now)` in `operating-state.ts` is the pure rule: the account status first (draft, suspended, terminated), then holiday close, closed by schedule, paused (any source), busy at the active-orders limit, open; busy mode is a flag on open. Only `open` takes orders (`takesOrders`). `MerchantsService.operatingState(id, now)` reads its parts; active orders are 0 until the ordering module counts them (slice 1.8).
- **Hours (RA §10):** `weekly_schedules` are versions, never changed; the newest is in force, and none is closed every day. A period is a weekday (0 Sunday to 6 Saturday) and two times in minutes after midnight on the city's clock (`GeoService.cityTimeZone`). One whose closing time is not after its opening time crosses midnight and belongs to the day it starts; 00:00 to 00:00 is the whole day. Periods last 30 minutes or more and never overlap, across midnight and the end of the week included (`checkWeeklySchedule` in `hours.ts`). A restaurant opens at the opening minute and closes at the closing minute.
- **Holiday closes and pauses** are rows with an end and `ended_at` when ended early. An operations or auto pause may have no end (until resumed); a restaurant pause always has one, at the latest the end of its opening period (S3, a `CHECK`). Only the state reads them so far; setting them, the one-pause rule and the auto-pause join with the status slices.
- **Coverage (owner decision 2026-11-06):** a radius in whole metres around the pin, or one or more of the city's zones (`coverage_zones`). `covers(id, point)` needs the point inside an active service zone (`GeoService.zoneAt`) and then inside the radius (a straight line on the sphere, `ST_DWithin` on geography) or inside one of the restaurant's zones.
- **Commission (AP §9.6.5, D16):** `commission_rates` are versions in basis points of the items value (1500 is 15%), each in effect from its start for new orders, never changed. A change needs a reason and starts now or later, never in the past (D16). `commissionAt` takes the latest start reached; of two with the same start, the later version. A launch commission is two versions: the launch rate now and the standard rate from its end date.
- **Money:** the minimum order, item prices and option deltas are `bigint` minor units in the city's base currency, checked under `lockCityCurrencies` (until the first order locks the base, another of the city's currencies is allowed, as for the delivery fee). The module registers them with `FxService.useCityAmounts` (terminated restaurants left out), so fx refuses a base change while one is in another currency. Each amount keeps its own currency, and `menu` returns item prices and option deltas as `Money`. An item price is above 0, a delta 0 or more (RA §9.4). The audit log keeps amounts as strings.
- **Menu (AP §9.6.6, RA §9.1):** categories, items (a code unique in the restaurant, `I0001` onward when left empty), option groups with options, and the links of groups to items. A group with a minimum of 1 or more is required; 0 makes it an add-on group. Rows created by an admin are published at once; a restaurant's new rows stay unpublished until their change request is approved (RA §9.2). Rows are never deleted: `deleted_at` and `removed_at` hide them. `menu(id)` reads the published menu in order; an item is unavailable when it, its category, or every option of one of its required groups is (RA §9.3).
- **Logs:** `price_changes` («سجل الأسعار») is never changed. `change_requests` holds one pending request per field of a target (a partial unique index); a newer edit replaces its pending value.
