# merchants module

Restaurants, their account status, hours, operating state, coverage, minimum order, commission and menu (architecture §4.2; AP §9.6, RA §7, §9, §10).

## Rules

- **The record (AP §9.6.3):** `createRestaurant` adds a visible `draft` with its pin, coverage, minimum order, first commission version and default prep time (within `merchant.prep_time_floor_min` and `merchant.prep_time_ceiling_min`, RA §10), audited as `restaurant.create`. Every change takes the restaurant's row lock (`FOR UPDATE`) and is audited in its transaction (`restaurant.status`, `restaurant.hours`, `restaurant.commission`, `menu.*.create`).
- **Account status (AP §9.6.1):** `draft`, `test`, `active`, `suspended`, `terminated`, changed by `setAccountStatus` along `statusChanges` in `merchants.service.ts` (draft → test or active; test → active or terminated; active ↔ suspended; active or suspended → terminated; terminated is final). Suspending, reactivating and terminating need a reason (AP §9.6.5, `reason_required`); a suspension keeps it for the restaurant app (RA §5.5). The activation checklist and the zero balance before termination join with the onboarding wizard and the finance slices.
- **Operating state (RA §7.1):** `operatingState(input, now)` in `operating-state.ts` is the pure rule: the account status first (draft, suspended, terminated), then holiday close, closed by schedule, paused (any source), busy at the active-orders limit, open; busy mode is a flag on open. Only `open` takes orders (`takesOrders`). `MerchantsService.operatingState(id, now)` reads its parts, and `storefrontState(id, now)` adds what customers see (CU §9.2): a holiday close's or an operations pause's message and, while closed by the schedule, the next opening (`nextOpening` in `hours.ts`); active orders come from the count the ordering module registers with `countActiveOrders` (0 until it does), since this module cannot read `ordering`.
- **Hours (RA §10):** `weekly_schedules` are versions, never changed; the newest is in force, and none is closed every day. A period is a weekday (0 Sunday to 6 Saturday) and two times in minutes after midnight on the city's clock (`GeoService.cityTimeZone`). One whose closing time is not after its opening time crosses midnight and belongs to the day it starts; 00:00 to 00:00 is the whole day. Periods last 30 minutes or more and never overlap, across midnight and the end of the week included (`checkWeeklySchedule` in `hours.ts`). A restaurant opens at the opening minute and closes at the closing minute.
- **Holiday closes and pauses** are rows with an end and `ended_at` when ended early. An operations or auto pause may have no end (until resumed); a restaurant pause always has one, at the latest the end of its opening period (S3, a `CHECK`).
- **Status commands (`restaurant-status.service.ts`, RA §7.1–7.4, §10, §18; slice 2.6a):** `RestaurantStatusService` takes the restaurant's lock, reads the state inside its transaction (`storefrontState(id, now, tx)`, which also answers `closesAt`, the end of the opening period it is in), checks the pure rules of `status-rules.ts`, writes the change, audits it with the user, role and device (`restaurant.pause`, `.resume`, `.busy_mode`, `.holiday_close`, `.holiday_close.end`, `.prep_time`, `.max_active_orders`, `.pickup_currencies`, `.auto_pause`) and records a state change (below). The restaurant pauses for 15, 30 or 60 minutes or «حتى أعيد الفتح» only while open or busy and never over an operations or auto pause (one pause at a time: its own pause is replaced; `409 paused_by_operations`, `409 restaurant_closed`); its pause and busy mode end with the opening period (S3); it resumes only its own pause. Busy mode takes one of `merchant.busy_extra_options_min`, sets `busy_until` to the period's end and `busy_started_at`. A holiday close starts now or later and ends after it starts, with a message up to 100 characters, one at a time (a new one ends the one in force or set), not while suspended. The default prep time stays within the chooser's floor and ceiling; the maximum active orders is off or 1 to 50. **The auto-pause** (`autoPauseIfDue(tx, id, unanswered, now)`) runs in every automatic cancellation's transaction: it takes the restaurant's lock, then reads the business day's count through the transaction (so two cancellations at once never both miss it) and pauses at `merchant.auto_pause_unanswered_count`; it ends a restaurant pause and is not added over an operations or auto pause, nor for a terminated restaurant (the timers and the auto-pause leave a terminated restaurant alone, `lockAny`, rather than fail its cancellation).
- **A rejection's side effects (RA §8.6, S2; slice 2.7a):** the ordering module calls them inside the rejection's transaction, after the order's lock: `switchOffForRejection(tx, …)` switches the items off as `setAvailability` does (`switchedOffUntil` gives the end before the transaction), and `RestaurantStatusService.afterRejection(tx, …)` sets the restaurant's own pause (`pauseIn`) or busy mode (`busyModeIn`) only while the restaurant is open or busy and the pure rules allow it, answering false otherwise without a refusal, so the rejection stands; it takes the lock that leaves a terminated restaurant alone (`lockAny`), so operations can still reject its orders. `pause` and `setBusyMode` run the same parts in their own transaction. The item-unavailable report after acceptance (RA §8.12, slice 2.9) switches its items off with the same `switchOffForRejection`, and its withdrawal switches them on again with `switchOnItems(tx, …)`, both in the order's transaction.
- **Pickup currencies (RA §12.7, AP §9.6.4; slice 2.14a):** `restaurants.pickup_currencies` (text[], empty by default) holds the other currencies the restaurant accepts from the captain; the base is always accepted and never listed. `RestaurantStatusService.pickupCurrencies(id)` answers the base, the ones switched on that the city still has enabled and the choices (every other enabled currency, in display order), each with its Arabic name from fx; a city without a base answers no base and no choices; `setPickupCurrencies(id, currencies, change)` takes enabled non-base currencies once each (`400 invalid_pickup_currencies`), stores them in display order and audits `restaurant.pickup_currencies`; it applies to the pickups requested after it. `restaurant(id)` gives `pickupCurrencies` as stored. The panel's status read carries `pickupBaseCurrency`, `pickupCurrencies` and `pickupCurrencyChoices`. The owner's own route and screen come with 2.14b.
- **Operations' commands (AP §9.6.5, RA §16 #23; slice 2.6b):** `operationsPause` (15, 30 or 60 minutes, until a later time, or until resumed; a required reason, an optional message to customers up to 100 characters, `checkOperationsPause` in `status-rules.ts`) ends any pause in force, the auto-pause included, and works in any state, holding across opening periods as the auto-pause does; a timed one ends with `QUEUES.pauseEnd`. `operationsResume` ends any pause in force (none: nothing). `operationsEndBusyMode` is `setBusyMode(null)` by an admin. Each records `paused_by_operations` (with the reason), `resumed_by_operations` or `busy_ended_by_operations`, with `endedAuto: true` when an auto-pause ended, so the relay resolves «مطعم أوقف تلقائياً» at once. An admin's holiday close needs a reason (`reason_required`), stored on the row; `setOrderSettings` without `defaultPrepMin` changes only the maximum. Ended pauses keep who ended them (`ended_by_type`, `ended_by_id`). The panel's routes (`admin-restaurant-status.controller.ts`): `GET /v1/admin/restaurants/{id}/status` (`restaurants.read`; the status with the name, the account status and `heldPauseSource` and `heldPauseUntil`, the pause in force whatever the state, since an operations or auto pause holds while closed by the schedule or a holiday, with an operations pause's message and reason), `POST .../status/pause`, `POST .../status/resume`, `DELETE .../status/busy-mode`, `PUT` and `DELETE .../holiday-close`, `PUT .../max-active-orders` and `PUT .../pickup-currencies` (`restaurants.manage`, OWN and OPS). None takes an `Idempotency-Key`: a resume, an end of busy mode, a holiday close and the maximum set a state to a value, and the same operations pause again (the same message and reason, and the same end, or the same length within `REPEAT_WINDOW_MS`, 60 s) is a repeat that changes nothing, so a retry neither moves its end nor pushes twice; each answers the status after it. `isAutoPaused` lets the relay keep the alert of a newer auto-pause.
- **State changes (`state-events.ts`, RA §18, E4):** every status change writes a row of `merchants.state_outbox` (`recordStateChange`, with a `QUEUES.restaurantStateRelay` job) in its transaction; the application role may change only `relayed_at`. The relay lives in `notifications` (`RestaurantStateRelay`), which already imports this module: `relayStateChanges(relay)` lends it the rows in id order under `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` and marks those it returned from. The timers (`status-timers.ts`, `MerchantsWorkerModule` in the worker): `QUEUES.pauseEnd` at a pause's end records `pause_ended` (with `push` only when the restaurant takes orders again) once (`pauses.end_told_at`) if the pause was not ended early, due by the worker's or the database's clock (one early by both is sent again); `QUEUES.busyReminder` every `merchant.busy_mode_reminder_min` records `busy_reminder` while busy mode is still the one it was set for (`busy_started_at`) and its moment (`due`) is newer than `busy_reminded_at`, and sends the next with an id derived from its moment, so a repeated job neither reminds twice nor starts a second chain.
- **Coverage (owner decision 2026-11-06):** a radius in whole metres around the pin, or one or more of the city's zones (`coverage_zones`). `covers(id, point)` needs the point inside an active service zone (`GeoService.zoneAt`) and then inside the radius (a straight line on the sphere, `ST_DWithin` on geography) or inside one of the restaurant's zones.
- **The restaurants a customer sees (CU §8.2):** `coveringRestaurants(cityId, point)` returns the `active` and visible restaurants that cover the point, as `covers` does, in any operating state (closed ones are listed last by the customer's list).
- **Commission (AP §9.6.5, D16):** `commission_rates` are versions in basis points of the items value (1500 is 15%), each in effect from its start for new orders, never changed. A change needs a reason and starts now or later, never in the past (D16). `commissionAt` takes the latest start reached; of two with the same start, the later version. A launch commission is two versions: the launch rate now and the standard rate from its end date.
- **Money:** the minimum order, item prices and option deltas are `bigint` minor units in the city's base currency, checked under `lockCityCurrencies` (until the first order locks the base, another of the city's currencies is allowed, as for the delivery fee). The module registers them with `FxService.useCityAmounts` (terminated restaurants left out), so fx refuses a base change while one is in another currency. Each amount keeps its own currency, and `menu` returns item prices and option deltas as `Money`. An item price is above 0, a delta 0 or more (RA §9.4). The audit log keeps amounts as strings.
- **Menu (AP §9.6.6, RA §9.1):** categories, items (a code unique in the restaurant, `I0001` onward when left empty), option groups with options, and the links of groups to items. A group with a minimum of 1 or more is required; 0 makes it an add-on group. Rows created by an admin are published at once; a restaurant's new rows stay unpublished until their change request is approved (RA §9.2). Rows are never deleted: `deleted_at` and `removed_at` hide them. `menu(id)` reads the published menu in order; an item is unavailable when it, its category, or every option of one of its required groups is (RA §9.3). A category's daily window (`window_starts_at`, `window_ends_at`, minutes on the city clock, crossing midnight when the end is not after the start) makes it and its items unavailable outside it (`inDailyWindow` in `hours.ts`, CU §9.3).
- **Availability (RA §9.3):** `setAvailability` switches a category, an item or an option on, or off «حتى الغد» (`unavailable_until` = the next `finance.business_day_cutoff` on the city's clock, `nextCutoff` from `geo`) or «حتى أعيده» (no end). `menu(id, now)` reads a switch off «حتى الغد» as on from its time, so nothing has to switch it back; it returns each row's own switch (`switchedOff`) beside `available`, the one customers see (a category's switch leaves its items' own switches alone). Audited as `menu.<type>.availability` with the user, their role and the device id as the audit device.
- **Devices (RA §5.2 item 5, §5.3):** `devices`, one per restaurant and app installation, with its name, platform, app version and the restaurant user who named it last (an `identity` account id, a plain column). `nameDevice` lets a cashier name a device the first time only; renaming is managing devices, an owner's (RA §5.4, `device_rename_not_allowed`). The ring setting, the device check and last seen join with staff and devices (RA §14).
- **Status routes** (`merchant-status.controller.ts`): `GET /v1/merchant/restaurant/status` (the pill and sheet: state, pause source, ends, busy mode, active orders against the maximum, the holiday close, the choices), `POST .../status/pause`, `POST .../status/resume` and `PUT .../status/busy-mode` for owners and cashiers; `GET` and `PUT /v1/merchant/restaurant/hours`, `PUT /v1/merchant/restaurant/order-settings` and `PUT`/`DELETE /v1/merchant/restaurant/holiday-close` for owners (`403 owner_only`). None takes an `Idempotency-Key` (to confirm with the owner: RA §18 asks a key of every command; the app does not retry a status command, and reads the status again after any failure), and each answers the status or the hours after it. A terminated restaurant's owners get `403 restaurant_terminated` on every one (RA §5.5). `/v1/merchant/status` is the health route, hence `restaurant/status`.
- **Restaurant app routes** (`merchant-menu.controller.ts`): `GET /v1/merchant/restaurant` (status, suspension reason, operating state), `GET /v1/merchant/menu` and `PUT /v1/merchant/menu/availability`, for owners and cashiers (RA §5.4), scoped to `AppSession.merchant.restaurantId`; a row of another restaurant answers `404`. A terminated restaurant's owners keep only the money section (RA §5.5), so the menu routes answer `403 restaurant_terminated` (`setAvailability` checks it under the restaurant's lock). Any reader of the menu goes through `menu(id, now)`, never the raw `available` column, which still reads off after «حتى الغد» ends.
- **Images (AP §9.6.3, §9.6.6; slice 2.5):** `restaurants.logo_key`, `restaurants.cover_key` and `items.photo_key` are keys in `public-media` (architecture §5.8). `setRestaurantImage(id, 'logo' | 'cover', uploadId, change)` and `setItemPhoto(id, itemId, uploadId, change)` call `FilesService.prepare` before their transaction (it copies and checks the file, so no storage call runs under the restaurant's lock), then take the restaurant's lock and `FilesService.link` the upload in the same transaction, publish at once (an admin's edit), and audit `restaurant.logo`, `restaurant.cover` or `menu.item.photo` with the old and new keys. The same upload again is a repeat that changes nothing; an upload already linked to another record is refused (`upload_used`). The old object stays in storage. The panel's routes (`admin-restaurant-images.controller.ts`, the temporary «الصور» screen, owner decision 2026-11-09): `GET /v1/admin/restaurants?cityId=` and `GET /v1/admin/restaurants/{id}/images` (`restaurants.read` or `restaurants.menus`), `PUT /v1/admin/restaurants/{id}/logo` and `/cover` (`RESTAURANT_PROFILE`: `restaurants.manage` or `restaurants.create`, AP §9.6.3 step 1), `PUT /v1/admin/restaurants/{id}/items/{itemId}/photo` (`restaurants.menus`); the onboarding wizard (2.30) and the menu editor (2.31) take them over. The restaurant's own photos, with their review, come with 2.32.
- **Logs:** `price_changes` («سجل الأسعار») is never changed. `change_requests` holds one pending request per field of a target (a partial unique index); a newer edit replaces its pending value.
