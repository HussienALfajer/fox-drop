# merchants module

Restaurants, their account status, hours, operating state, coverage, minimum order, commission and menu (architecture §4.2; AP §9.6, RA §7, §9, §10).

## Rules

- **The record (AP §9.6.3):** `createRestaurant` adds a visible `draft` with its pin, coverage, minimum order, first commission version and default prep time (within `merchant.prep_time_floor_min` and `merchant.prep_time_ceiling_min`, RA §10), audited as `restaurant.create`. Every change takes the restaurant's row lock (`FOR UPDATE`) and is audited in its transaction (`restaurant.status`, `restaurant.hours`, `restaurant.commission`, `menu.*.create`).
- **The wizard's draft (AP §9.6.3 steps 1–5; slice 2.30a, `restaurant-drafts.service.ts`):** «إضافة مطعم» makes a `draft` saved at every step. A draft's record (the pin, the coverage, the minimum order with its currency and the prep time) stays empty until its step (migration 0116: `restaurants_record_complete` asks for it in every other status, `restaurants_operations_together` keeps the operations whole), and `restaurant(id)` answers null for a draft without it, so ordering, dispatch, the customer's lists and the status routes never meet an incomplete restaurant; `cityAndStatus(id)` reads any restaurant's city and status (identity's accounts). `setAccountStatus` refuses a draft without its record (`status_change_not_allowed`). `RestaurantDraftsService` (each save under the restaurant's lock, only on a `draft`, else `409 not_a_draft`; the same values again write nothing; audited): `saveBasics(id, cityId, …)` starts the draft with the id the panel names (an insert that does nothing on a conflict, so a retry makes no second draft; `restaurant.create`) or saves step 1 (`restaurant.basics`: names, categories as `setRestaurantCategories` checks them, description; the logo and cover through the image routes); `saveLocation` the pin, address, the restaurant's number (E.164, any country) and the owner's name (`restaurant.location`, never a number: the owner's number is the step-6 account, which identity keeps encrypted); `saveOperations` at least one opening period (`checkWeeklySchedule`; a new schedule version only when the hours change), the prep time within the chooser's bounds, the minimum order in the base (`checkBaseCurrency`) and the coverage (`restaurant.operations`); `saveCommission` (step 4, OWN) withdraws every version not yet in force (`commission_rates.withdrawn_at`, the only column the application role may update there; readers skip withdrawn versions) and adds the rate, or the launch rate now and the standard rate from its end, so a new save replaces the plan (`restaurant.commission`, no reason: the first commission, not a change); `saveContract` (step 5) the signing date, the duration (1 to 120 months) and the scans (`restaurant_contract` and `restaurant_licence` uploads in `private-docs`, `prepare` before the transaction and `link` in it), clearing a confirmation when anything changes (`restaurant.contract`); `confirmContract` (OWN, OPS) with the price-parity pledge needs the date, duration and contract scan (`invalid_contract`; the licence is optional, decided by us) (`restaurant.contract.confirm`). `merchants.contracts` holds one row per restaurant. `draft(id)` reads every step with `steps` (basics always, location with a pin, operations with the record and a period, commission with a version, contract once confirmed); `drafts(cityId)` lists the city's drafts, the latest saved first. Routes (`admin-restaurant-drafts.controller.ts`, no key: the panel names the draft, so each save sets values): `GET /v1/admin/restaurant-drafts?cityId=`, `GET …/choices?cityId=` (active categories, active zones, the base, the prep bounds and the defaults of `merchant.default_prep_min`, `default_min_order` and `default_commission_pct`) and `GET …/{id}` (`restaurants.read` or `restaurants.create`; a restaurant past its draft answers `404` without `restaurants.read`, so DATA reads drafts only); every step's save moves `restaurants.updated_at`, the list's order; `PUT …/{id}/basics`, `/location`, `/operations` and `/contract` (`restaurants.create`: DATA, OPS, OWN), `PUT …/{id}/commission` (`restaurants.commission`: OWN), `POST …/{id}/contract/confirm` (`restaurants.manage`: OWN, OPS; `{ parityPledge: true }`); its shared shapes carry OpenAPI ids (`AdminDraftPoint`, `AdminDraftMoney`, `AdminDraftPeriod`, `AdminDraftCoverage`, a flat object since the Dart client cannot build a `oneOf`, and `AdminDraftChoice`), so the generator never merges them with another route's alike shape and renames the apps' models; each answers the draft, the scans as 10-minute signed GETs on the panel's origin (`privateImageUrl`, `/private-docs/restaurants/`). `400 invalid_restaurant`, `invalid_schedule`, `invalid_prep_time`, `invalid_coverage`, `invalid_commission`, `invalid_contract`, `unknown_category`, `upload_wrong_purpose`, `upload_invalid`; `404 unknown_restaurant`, `unknown_city`, `upload_not_found`; `409 not_a_draft`, `not_in_base_currency`, `upload_used`, `upload_missing`, `upload_expired`.
- **The menu editor (AP §9.6.3, §9.6.6; slices 2.30b and 2.31, `menu-editor.service.ts`, `admin-restaurant-menu.controller.ts`):** `MenuEditorService` edits the menu of a restaurant in any status but `terminated` (`403 restaurant_terminated`, `lockEditable`): the wizard's step 7 and the profile's «القائمة». The panel names each new row's id, so `saveCategory` (name, hiding, the daily window: two different minutes of the day, `checkWindow`) and `saveItem` (category, code, name, description, price above 0 in the base, hiding, badge `new`, `most_ordered`, `spicy`) create the row the first time (at the end of the menu or of its category, published at once) and set its fields after; the same values again write nothing; an empty code is generated on creation (`I0001` onward, skipping codes typed by hand) and kept on an edit; a move to another category puts the item at its end. `orderCategories` and `orderItems` take every row once in the new order (`invalid_menu` otherwise). Audited `menu.category.create`, `.update`, `.order`, `menu.item.create`, `.update`, `.order`. Names up to `MENU_NAME_MAX` (80), descriptions `MENU_DESCRIPTION_MAX` (300), codes `ITEM_CODE_MAX` (20), decided by us. **Prices (slice 2.31, owner decision 2026-11-19):** past test mode (`active`, `suspended`) a price set (a new item's, a changed price, a new option or a changed delta) needs `menus.edit_prices` (`MenuChange.mayEditPrices` from the session; `checkPriceEdit`, `403 price_edit_not_allowed`); migration 0121 took that permission from DATA, so DATA edits names, photos, order and availability of an active restaurant but not its prices (AP §9.6.5 «DATA only before activation»). `logPrices(tx, …)` writes a `price_changes` row (`changedBy` the admin) for every changed price in any status, in the change's transaction, and while the restaurant is `active` or `suspended` one state row: `price_change` (`itemId` or `optionId`, `name`, `oldPrice`, `newPrice`, `currency`) for one change, `price_change_bulk` (`count`) for several. Routes under `/v1/admin/restaurant-drafts/{restaurantId}/menu`: `GET` (`restaurants.read` or `restaurants.menus`; every published row, hidden ones included, with its code, badge, photo, switch, window and `optionGroupIds`, `optionGroups` (every live group by name with its options and linked items), `accountStatus`, `editable` and `pricesLocked`, true past test mode for a session without `menus.edit_prices`), `PUT …/categories/{categoryId}`, `PUT …/items/{itemId}`, `PUT …/category-order`, `PUT …/categories/{categoryId}/item-order`, `PUT …/availability` (`type` category, item or option) (`restaurants.menus`: OWN, OPS, DATA), each answering the menu and taking no key (each sets a state to a value); the photo stays `PUT /v1/admin/restaurants/{id}/items/{itemId}/photo` (no crop yet; imgproxy resizes). `menu(id)` gives each item's `badge`. `400 invalid_menu`; `403 restaurant_terminated`, `price_edit_not_allowed`; `404 unknown_restaurant`, `unknown_menu_row`; `409 item_code_taken`, `not_in_base_currency`.
- **Option groups and add-ons (AP §9.6.6; slice 2.31, `option-groups.service.ts`):** `OptionGroupsService`, each write under the restaurant's lock (`lockEditable`) and audited: `saveGroup` (name up to 80, `0 ≤ min ≤ max`, `max ≥ 1`, and once it has live options `max` at most their count; `menu.option_group.create`, `.update`), `saveOption` (name, delta 0 or more in the base, `removed` sets `deleted_at` and keeps `max` within the options left; a new option or a changed delta follows the price rule above and is logged and told as a price; `menu.option.create`, `.update`), `orderOptions` (`menu.option.order`), `linkItems` (the group's items as a set: a new link at the end of the item's groups, an old link again by clearing `removed_at`, an unlinked one `removed_at`; `menu.option_group.items`) and `setItemGroups` (an item's groups in order; `menu.item.option_groups`); `groups(id)` reads them for the editor. Routes: `PUT …/menu/option-groups/{groupId}`, `…/option-groups/{groupId}/options/{optionId}`, `…/option-groups/{groupId}/option-order`, `…/option-groups/{groupId}/items`, `…/menu/items/{itemId}/option-groups` (`restaurants.menus`, no key, each answering the menu). A group is never deleted yet.
- **Bulk changes (AP §9.6.6 «تعديل جماعي», «توفر جماعي»; slice 2.31, `bulk-price.ts`, `menu-bulk.service.ts`):** `bulkPrices` (pure, `bulk-price.test.ts`): by a percentage in basis points or a fixed amount in minor units, up or down; each new price rounded to the base's `currency.<code>.rounding_unit` by its `rounding_mode` (`roundToUnit` from fx), never below one unit while the raw price stays above 0; a price the change brings to 0 or below is refused, never floored (decided by us); a price off the unit's grid never moves against the direction asked (a lowering never raises it, a raise never lowers it, the floor included); a percentage is 1 to `BULK_MAX_BP` (100,000, 1000%) and a new price at most 15 digits (`invalid_menu`). `MenuBulkService.preview` (a read, no lock) answers every published item of the menu or one category with its price before and after (an item still priced in another currency than the base is left out); `apply` takes `Idempotency-Key` (`merchants.idempotency_keys`, migration 0122: per admin account, the request's hash and the answer, 24 hours, in the command's transaction; `replay` and `keep`, shared with the import; `422 idempotency_key_reused`), recomputes under the restaurant's lock, needs `menus.edit_prices` past test mode, refuses the whole change while a price would reach 0 (`409 price_reaches_zero`; `409 rounding_unit_missing` without the unit), updates the changed items, audits `menu.bulk_price` (the change and each item's before and after) and logs and tells them through `logPrices` (one summary). `setItemsAvailability` switches many items in one transaction (`MerchantsService.setAvailabilities`, each row audited as its own switch; one row not the restaurant's refuses them all). Routes: `POST …/menu/bulk-price/preview` (a read, no key), `POST …/menu/bulk-price` (answers `changed`, `unchanged`), `PUT …/menu/bulk-availability` (answers the menu, no key) (`restaurants.menus`).
- **The Excel import (AP §9.6.6 «استيراد من Excel», AP §5.9; slice 2.31, owner decision 2026-11-19; `menu-import.ts`, `menu-import.service.ts`, `admin-restaurant-menu-import.controller.ts`):** `planImport` (pure, `menu-import.test.ts`) reads the template's Arabic headers in any order («رمز الصنف», «التصنيف», «الصنف», «الوصف», «السعر», «مجموعات الخيارات»; `missing_headers`, `no_rows`, `too_many_rows` above `IMPORT_MAX_ROWS` 2,000) and reports each row's errors by the sheet's row number and column (`required`, `too_long`, `invalid_price`: a price in major units above 0 and a whole amount of the base's minor unit, `repeated_code` in the file or held by a deleted item, `unknown_group`: group names separated by «,» or «،», groups already made in the editor, never created or changed by the import; `ambiguous_name` for a category or group name two published rows share); a row whose code matches an item changes it (`fields`: category, name, description, price, groups), a row with a new code or none adds an item (an empty code is generated), a new category name is added at the end; nothing is ever deleted. `GET …/menu/import-template` (`restaurants.menus`) answers the workbook filled with the menu (base64 in JSON, one right-to-left sheet, the prices in major units, the groups by name), so a re-import changes only what was edited. The upload is files' `menu_import` (one `.xlsx` of `MAX_IMPORT_BYTES` 2 MB at most, in `private-docs`; migration 0123). `POST …/menu/import/dry-run { uploadId }` (a read, no key) answers the report and the diff, writing nothing; `POST …/menu/import { uploadId }` (`Idempotency-Key`) reads the file before the transaction (`FilesService.prepare` and `preparedBytes`; a zip whose central directory says it unzips beyond `IMPORT_MAX_UNZIPPED`, 20 MB, is refused before it is opened, `unzippedSize`, and only the first 2,001 filled rows are kept, with their sheet numbers), then under the restaurant's lock recomputes the plan through the transaction (`menu`, `groups`, fx's base and currencies take its executor, so no pool read runs under the lock; an item still priced in another currency than the base is left out of the template and the plan) (`409 import_invalid` while any row errs or the file is not the template), needs `menus.edit_prices` past test mode when it adds an item or changes a price, links the upload (`409 upload_used` for one imported before), adds the categories and items, changes the items (a row moves an item only when its category changed) and their groups, audits each row (`menu.category.create`, `menu.item.create`, `.update`) and `menu.import` with the counts, and logs and tells the price changes as one summary. `400 import_unreadable` for a file that is no workbook; the files refusals as the images'.

- **Change requests and their review (RA §9.2 «After admin review», §9.5, §16 #22, §18; AP §9.6.7; slice 2.32b, owner decisions 2026-11-19; `change-requests.service.ts`, `admin-menu-change-requests.controller.ts`):** `ChangeRequestsService` writes `merchants.change_requests` under the restaurant's lock (`lockEditable`), each write audited (`menu.change_request`, `.withdraw`, `.approve`, `.reject`, target `menu_<type>`, with `restaurantId`, `requestId` and `field`) and told to every device (`menu_changed`). - **Operating state (RA §7.1):** `operatingState(input, now)` in `operating-state.ts` is the pure rule: the account status first (draft, suspended, terminated), then holiday close, closed by schedule, paused (any source), busy at the active-orders limit, open; busy mode is a flag on open. Only `open` takes orders (`takesOrders`). `MerchantsService.operatingState(id, now)` reads its parts, and `storefrontState(id, now)` adds what customers see (CU §9.2): a holiday close's or an operations pause's message and, while closed by the schedule, the next opening (`nextOpening` in geo's `weekly-hours.ts`); active orders come from the count the ordering module registers with `countActiveOrders` (0 until it does), since this module cannot read `ordering`.
- **Hours (RA §10):** `weekly_schedules` are versions, never changed; the newest is in force, and none is closed every day. A period is a weekday (0 Sunday to 6 Saturday) and two times in minutes after midnight on the city's clock (`GeoService.cityTimeZone`). One whose closing time is not after its opening time crosses midnight and belongs to the day it starts; 00:00 to 00:00 is the whole day. Periods last 30 minutes or more and never overlap, across midnight and the end of the week included (`checkWeeklySchedule` in geo's `weekly-hours.ts`). A restaurant opens at the opening minute and closes at the closing minute.
- **Holiday closes and pauses** are rows with an end and `ended_at` when ended early. An operations or auto pause may have no end (until resumed); a restaurant pause always has one, at the latest the end of its opening period (S3, a `CHECK`).
- **Status commands (`restaurant-status.service.ts`, RA §7.1–7.4, §10, §18; slice 2.6a):** `RestaurantStatusService` takes the restaurant's lock, reads the state inside its transaction (`storefrontState(id, now, tx)`, which also answers `closesAt`, the end of the opening period it is in), checks the pure rules of `status-rules.ts`, writes the change, audits it with the user, role and device (`restaurant.pause`, `.resume`, `.busy_mode`, `.holiday_close`, `.holiday_close.end`, `.prep_time`, `.max_active_orders`, `.pickup_currencies`, `.auto_pause`) and records a state change (below). The restaurant pauses for 15, 30 or 60 minutes or «حتى أعيد الفتح» only while open or busy and never over an operations or auto pause (one pause at a time: its own pause is replaced; `409 paused_by_operations`, `409 restaurant_closed`); its pause and busy mode end with the opening period (S3); it resumes only its own pause. Busy mode takes one of `merchant.busy_extra_options_min`, sets `busy_until` to the period's end and `busy_started_at`. A holiday close starts now or later and ends after it starts, with a message up to 100 characters, one at a time (a new one ends the one in force or set), not while suspended. The default prep time stays within the chooser's floor and ceiling; the maximum active orders is off or 1 to 50. **The auto-pause** (`autoPauseIfDue(tx, id, unanswered, now)`) runs in every automatic cancellation's transaction: it takes the restaurant's lock, then reads the business day's count through the transaction (so two cancellations at once never both miss it) and pauses at `merchant.auto_pause_unanswered_count`; it ends a restaurant pause and is not added over an operations or auto pause, nor for a terminated restaurant (the timers and the auto-pause leave a terminated restaurant alone, `lockAny`, rather than fail its cancellation).
- **A rejection's side effects (RA §8.6, S2; slice 2.7a):** the ordering module calls them inside the rejection's transaction, after the order's lock: `switchOffForRejection(tx, …)` switches the items off as `setAvailability` does (`switchedOffUntil` gives the end before the transaction), and `RestaurantStatusService.afterRejection(tx, …)` sets the restaurant's own pause (`pauseIn`) or busy mode (`busyModeIn`) only while the restaurant is open or busy and the pure rules allow it, answering false otherwise without a refusal, so the rejection stands; it takes the lock that leaves a terminated restaurant alone (`lockAny`), so operations can still reject its orders. `pause` and `setBusyMode` run the same parts in their own transaction. The item-unavailable report after acceptance (RA §8.12, slice 2.9) switches its items off with the same `switchOffForRejection`, and its withdrawal switches them on again with `switchOnItems(tx, …)`, both in the order's transaction.
- **Pickup currencies (RA §12.7, AP §9.6.4; slice 2.14a):** `restaurants.pickup_currencies` (text[], empty by default) holds the other currencies the restaurant accepts from the captain; the base is always accepted and never listed. `RestaurantStatusService.pickupCurrencies(id)` answers the base, the ones switched on that the city still has enabled and the choices (every other enabled currency, in display order), each with its Arabic name from fx; a city without a base answers no base and no choices; `setPickupCurrencies(id, currencies, change)` takes enabled non-base currencies once each (`400 invalid_pickup_currencies`), stores them in display order and audits `restaurant.pickup_currencies`; it applies to the pickups requested after it. `restaurant(id)` gives `pickupCurrencies` as stored. The panel's status read carries `pickupBaseCurrency`, `pickupCurrencies` and `pickupCurrencyChoices`. The owners' own `GET` and `PUT /v1/merchant/settings/pickup-currencies` (slice 2.14b; `403 owner_only` for a cashier, `403 restaurant_terminated`; no key, since it sets a value: to confirm with the owner, as for the status routes, since RA §18 asks a key of every command) answer `baseCurrency`, `accepted` and `choices`, audited with the user and the device (`changeOf`).
- **Operations' commands (AP §9.6.5, RA §16 #23; slice 2.6b):** `operationsPause` (15, 30 or 60 minutes, until a later time, or until resumed; a required reason, an optional message to customers up to 100 characters, `checkOperationsPause` in `status-rules.ts`) ends any pause in force, the auto-pause included, and works in any state, holding across opening periods as the auto-pause does; a timed one ends with `QUEUES.pauseEnd`. `operationsResume` ends any pause in force (none: nothing). `operationsEndBusyMode` is `setBusyMode(null)` by an admin. Each records `paused_by_operations` (with the reason), `resumed_by_operations` or `busy_ended_by_operations`, with `endedAuto: true` when an auto-pause ended, so the relay resolves «مطعم أوقف تلقائياً» at once. An admin's holiday close needs a reason (`reason_required`), stored on the row; `setOrderSettings` without `defaultPrepMin` changes only the maximum. Ended pauses keep who ended them (`ended_by_type`, `ended_by_id`). The panel's routes (`admin-restaurant-status.controller.ts`): `GET /v1/admin/restaurants/{id}/status` (`restaurants.read`; the status with the name, the account status and `heldPauseSource` and `heldPauseUntil`, the pause in force whatever the state, since an operations or auto pause holds while closed by the schedule or a holiday, with an operations pause's message and reason), `POST .../status/pause`, `POST .../status/resume`, `DELETE .../status/busy-mode`, `PUT` and `DELETE .../holiday-close`, `PUT .../max-active-orders` and `PUT .../pickup-currencies` (`restaurants.manage`, OWN and OPS). None takes an `Idempotency-Key`: a resume, an end of busy mode, a holiday close and the maximum set a state to a value, and the same operations pause again (the same message and reason, and the same end, or the same length within `REPEAT_WINDOW_MS`, 60 s) is a repeat that changes nothing, so a retry neither moves its end nor pushes twice; each answers the status after it. `isAutoPaused` lets the relay keep the alert of a newer auto-pause.
- **State changes (`state-events.ts`, RA §18, E4):** every status change writes a row of `merchants.state_outbox` (`recordStateChange`, with a `QUEUES.restaurantStateRelay` job) in its transaction; the application role may change only `relayed_at`. The relay lives in `notifications` (`RestaurantStateRelay`), which already imports this module: `relayStateChanges(relay)` lends it the rows in id order under `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` and marks those it returned from. The timers (`status-timers.ts`, `MerchantsWorkerModule` in the worker): `QUEUES.pauseEnd` at a pause's end records `pause_ended` (with `push` only when the restaurant takes orders again) once (`pauses.end_told_at`) if the pause was not ended early, due by the worker's or the database's clock (one early by both is sent again); `QUEUES.busyReminder` every `merchant.busy_mode_reminder_min` records `busy_reminder` while busy mode is still the one it was set for (`busy_started_at`) and its moment (`due`) is newer than `busy_reminded_at`, and sends the next with an id derived from its moment, so a repeated job neither reminds twice nor starts a second chain.
- **Coverage (owner decision 2026-11-06):** a radius in whole metres around the pin, or one or more of the city's zones (`coverage_zones`). `covers(id, point)` needs the point inside an active service zone (`GeoService.zoneAt`) and then inside the radius (a straight line on the sphere, `ST_DWithin` on geography) or inside one of the restaurant's zones.
- **The restaurants a customer sees (CU §8.2):** `coveringRestaurants(cityId, point, testAccount)` returns the `active` and visible restaurants that cover the point, and for an admin test account those in `test` mode too (AP §9.8, slice 2.25b), as `covers` does, in any operating state (closed ones are listed last by the customer's list). `seenByCustomer(restaurant, testAccount)` (`operating-state.ts`) is the same rule for one restaurant, which the restaurant page and the quote use.
- **The panel's account actions (AP §9.6.5, D16; slice 2.30c, `admin-restaurant-account.controller.ts`):** `GET /v1/admin/restaurants/{id}/account` (`restaurants.read`: the status with a suspension's reason, `visible`, `sortWeight` with `sortWeightMax`, the commission in force, the next one and `commissionHistory`, every version not withdrawn, the latest start first, `commissionVersions`), `POST …/suspend` and `…/reactivate` (`restaurants.manage`, a reason; `changeAccountStatusFrom`: only from active and from suspended, so a reactivation never skips test mode's checklist, `409 status_change_not_allowed`; the status already reached changes nothing), `PUT …/visibility` (`setVisibility`, audited `restaurant.visibility`) and `PUT …/sort-weight` (`setSortWeight`, 0 to `SORT_WEIGHT_MAX` 1000, decided by us, `400 invalid_sort_weight`, audited `restaurant.sort_weight`; the customers' lists sort by it, the higher first), neither on a terminated restaurant (`403 restaurant_terminated`), and `POST …/commission` (`restaurants.commission`: OWN; `{ rateBp, effectiveFrom|null, reason }`, `setCommission`), each answering the account and taking no key (each sets a value; the same commission again is a repeat). The termination is cashdesk's `POST …/terminate` (its `CLAUDE.md`).
- **Commission (AP §9.6.5, D16):** `commission_rates` are versions in basis points of the items value (1500 is 15%), each in effect from its start for new orders, never changed (a version not yet in force may only be withdrawn, above). A change needs a reason and starts now or later, never in the past (D16), never for a draft (`409 not_a_draft`: the wizard's step 4) or a terminated restaurant; the same rate with the same start, or the same rate «now» within 60 s of the latest version, is a repeat that writes nothing (slice 2.30c). Each change records `commission_change` (`rateBp`, `from`) in the state outbox, whose relay pushes the owners RA §16 #33 «تتغيّر عمولتك إلى {النسبة}% اعتباراً من {التاريخ}» (the date on the city's clock); an order keeps the rate in force at its placing (`orders.commission_bp`), so a change from tomorrow leaves today's order at the old rate (`ordering/merchant-money.test.ts`). `commissionAt` takes the latest start reached; of two with the same start, the later version. A launch commission is two versions: the launch rate now and the standard rate from its end date. `commissionSchedule(id, now)` gives the rate in force and the next version after now (the owners' «الحساب», slice 2.19d).
- **Money:** the minimum order, item prices and option deltas are `bigint` minor units in the city's base currency, checked under `lockCityCurrencies` (until the first order locks the base, another of the city's currencies is allowed, as for the delivery fee). The module registers them with `FxService.useCityAmounts` (terminated restaurants left out), so fx refuses a base change while one is in another currency. Each amount keeps its own currency, and `menu` returns item prices and option deltas as `Money`. An item price is above 0, a delta 0 or more (RA §9.4). The audit log keeps amounts as strings.
- **Menu (AP §9.6.6, RA §9.1):** categories, items (a code unique in the restaurant, `I0001` onward when left empty), option groups with options, and the links of groups to items. A group with a minimum of 1 or more is required; 0 makes it an add-on group. Rows created by an admin are published at once; a restaurant's new rows stay unpublished until their change request is approved (RA §9.2). Rows are never deleted: `deleted_at` and `removed_at` hide them. `menu(id)` reads the published menu in order; an item is unavailable when it, its category, or every option of one of its required groups is (RA §9.3). A category's daily window (`window_starts_at`, `window_ends_at`, minutes on the city clock, crossing midnight when the end is not after the start) makes it and its items unavailable outside it (`inDailyWindow` in geo's `weekly-hours.ts`, CU §9.3).
- **Availability (RA §9.3):** `setAvailability` switches a category, an item or an option on, or off «حتى الغد» (`unavailable_until` = the next `finance.business_day_cutoff` on the city's clock, `nextCutoff` from `geo`) or «حتى أعيده» (no end). `menu(id, now)` reads a switch off «حتى الغد» as on from its time, so nothing has to switch it back; it returns each row's own switch (`switchedOff`) beside `available`, the one customers see (a category's switch leaves its items' own switches alone). Audited as `menu.<type>.availability` with the user, their role and the device id as the audit device.
- **Devices (RA §5.2 item 5, §5.3):** `devices`, one per restaurant and app installation, with its name, platform, app version and the restaurant user who named it last (an `identity` account id, a plain column). `nameDevice` lets a cashier name a device the first time only; renaming is managing devices, an owner's (RA §5.4, `device_rename_not_allowed`): `renameDevice` (the owners' «الأجهزة», RA §14.2, slice 2.33) changes only the name, refuses a terminated restaurant and an unknown device (`unknown_device`), and audits `merchant_device.rename`. **The receiving devices (RA §4.3–4.6, §14.2; slice 2.42, migration 0143, `receiving.ts`, `receiving-devices.service.ts`, `merchant-device.controller.ts`):** each device keeps `ringing` (an owner's «الرنين على هذا الجهاز», on by default; a cashier's device rings whatever it holds), its last check (`checked_at`, `check_passed`, `check_failed` with the codes of `deviceCheckCodes`, `check_delay_ms`; a `CHECK` keeps them together; `sleep`, Windows' power plan, never fails it) and `present_at`, the last moment the worker saw it on the channel; `merchants.receiving` keeps since when an open restaurant has had no receiving device. None of these is audited or a state change. `connectedAt` (within `DISCONNECTED_AFTER_MS`, 32 s of the last sighting, 60 s of the last heartbeat), `isReceiving` and `withoutDeviceStep` are the pure rules (`receiving.test.ts`); the notifications worker applies them. The app's routes (`@AppSignedIn()`, owners and cashiers, no key: each sets a value): `PUT /v1/merchant/device` (`appVersion` at every start, `ringing` only from an owner, null leaves it) and `PUT /v1/merchant/device/check` (`failed`, `delayMs`), each answering `ringing` (always true for a cashier) and the check; `404 unknown_device` before the device is named. `devices(id)` answers them with each device.
- **Status routes** (`merchant-status.controller.ts`): `GET /v1/merchant/restaurant/status` (the pill and sheet: state, pause source, ends, busy mode, active orders against the maximum, the holiday close, the choices), `POST .../status/pause`, `POST .../status/resume` and `PUT .../status/busy-mode` for owners and cashiers; `GET` and `PUT /v1/merchant/restaurant/hours`, `PUT /v1/merchant/restaurant/order-settings` and `PUT`/`DELETE /v1/merchant/restaurant/holiday-close` for owners (`403 owner_only`). None takes an `Idempotency-Key` (to confirm with the owner: RA §18 asks a key of every command; the app does not retry a status command, and reads the status again after any failure), and each answers the status or the hours after it. A terminated restaurant's owners get `403 restaurant_terminated` on every one (RA §5.5). `/v1/merchant/status` is the health route, hence `restaurant/status`.
- **Restaurant app routes** (`merchant-menu.controller.ts`): `GET /v1/merchant/restaurant` (status, suspension reason, operating state), `GET /v1/merchant/menu` and `PUT /v1/merchant/menu/availability`, for owners and cashiers (RA §5.4), scoped to `AppSession.merchant.restaurantId`; a row of another restaurant answers `404`. A terminated restaurant's owners keep only the money section (RA §5.5), so the menu routes answer `403 restaurant_terminated` (`setAvailability` checks it under the restaurant's lock). Any reader of the menu goes through `menu(id, now)`, never the raw `available` column, which still reads off after «حتى الغد» ends.
- **Images (AP §9.6.3, §9.6.6; slice 2.5):** `restaurants.logo_key`, `restaurants.cover_key` and `items.photo_key` are keys in `public-media` (architecture §5.8). `setRestaurantImage(id, 'logo' | 'cover', uploadId, change)` and `setItemPhoto(id, itemId, uploadId, change)` call `FilesService.prepare` before their transaction (it copies and checks the file, so no storage call runs under the restaurant's lock), then take the restaurant's lock and `FilesService.link` the upload in the same transaction, publish at once (an admin's edit), and audit `restaurant.logo`, `restaurant.cover` or `menu.item.photo` with the old and new keys. The same upload again is a repeat that changes nothing; an upload already linked to another record is refused (`upload_used`). The old object stays in storage. The panel's routes (`admin-restaurant-images.controller.ts`, the temporary «الصور» screen, owner decision 2026-11-09): `GET /v1/admin/restaurants?cityId=` and `GET /v1/admin/restaurants/{id}/images` (`restaurants.read` or `restaurants.menus`), `PUT /v1/admin/restaurants/{id}/logo` and `/cover` (`RESTAURANT_PROFILE`: `restaurants.manage` or `restaurants.create`, AP §9.6.3 step 1), `PUT /v1/admin/restaurants/{id}/items/{itemId}/photo` (`restaurants.menus`); the onboarding wizard (2.30) and the menu editor (2.31) take them over. The restaurant's own photos go through their review (slice 2.32b).
- **Categories (AP §9.13, AP §9.6.3 step 1; slice 2.27a):** `restaurants.category_ids` (uuid[], content's ids as plain values, in the chosen order), set by `createRestaurant`'s `categoryIds` or `setRestaurantCategories(id, ids, change)`: active categories (`CategoriesService.checkActive`), once each, at most `RESTAURANT_CATEGORIES_MAX` (5, decided by us, to confirm with the owner), else `unknown_category`; checked before the transaction, so a category removed at that moment can still be linked, which is harmless since readers drop inactive ids; under the restaurant's lock, audited `restaurant.categories` (the same list again writes nothing). A removed category stays in the list until it is set again; readers keep only the active ones. The old free-text `category` column is no longer read or written, to be dropped in a later migration. The panel's `GET` (`restaurants.read` or `RESTAURANT_PROFILE`) and `PUT /v1/admin/restaurants/{id}/categories` (`RESTAURANT_PROFILE`; `400 unknown_category`, `404 unknown_restaurant`, no key) in `admin-restaurant-images.controller.ts`, until the restaurant profile (2.30) takes them.
- **Logs:** `price_changes` («سجل الأسعار») is never changed; `priceLog(id, limit)` reads it newest first with the item's or option's name (and the option's group) for reporting's `GET /v1/admin/restaurants/{id}/menu/price-log`, and `menuRowIds(id)` gives every menu row's id by audit target type for «سجل التغييرات» (slice 2.31). `change_requests` holds one pending request per field of a target (a partial unique index); a newer edit replaces its pending value.
