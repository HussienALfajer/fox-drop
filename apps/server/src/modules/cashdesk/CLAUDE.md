# cashdesk module

The cash desk «الصندوق» at the office counter: drawer sessions, numbered receipts and the desk's transactions (architecture §4.2; AP §9.10.3, §4.8, §8.5.1, §8.5.4). The money itself moves only through the ledger.

## Rules

- **What this module owns:** `cashdesk.drawer_sessions` and their `drawer_session_currencies`, `receipts` and their `receipt_lines`, `receipt_counters` and `idempotency_keys`. Captains, cities and admin accounts are plain ids. Custody is read and locked through `CaptainsService` (`deskCustody`, `deskLock`), never `ledger.*` directly; journals go through `LedgerService.post` (`test/ledger-boundary.test.ts`).
- **Drawer sessions (AP §9.10.3):** `openSession` opens one office session per user (`drawer_sessions_one_open`, under the user's advisory lock; `409 session_open`; `404 unknown_city`) with a float per enabled city currency (`deskCurrencies`; one not given is 0). Each session is the owner of its own `platform.cash` accounts, so the expected cash of a currency is the float plus that account's balance. `closeSession` takes the counted cash of every currency of the session (`400 invalid_amounts` otherwise), only by the session's own user (`403 not_your_session`); a variance needs a note (`400 note_required`), and the expected, counted and note are kept on the session only, never in the ledger (owner decision 2026-11-12). The close locks the session `FOR UPDATE` and a desk transaction holds it `FOR SHARE`, so a close waits for one still running and none is left unconfirmed. A closed session and a counted currency are never changed: two triggers of migration 0064 refuse it. Audited `cash_desk.session_open` and `cash_desk.session_close` (with each currency's variance). The field session of a restaurant visit joins with slice 2.19 (`session_kinds` has `office` only).
- **The daily hand-in (AP §9.10.3 «تسليم النقد اليومي»):** `handIn` takes the cash per currency: a currency the captain must hand in posts `custody.hand_in` (custody −, the drawer's cash +), one the office owes them posts `custody.reimburse` (the other way; the first captain of a handover is paid back so, CA §19 item 21). Never more than the custody in that currency (`409 above_custody`), never a currency at zero (`409 nothing_due`); a reimbursement never above the drawer's cash in that currency, its float plus what the session moved (`409 drawer_short`; to confirm with the owner). The rest stays open custody. It needs the user's open session (`409 no_open_session`) of the captain's city (`404 unknown_captain` otherwise). Locks: the session `FOR SHARE`, then the captain (`deskLock`), so two desks never settle one balance twice (the second finds what is left); the key is checked again under the captain's lock, so a request sent twice at once answers the first one's receipts (`cashdesk.test.ts` proves the three cases). One receipt per type («تسليم نقد», «صرف للكابتن»), hand-in first. Cross-currency settlement (`custody.fx_convert`) comes with 2.17b.
- **Receipts (AP §4.8):** `{CITY}-{YYYY}-{000001}` per city and year on the city's clock, the number taken by an upsert of `receipt_counters` in the receipt's transaction, so a rollback gives it back and the numbers keep no gap (a sequence would leave one); `receipts_city_year_sequence` refuses a repeat. A receipt and its lines are never changed (the application role has no `UPDATE`); each line keeps the custody expected before (the captain's side), the amount and its journal, whose source is `{ kind: 'receipt', id }`. The cashier's name is read from identity at display; the captain's is kept as issued. Audited `cash_desk.receipt`.
- **What the captain hears (CA §14 #19, AP §9.10.3):** in the receipt's transaction, the push `captain.desk_receipt` «إيصال {رقم}: {النوع} {المبلغ}» (silent; `receiptTypeLabels` from content, the amounts joined with « + »; data `target: custody`, `receiptId`; source `cashdesk:receipt:<id>`) and `CaptainsService.custodyChanged`, whose listener writes the captain channel's `captain.availability_changed`, so the app reads its state again and the block of a closed day lifts at once.
- **Keys (architecture §5.3):** the hand-in takes `Idempotency-Key`: `idempotency_keys` per admin account with a hash of the captain and lines and the receipts issued, kept in the command's transaction; a repeat within 24 hours answers those receipts (one rule for the expiry in `replay` and `keepKey`), another command with the key `422 idempotency_key_reused`. Opening and closing a session take none: a repeat is refused by what the first changed and names its target.
- **Admin routes (`admin-cash-desk.controller.ts`, `cash_desk.record`: OWN, FIN):** `POST /v1/admin/cash-desk/captains/search` (`{ cityId, q }` in the body, so a number never sits in a URL; a part of the name, or a number in E.164, `00…`, or the local Syrian `09…` and Turkish `05…` forms (`phoneOf`), found by identity's `captainByPhone`; a read, so no key), `GET …/captains/{captainId}` (the custody: usage, limit, `closedDue`, the last cutoff and each currency's balance, closed-day part, today's part and base equivalent), `GET …/sessions/current?cityId=` (the user's open session and the city's currencies to count), `GET …/sessions?cityId=` (every user's for OWN, the user's own otherwise; 50 newest), `POST …/sessions`, `POST …/sessions/{sessionId}/close`, `POST …/hand-ins` (answers the receipts) and `GET …/receipts/{receiptId}`. Money travels as strings of minor units.
