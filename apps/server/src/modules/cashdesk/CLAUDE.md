# cashdesk module

The cash desk «الصندوق» at the office counter: drawer sessions, numbered receipts and the desk's transactions (architecture §4.2; AP §9.10.3, §4.8, §8.5.1, §8.5.4). The money itself moves only through the ledger.

## Rules

- **What this module owns:** `cashdesk.drawer_sessions` and their `drawer_session_currencies`, `receipts` and their `receipt_lines` and `receipt_conversions`, `receipt_counters`, `statements`, `merchant_statements` and their `merchant_statement_lines`, `manual_entries`, and `idempotency_keys`. Captains, cities and admin accounts are plain ids. Custody is read and locked through `CaptainsService` (`deskCustody`, `deskLock`), never `ledger.*` directly; journals go through `LedgerService.post` (`test/ledger-boundary.test.ts`).
- **Drawer sessions (AP §9.10.3):** `openSession` opens one office session and one field session per user (`drawer_sessions_one_open` on the user and kind, under the user's advisory lock; `409 session_open`; `404 unknown_city`) with a float per enabled city currency (`deskCurrencies`; one not given is 0). Each session is the owner of its own `platform.cash` accounts, so the expected cash of a currency is the float plus that account's balance. `closeSession` takes the counted cash of every currency of the session (`400 invalid_amounts` otherwise), only by the session's own user (`403 not_your_session`); a variance needs a note (`400 note_required`), and the expected, counted and note are kept on the session only, never in the ledger (owner decision 2026-11-12). The close locks the session `FOR UPDATE` and a desk transaction holds it `FOR SHARE`, so a close waits for one still running and none is left unconfirmed. A closed session and a counted currency are never changed: two triggers of migration 0064 refuse it. Audited `cash_desk.session_open` (with its kind) and `cash_desk.session_close` (with each currency's variance). A desk transaction finds the user's open session of its kind by `openOf`: a captain's hand-in and settlement take the office session only.
- **The daily hand-in (AP §9.10.3 «تسليم النقد اليومي»):** `handIn` takes the cash per currency: a currency the captain must hand in posts `custody.hand_in` (custody −, the drawer's cash +), one the office owes them posts `custody.reimburse` (the other way; the first captain of a handover is paid back so, CA §19 item 21). Never more than the custody in that currency (`409 above_custody`), never a currency at zero (`409 nothing_due`); a reimbursement never above the drawer's cash in that currency, its float plus what the session moved (`409 drawer_short`; to confirm with the owner). The rest stays open custody. It needs the user's open session (`409 no_open_session`) of the captain's city (`404 unknown_captain` otherwise). Locks: the session `FOR SHARE`, then the captain (`deskLock`), so two desks never settle one balance twice (the second finds what is left); the key is checked again under the captain's lock, so a request sent twice at once answers the first one's receipts (`cashdesk.test.ts` proves the three cases). One receipt per type («تسليم نقد», «صرف للكابتن»), hand-in first.
- **Cross-currency settlement (AP §9.10.3, §8.6; slice 2.17b):** `quoteConversion(cityId, { from, to, amount })` answers what the screen shows before the confirmation: `amount` of `from` in `to` at the current rates (`FxService.snapshot`: both currencies enabled, with a rate and a rounding unit, else `409 rate_missing`), rounded by the target's rule (`exchange` from fx; `400 invalid_amounts` at zero), the rate version of each currency but the base, and one unit of the dearer currency in the other to 4 places (`unit`). The hand-in's `conversions` (each `from` once, never also a `to`) run first, under the captain's lock: `amount` comes off the `from` balance towards zero (`409 nothing_due`, `above_custody` as for cash), the quote is computed again and must give the same `converted` and rate versions (`409 rate_changed`), then the cash lines are checked and taken against the custody after it, so a captain holding dollars settles in lira, or dollars owed to them net against lira due. Each posts `custody.fx_convert` (custody − in `from` and + in `to`, the other sides on `platform.fx_rounding`, which keeps the rounding difference; the rates on the journal) sourced from the first receipt of the hand-in, kept in `receipt_conversions` with its journal. A conversion alone, with no cash, is refused (`invalid_amounts`): every desk transaction is a receipt. FIN and OWN only, as AP §9.10.3 says, through `cash_desk.record`. The push names the cash lines only.
- **«أرصدة الكباتن» (AP §9.10.2, slice 2.17b):** `balances(cityId, now)` takes `CaptainsService.balances` (every captain not terminated, and a terminated one still holding custody or earnings: the custody, its blocks, the grace, the earnings) and adds the netted custody in base (`custodyInBase`, the sort, highest first), the latest «تسليم نقد» receipt (`lastHandInAt`) and the latest «صرف المستحقات» receipt (`lastPayoutAt`, slice 2.18a).
- **Receipts (AP §4.8):** `{CITY}-{YYYY}-{000001}` per city and year on the city's clock, the number taken by an upsert of `receipt_counters` in the receipt's transaction, so a rollback gives it back and the numbers keep no gap (a sequence would leave one); `receipts_city_year_sequence` refuses a repeat. A receipt and its lines are never changed (the application role has no `UPDATE`); each line keeps the custody expected before (the captain's side), the amount and its journal, whose source is `{ kind: 'receipt', id }`. The cashier's name is read from identity at display; the captain's is kept as issued. Audited `cash_desk.receipt`.
- **What the captain hears (CA §14 #19, AP §9.10.3):** in the receipt's transaction, the push `captain.desk_receipt` «إيصال {رقم}: {النوع} {المبلغ}» (silent; `receiptTypeLabels` from content, the amounts joined with « + »; data `target: custody`, `receiptId`; source `cashdesk:receipt:<id>`) and `CaptainsService.custodyChanged`, whose listener writes the captain channel's `captain.availability_changed`, so the app reads its state again and the block of a closed day lifts at once.
- **Keys (architecture §5.3):** the hand-in takes `Idempotency-Key`: `idempotency_keys` per admin account with a hash of the captain and lines and the receipts issued, kept in the command's transaction; a repeat within 24 hours answers those receipts (one rule for the expiry in `replay` and `keepKey`), another command with the key `422 idempotency_key_reused`. Opening and closing a session take none: a repeat is refused by what the first changed and names its target.
- **Admin routes (`admin-cash-desk.controller.ts`, `cash_desk.record`: OWN, FIN):** `POST /v1/admin/cash-desk/captains/search` (`{ cityId, q }` in the body, so a number never sits in a URL; a part of the name, or a number in E.164, `00…`, or the local Syrian `09…` and Turkish `05…` forms (`phoneOf`), found by identity's `captainByPhone`; a read, so no key), `GET …/captains/{captainId}` (the custody: usage, limit, `closedDue`, the last cutoff and each currency's balance, closed-day part, today's part and base equivalent), `GET …/sessions/current?cityId=` (the user's open session and the city's currencies to count), `GET …/sessions?cityId=` (every user's for OWN, the user's own otherwise; 50 newest), `POST …/sessions`, `POST …/sessions/{sessionId}/close`, `POST …/hand-ins` (answers the receipts; `conversions` optional), `POST …/conversions/quote` (a read, so no key), `GET …/receipts/{receiptId}` (with its `conversions`) and `GET …/balances?cityId=` (`finance.read`: OWN, FIN). `GET …/captains/{captainId}` answers the captain's `handInGrace` and `closedDueBlocks` (above the tolerance with no grace: what a grace lifts) too. Money travels as strings of minor units.

- **«عهدتي» (CA §10.2, AP §9.10.3; slice 2.17c):** `GET /v1/captain/custody` (`captain-custody.controller.ts`, `@AppSignedIn()`) answers the custody per currency with its base equivalent, «من أيام سابقة» and «من اليوم», the limit, usage and `warn`, the netted `total` (+ «عليك تسليم», − «لك عند المكتب»), the grace's end, the latest `CUSTODY_ENTRIES` (100) entries of the custody accounts (`CaptainsService.custodyEntries`, through `LedgerService.accountEntries`; each with its kind, the order's number from `OrdersService.orderNumbers` or the receipt's from `receiptNumbers`; decided by us: the latest, not every one ever) and the latest `CUSTODY_RECEIPTS` (50) receipts (`captainReceipts`, newest first, with their lines, conversions and cashier). The module imports `OrderingModule` for the numbers. The office comes with the captain's availability, not here. Every receipt already sends `captain.availability_changed`, which the app follows to read it again.

- **«تصفية الخميس» (AP §8.5.5, §9.10.3, D12; slice 2.18a, owner decisions 2026-11-15; `settlement.service.ts`):** `SettlementService.list(cityId, now)` answers the day (`payoutDay`: `captain.payout_weekday` against the business day on the city's clock, `finance.business_day_cutoff`), `captain.negative_earnings_policy` and the balances of every captain with earnings or custody not at zero. `preview(captainId, payoutCurrency, now)` answers what the screen shows: the custody not at zero, the earnings (base; `captain.earnings` in another currency than the base throws), the payout (the whole positive balance, in the base or another enabled currency by `quoteOf` at the current rate, with the rate versions and the unit), the negative balance to collect under `collect_at_desk`, and the net per currency (+ the captain gives, − the desk pays: owing 300 ₺ of custody with 500 ₺ of earnings, −200 ₺). `settle(user, command, now)` takes what the screen showed (the custody, the earnings, the payout, `reason`) with `Idempotency-Key`: off the payout day only OWN (`403 not_payout_day`), with a reason (`400 reason_required`; owner decision 2026-11-15: the same full settlement, audited); then, as the hand-in, the session `FOR SHARE` and the captain's lock, the key again, `409 nothing_due` with no custody and no earnings, `409 balance_changed` when the custody or earnings moved since the screen, `409 rate_changed` for a payout quote that moved, `409 drawer_short` when a currency's net out of the drawer is above its cash. Every currency of the custody is settled whole (no conversion here: the daily hand-in has it), each kind on its receipt, hand-in first: «تسليم نقد» (`custody.hand_in`), «صرف للكابتن» (`custody.reimburse`), «صرف المستحقات» (`captain_payout`: `earn.payout`, the earnings + and the drawer's cash −, in another currency through `platform.fx_rounding` with the rate versions; the line's expected is the amount owed, below zero) and «تحصيل رصيد سالب» (`captain_earnings_collection`: `earn.payout` the other way, base only; the label is ours, to confirm with the owner). The ledger keeps the hand-in and the payout apart while the cash nets on the screen (owner decision 2026-11-15). A negative balance under `carry_forward` stays. Each receipt pushes `captain.desk_receipt`; the statement pushes `captain.weekly_statement` «كشف الأسبوع جاهز: قبضت {المبلغ}» when something was paid (CA §14 #20; silent; the payout in its currency; data `target: statement`, `statementId`; source `cashdesk:statement:<id>`). Audited `cash_desk.settlement` (with the reason off cycle). The key keeps the statement (`idempotency_keys.statement_id`), so a repeat answers the same settlement. No deposit instalment is posted until the kit (2.35).
- **The weekly statement (`cashdesk.statements`, never changed):** one row per settlement. It covers the period since the previous actual payout (owner decision 2026-11-15): the anchor is the captain's latest statement that paid or collected (by `ledger_to`), so a week carried forward stays in the next statement. The ledger stamps an entry with its transaction's start, so an entry committed after the anchor read may carry an earlier time: the period reads the base `captain.earnings` entries from `PERIOD_OVERLAP_MS` (1 hour, the longest a transaction may run) before the anchor's `ledger_to` (`LedgerService.periodEntries`) and leaves out the anchor's `journal_ids` (the journals it covered) and its receipts' journals; the entries and the balance are separate reads, so they are read again (up to 3 times) while a commit between them leaves them apart. Each row keeps: the orders whose `earn.order_pay` nets above zero, pay by part (`distance_pay` from the collection, `OrdersService.distancePay`; `base_pay` the rest of `earn.order_pay`, with the minimum's top-up, the pay of an order not delivered, and a reversal of an earlier week's pay, so a pay line may be below zero), waiting, peak, bonuses, charges, instalments, adjustments, `carried_in` (the balance less what the period's entries explain: the anchor's `carried_out`), `balance`, `paid` (base), the payout's currency and amount, `collected`, `carried_out`, `off_cycle` and the reason, the receipts and `journal_ids`. `CHECK`s keep the sums. `period_from` is the anchor's time (null for the first). The push «كشف الأسبوع جاهز: قبضت {المبلغ}» goes only when something was paid. `GET …/statements/{statementId}` answers it for printing.
- «عهدتي»'s receipts (`captainReceipts`) are the custody ones only; payouts belong to «أرباحي» (2.18b).
- **«أرباحي» (CA §10.6, §10.7; slice 2.18b, owner decision 2026-11-15; `earnings.service.ts`, `earnings.ts`):** `GET /v1/captain/earnings` (`captain-earnings.controller.ts`, `@AppSignedIn()`) answers, in the city's base: `today` (since the last `finance.business_day_cutoff`) and `week` (`payoutCycle`: since the start of the latest business day on `captain.payout_weekday`, today included, so a Thursday's payout is the first line of its week), each with the earnings (every entry but `earn.payout`), the orders (whose `earn.order_pay` nets above zero, as the statement counts them) and the online minutes (`CaptainsService.onlineMinutes`); this week's orders newest first (`orderLines`: the order's pay, waiting and peak bonus, its total) with the parts the collection recorded (`OrdersService.payParts`), null when they do not add up to the ledger's (an outcome without a collection, a reversal); the other lines (`otherLines`: `bonus`, `charge` with its order, `instalment`, `payout` with its receipt, a collection being a payout above zero, `adjustment`), each with its journal's reason; the balance (`CaptainsService.earnings`), `nextPayout` (the date of today on a payout day, unless the captain was already paid today, else the next one) and `negativePolicy`. The app reads it again on `captain.availability_changed`, which every receipt sends.
- **The statements in the captain app (CA §10.7, §14 #20; slice 2.18c):** `GET /v1/captain/statements` and `GET /v1/captain/statements/{statementId}` (`captain-statements.controller.ts`, `@AppSignedIn()`) read `cashdesk.statements` through `SettlementService.captainStatements` (the latest `CAPTAIN_STATEMENTS` (52), newest first by `created_at` then id; decided by us: the latest, not every one ever) and `captainStatement` (by id and captain together, so another captain's statement is `404 unknown_statement`, as an unknown one). The list gives each statement's time, period, orders, paid, payout, collected and carried; the statement gives the desk's lines but the cashier, the session, the receipts and the off-cycle reason (internal). Charges and instalments travel as the amounts taken off, above zero.
- **Settlement routes (`cash_desk.record`):** `GET /v1/admin/cash-desk/settlements?cityId=`, `POST …/settlements/preview` (a read, so no key), `POST …/settlements` (answers the statement and the receipts) and `GET …/statements/{statementId}`.

- **The restaurant's weekly statement (AP §8.7, RA §12.3, §12.5, §16 #30; slice 2.19a; `merchant-statement.ts`, `merchant-statements.service.ts`, `merchant-statements.worker.ts`):** `cashdesk.merchant_statements` (one per restaurant and cutoff, `merchant_statements_restaurant_cutoff`; never changed) and `merchant_statement_lines`, every amount from the restaurant's side (+ it owes, − the platform owes it; the ledger's party view negated). Each restaurant's statement is built and written in one transaction under its advisory lock (`merchant-statement:<id>`), re-reading the previous statement there, so two workers never build two from the same one. `MerchantStatementsService.cutoffs(cityId, now)` gives the latest cutoff that starts `merchant.settlement_weekday` (`payoutCycle` from `earnings.ts` with that weekday) and the next one. The worker's `MerchantStatementsWorker` (`CashDeskWorkerModule`, every `MERCHANT_STATEMENTS_MS`, 1 minute, and once at start) runs `closeDue` per active city until a sweep of that cutoff completes; `closeAt` takes each restaurant of the city (`MerchantsService.restaurantIds`) on its own: nothing when its latest statement is at or after the cutoff; the `merchant.account` entries in the base (another currency throws) read from `STATEMENT_OVERLAP_MS` (1 hour) before the previous cutoff, before this cutoff and outside the journals the previous statement covered (an entry committed late goes to the next one); none and nothing due takes no statement. `buildStatement` (pure, `merchant-statement.test.ts`) makes the lines: an order for each order with a commission or discount share raising what is owed (the pickup payment and its lines from `OrdersService.pickups`, null without a confirmed pickup), an exception for every other entry with its `cause`, a payment for `merchant.settlement`. The cause is ordering's `merchantEntryCause(type, reason, owes)`, read from the reasons ordering posts with: `order` (a commission or discount share without a reason raising what is owed: the order line), `food_credit`, `credit`, `pickup_refund`, `pickup_kept` (a debit without a reason: the pickup payment kept on a cancelled or withdrawn order), `items_removed`, `fee_lowered`, `fee_cut_returned`, `debit`, `commission_reversal`, `commission_restored`, `discount_reversal`, `outcome_changed` (any reversal of «تغيير النتيجة»), `adjustment`; `merchantExceptionCauses` in the schema is that list but `order` (the unit test compares them); and the totals, `CHECK`ed: `week_total` = commission + discount share + exceptions, `earlier_unpaid` = the previous `total_due` − the payments since, `total_due` = `earlier_unpaid` + `week_total`. The owners' push `merchant.weekly_statement` «كشف الأسبوع جاهز: المستحق {المبلغ}…» (silent, owners only, data `target: statement`, `statementId`, source `cashdesk:merchant-statement:<id>`, expiring a day after the cutoff) goes in the statement's transaction only when something is due and only within a day of the cutoff, so a worker back late or the first deploy takes the statement quietly. `restaurants(cityId, now)` lists each restaurant with a balance not at zero: the balance, the latest statement, what is unpaid of it (its total due less the payments since its cutoff) the latest statement's commission, discount share and exceptions, and `overdueSince`: payments settle the oldest statement first (owner decision 2026-11-16), so `oldestUnpaid` walks the latest `OVERDUE_LOOKBACK` (52) statements newest first, each week's positive total covering the newest part of the unpaid amount, and the statement it reaches back to is overdue at its cutoff + `merchant.settlement_overdue_days` days (24 hours each, so Monday 06:00 by default; owner decision 2026-11-16, rather than the end of that business day). The overdue first, then the largest owed. A city without a base currency counts as not swept, and a restaurant whose statement fails is retried every minute but reported once per cutoff. Routes (`admin-restaurant-settlement.controller.ts`, `cash_desk.record`): `GET /v1/admin/cash-desk/restaurants?cityId=` and `GET …/merchant-statements/{statementId}` (`404 unknown_statement`).

- **«تسوية» of a restaurant at the office (AP §8.7, §9.10.3 tab 3, RA §12.4, §16 #31; slice 2.19b; `merchant-settlement.service.ts`):** a receipt names exactly one party (`receipts_party`, migration 0072): a captain, or a restaurant with `due_before` (what it owed before, + it owes, in its account's base `due_currency`); a restaurant's line keeps `base_amount` (its value in the base) instead of `expected` (`receipt_lines_expected_or_base`). The captain readers (`receiptsOf`, `GET …/receipts/{id}`) take captain receipts only (`captainReceiptTypes`). `MerchantSettlementService.preview(restaurantId, lines)` answers the balance (+ owes, − the platform owes it; the `merchant.account` in another currency than the base throws while not at zero), each line's value in the base at the current rate (`quoteOf` towards the base, rounded by the base's rule, with the rate version and the unit; the base itself as is), the total and what stays (`remaining`, of the sign of the balance); no lines reads the balance alone. `settle(user, command, now)` takes what the screen showed (`due`, each line's `amount`, `base` and `rates`) with `Idempotency-Key`: the user's open session `FOR SHARE` of the restaurant's city (`409 no_open_session`; `404 unknown_restaurant` for an unknown one or another city), then `lockRestaurantAccount` (the statement's own advisory lock, so a payment is never half in a statement and two desks never settle one balance twice), the key again, `409 nothing_due` at zero, `balance_changed` when the balance moved, `rate_changed` when a line's base or rate version moved, `above_balance` when the total in the base is above what is owed (either way), and for a payout `drawer_short` when a currency is above the drawer's cash (under the session's advisory lock `cashdesk.drawer:<id>`, so two payouts of one session take turns; the captain's reimbursement does not take it yet). The balance is read under the restaurant's lock, which ordering's entries do not take, so a credit committed meanwhile can leave the restaurant settled past zero: the ledger then shows what the platform owes it. A line in another currency is valued by the base's rounding unit, so an amount owed below that unit is settled in the base. Partial payments leave the rest due. One receipt per confirmation: `merchant_settlement` when the restaurant owes (it pays), `merchant_payout` when the platform owes it (the desk pays); each line posts `merchant.settlement` (the restaurant's account against the drawer's `platform.cash`, through `platform.fx_rounding` with the rate version for another currency). Audited `cash_desk.receipt`. A payment pushes the owners `merchant.settlement_receipt` «إيصال {رقم}: استلمنا منك {المبلغ}» (silent, the cash lines joined with « + », data `target: receipt`, `receiptId`, source `cashdesk:receipt:<id>`); a payout pushes nothing (RA §16 #31 names a payment received; to confirm with the owner). `receipt(id)` answers a restaurant's receipt with its method and visitor, a captain's being null. Routes (`admin-restaurant-settlement.controller.ts`, `cash_desk.record`; the desk's `deskUser` and `refusalsAsHttp` are `desk-http.ts`): `POST …/restaurant-settlements/preview` (a read, no key), `POST …/restaurant-settlements` and `GET …/restaurant-receipts/{receiptId}` (`404 unknown_receipt`).
- **The field visit «زيارة ميدانية» (AP §9.10.3 tab 3, RA §12.4, AP §8.7; slice 2.19c, owner decisions 2026-11-16):** `openSession(…, 'field')` opens the user's field session beside the office one with a zero float in every currency (`400 invalid_amounts` for a float above zero; `409 name_required` for an account without a name, such as the first OWN, since the receipt names the visitor), from a phone. A restaurant's settlement names its session (`session`: `office` by default, or `field`, `409 no_open_session` without one of that kind). In the field session the receipt is issued and pushed at once, its method `field` and its visitor the session's user. At the office, a visit by a staff member without FIN is recorded when the cash reaches the desk, naming them (`visitorId`, an active named team member without `cash_desk.record` from `AdminSessionsService.teamWithout`, since an OWN or FIN visitor records in a field session; `404 unknown_visitor` otherwise; `400 invalid_visitor` with a field session): method `field`, receipt issued then. A receipt keeps `method` (`office` by default, so a server before 2.19c keeps working) and the visitor's id and name as issued (`receipts_visitor`: both exactly when the method is `field`, a restaurant's receipt only; migration 0073). The field session closes like an office one, on its counted cash, when the cash reaches the office; its cash stays on its own `platform.cash` accounts, never moved into the office drawer (owner decision 2026-11-16, as a closed office session's cash). The key's hash covers the session and visitor, left out at the office without a visitor so a 2.19b key still answers its receipt. Deploy: no field session may open while the 2.19b server still serves, and every field session is closed before a rollback below 2.19c (the old server reads any open session of the user as its drawer). Routes: `POST …/sessions` takes `kind`; `GET …/sessions/current` answers `field` beside `session`; `POST …/restaurant-settlements` takes `session` and `visitorId`; `GET …/visitors` (`cash_desk.record`) lists those team members by name. The panel's «تسوية» takes the field session whenever it is open.
- **A statement's status (RA §12.3, owner decision 2026-11-16; slice 2.19b):** what is unpaid of the latest statement (`standing`) is the balance now less what the entries since its cutoff raised (outside the journals of the statements read), between zero and its total due, so a payment and a credit since settle the oldest first and a commission or a payout since belongs to next week. A payout (a `merchant.settlement` raising what is owed) counts in its statement's exceptions, never as a negative payment (`buildStatement`). `statementStatuses` (pure, `merchant-statement.test.ts`) walks the latest statements newest first with that amount: each statement's unpaid part is what is left once the newer weeks took their positive totals, at most its own total due; none is `paid` «مسدّد», all of it `unpaid` «غير مسدّد», between `partly_paid` «مسدّد جزئياً»; a statement whose total due is at zero or below has none (null). A credit week settles the oldest first, as a payment does. The list's latest statement and the statement answer their `status`, the statement also its `unpaid`; a statement older than the year read back is `paid`.
- **«الحساب» in the restaurant app (RA §12.2, §12.5, R10; slice 2.19d; `merchant-account.service.ts`, `merchant-account.controller.ts`):** `GET /v1/merchant/account` (`@AppSignedIn()`, owners only: a cashier gets `403 owner_only`; a terminated restaurant's owners keep it, RA §5.5) answers in the city's base: the balance (+ owes), `settlementDate` («موعد التسوية» on the city's clock: the latest statement's cutoff while `standing` finds it unpaid, else the next cutoff), the commission in force and the next scheduled version (`MerchantsService.commissionSchedule`), today's pickups since the business day's cutoff (`OrdersService.restaurantPickups`: the order, the confirmation time, the captain's first name, the amount and the lines booked), and `weekLines`, every `merchant.account` entry since the latest statement as the next statement will take it (from `STATEMENT_OVERLAP_MS` before its cutoff, outside the journals of the statements `standing` read), `buildStatement` per business day so an order's lines stay on its day, newest day first, each line with its `day`. The lists are flat (the Dart generator misnames an enum inside a nested object). The app reads it on opening; nothing pushes a change.
- **«الكشوف» and «الإيصالات» in the restaurant app (RA §12.3–12.5, §16 #30–#31, R10; slice 2.19e; `merchant-records.service.ts`, `merchant-records.controller.ts`):** owners only (`403 owner_only` for a cashier; a terminated restaurant's owners keep them). `GET /v1/merchant/statements` lists the restaurant's latest statements (`standing`'s 52, newest first) with the period, week total, total due, status and unpaid part (`statementStatuses`); `GET /v1/merchant/statements/{statementId}` answers the statement line by line, each exception with «من قرّر» (`decidedBy`, the actor type of its journal from `LedgerService.journalActors`, and `decidedByName`, an admin's name from identity; null on an order line or a payment); another restaurant's or an unknown one is `404 unknown_statement`, checked by `MerchantStatementsService.statement(id, restaurantId)` before anything else is read. `MerchantRecordsService.deciders` is shared with «الحساب», whose `weekLines` carry the same `decidedBy` and `decidedByName`. `GET /v1/merchant/receipts` lists the latest `MERCHANT_RECEIPTS` (100) receipts of the restaurant, payments and payouts, newest first (`MerchantSettlementService.restaurantReceipts`, read as a batch with `receipt` through `views`); `GET /v1/merchant/receipts/{receiptId}` adds what was owed before and after (`404 unknown_receipt` for another restaurant's). A receipt's `staffName` is the visitor on a field visit, else the cashier. A receipt shows once the desk issues it, which is when the cash reaches the desk or the OWN or FIN visitor records it (RA §12.4).
- **The overdue settlement (AP §8.7, §9.1.3, RA §12.2, §12.6, §16 #32; slice 2.19f; owner decision 2026-11-16: never an automatic pause, AP §13 item 2):** `overdueOf` (pure, `merchant-statement.test.ts`) walks the statements newest first with `standing`'s unpaid amount: each newer week still within its days (its cutoff after now less `merchant.settlement_overdue_days` × 24 hours) takes its positive total first, and what reaches back past them is overdue, with `oldestUnpaid`'s statement; `restaurants`' `overdueSince` follows the same rule. `MerchantStatementsService.overdue(cityId, restaurantId, now)` answers the amount in the base, that statement and since when. The worker's `MerchantStatementsWorker` runs `syncOverdue` per active city every minute after the statements: for each restaurant, under `lockRestaurantAccount` (so a payment being recorded is read whole), `AlertsService.sync` raises «مطعم متأخر في التسوية» (`restaurant_settlement_overdue`, warning, OWN and FIN, subject the restaurant with its name and, through `codeDetails`, the oldest unpaid `statementId`, kept current, which the alert list answers for the panel's «فتح الكشف») while something is overdue and resolves it once paid, and queues the owners' push `merchant.settlement_overdue` «لديك مبلغ متأخر: {المبلغ}. يرجى التسوية في أقرب وقت.» (silent, data `target: account`, source `cashdesk:merchant-overdue:<statementId>`, so once per overdue statement: a part paid that leaves the same statement overdue sends no other); an alert of a restaurant no longer in the city's list is resolved. The city's base and overdue days are read once per sweep. A failing restaurant is reported once until it syncs again, and retried every minute. «الحساب» answers `overdue` (`amount`, `statementDate` on the city's clock: «متأخر: {المبلغ} من كشف {التاريخ}»), and `GET /v1/merchant/account/overdue` (owners only, `403 owner_only`) answers `{ overdue: { currency, amount, statementDate } | null }` for the owners' banner, read on its own so the banner does not read the whole account.
- **A restaurant's statement for any period (AP §9.10.5; slice 2.19g; `merchant-period.service.ts`):** `MerchantPeriodService.statement(restaurantId, fromDay, toDay)` (`periodFits`: real days, the last not before the first, at most `PERIOD_MAX_DAYS`, 366; `invalid_period` otherwise, null for an unknown restaurant) takes the business days from the cutoff of the first (`cutoffOn` from geo) to the cutoff of the day after the last; `between(restaurantId, from, to)` reads the opening (`LedgerService.balanceAt` before `from`) and the `merchant.account` entries in the base from `from` to `to` (`rangeEntries`) in one repeatable-read snapshot, builds the lines with `buildStatement` (the opening as its previous due, so its total due is the closing) and gives each payment its receipt (`receipt_lines` by journal). It lives in `CashDeskPeriodModule`, which the reporting module imports in the API and the worker.
- **The order's owner lines (RA §8.4; slice 2.19d):** ordering's board gives owners `owner` (its `CLAUDE.md`); the account's lines come from the ledger, so the two agree once the order is finished.
- **«أسعار الصرف» (AP §9.10.4, §8.6; slice 2.20a; `admin-exchange-rates.controller.ts`):** fx's screen served here, since fx cannot import identity for the editors' names. `GET /v1/admin/exchange-rates?cityId=` and `GET …/history?cityId=&currency=` (`finance.read`: OWN, FIN) answer the board and a currency's versions, newest first, each with its editor's name (`AdminSessionsService.names`); `PUT /v1/admin/exchange-rates` (`fx.edit_rates`; `{ cityId, currency, value, direction, reason?, confirmLargeChange? }`, no key: it sets the rate; the same rate again is a new version that confirms it) answers the board; `400 invalid_rate`, `not_city_currency`, `no_base_currency`, `404 unknown_city`, `409 large_change_unconfirmed` until the second confirmation. The rounding unit and mode are edited through the settings routes.

- **«القيود التصحيحية» (AP §9.10.7, AP §8.8; slice 2.20c; `manual-entries.service.ts`, `admin-manual-entries.controller.ts`):** `cashdesk.manual_entries` (never changed: the application role has no `UPDATE`) keeps each manual entry beside its journal: the kind (`adjustment` or `reversal`), the party (`captain`, `restaurant`, `customer`, a plain id), the journal it posted, the journal a reversal cancels (`manual_entries_reverses_journal`, once), the reason (1 to 500 characters) and the admin. `adjust(user, command, now)` posts one journal on one party account against `platform.adjustments` with the reason: `custody.adjustment` (`captain.custody`, any enabled city currency), `earn.adjustment` (`captain.earnings`), `merchant.adjustment` (`merchant.account`) or `dues.adjustment` (`customer.dues`, owner decision 2026-11-16), the last three in the base only (`400 invalid_currency`, `409 no_base_currency`); the account must be the party's (`400 invalid_account`); the amount is signed from the party's view (`partyAccounts`, `accountSigns`), never zero (`400 invalid_amounts`); the party must be of the city (`404 unknown_party`), a customer having dues there already (the dues block reads its order's city; decided by us). `reverse(user, command, now)` reverses a journal of the city (`404 unknown_journal`) once (`409 already_reversed`, under the journal's advisory lock `ledger.reverse:<id>`) through `LedgerService.reverse`, linked by `reverses_journal_id`; `409 not_reversible` for a reversal, a journal on no party account, or one with a source (an order's or a receipt's: «تغيير النتيجة» reverses an order's journals itself, so a manual reversal would make it fail, and a receipt is the cash that changed hands; corrected by an adjustment instead; decided by us, to confirm with the owner) or touching `platform.order_clearing`, `platform.cash` or `platform.fx_rounding`. Both take the party's desk lock first (`CaptainsService.deskLock`, `lockRestaurantAccount`; a customer's dues have none yet), so a hand-in, a settlement or a statement never reads a balance a manual entry is changing. A dues block follows the balance, so an adjustment that brings the dues to zero lifts it. Both take `Idempotency-Key` (`idempotency_keys.manual_entry_id`, checked again under the key's advisory lock, so a request sent twice at once answers one entry), are audited `ledger.manual_adjustment` and `ledger.reversal` (target `manual_entry`), and on a captain's account tell `custodyChanged`, so the app reads its custody and earnings again; nothing is pushed. `list(cityId)` answers the latest `MANUAL_ENTRIES_LISTED` (100), newest first, each with the party's name, the admin's name, the journal's party lines, what a reversal corrects (its type, reason, time, lines, order number and the manual entry that posted it, if any) and what reversed it (`LedgerService.journalRecords`). `account(cityId, party, partyId, account)` answers the currencies an adjustment of it takes, the account's balance per currency and its latest `ACCOUNT_ENTRIES_LISTED` (50) journals, each `reversible` or `reversed`: where the panel reverses an entry that no manual entry posted. `findParties` finds a captain by a part of the name or the number, a restaurant by a part of its Arabic name, a customer by the number only (identity's `customerByPhone`; the number is never answered). Routes (`ledger.manual_adjustment`: OWN, FIN): `GET /v1/admin/manual-entries?cityId=`, `POST /v1/admin/manual-entries`, `POST …/reversals`, `POST …/parties/search` (a read sent as a POST, no key) and `GET …/account?cityId=&party=&partyId=&account=`. Nested enums travel as strings (the Dart generator).
