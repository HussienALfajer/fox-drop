# geo module

Cities (AP §9.12.1), distances (architecture §7.1), service zones and the delivery fee (AP §9.12.2–9.12.3).

## Rules

- **Distances:** `GeoService.distance(from, to, { vehicle })` returns whole metres and how they were measured: `road` (OSRM) or `straight_x1.3`. Points are `{ latitude, longitude }` in degrees; one outside ±90 and ±180, or not a number, throws a `RangeError`, so the routes validate the points they take. Never call OSRM from another module or an app.
- **Fallback (AP §9.12.3):** the straight line (haversine) × 1.3, a fixed factor, when OSRM fails, takes longer than 1 second or has no server for the vehicle, and when it answers that there is no route between the points (owner decision 2026-11-06). It is also the only method when `pricing.distance_method` is `straight_line`, which never calls OSRM. Every fallback logs a warning with the vehicle; never log the points, since a delivery point is personal data.
- **The matrix (AP §9.12.6; slice 2.15b):** `GeoService.distancesTo(sources, to)` measures many points, each with its vehicle, to one point, in their order: one OSRM `table` call per vehicle (`RoadDistances.table`, a null for a source without a route), following `pricing.distance_method` (owner decision 2026-11-11: dispatch's ranking follows it). Each source without a route, and every source of a call that fails, takes too long or has the wrong rows, falls back to the straight line × 1.3 with a warning (`measureDistances`).
- **Routes (CA §4.6, CU §11.3; slice 2.29b):** `GeoService.route(cityId, from, to, { vehicle })` answers a leg's `Route` for the maps: OSRM's `route` with `overview=full&geometries=geojson` (`RoadDistances.route`) gives whole metres, whole seconds and the line (points from the start to the end); when it fails, takes longer than 1 second or has no route, the straight line × 1.3 timed at the city's `order.eta_speed_kmh` with `line` null (`measureRoute`). It always asks OSRM: `pricing.distance_method` is the fee's and dispatch's, not the map's (decided by us).
- **The port:** `RoadDistances` (`distance.ts`), injected as `ROAD_DISTANCES`. The server's adapter is `OsrmRoadDistances`; tests replace it with a fake (`overrideProvider(ROAD_DISTANCES)`), or point it at a local HTTP server.
- **OSRM:** one `osrm-routed` per vehicle profile (car, motorbike, bicycle, walking), at `OSRM_CAR_URL`, `OSRM_MOTORBIKE_URL`, `OSRM_BICYCLE_URL` and `OSRM_WALKING_URL`. An unset URL makes that vehicle fall back, with a warning at start; the tests and the OpenAPI generation set none. The data covers Azaz's region only (owner decision 2026-11-06; `infra/osrm/cut.sh`), so a point outside it gets a poor road distance: widen the region before adding a city.
- **Profiles (`infra/osrm/`):** OSRM's own car, bicycle and foot profiles, and `motorbike.lua`, the car profile with placeholder motorbike speeds until the ETA is tuned.
- **Zones (AP §9.12.2):** `geo.zones` holds each neighbourhood's polygon (`geometry(Polygon,4326)`, GiST-indexed, valid by a `CHECK`), its name, unique in its city, and whether it is active. `createZone` takes the corners in order and closes the ring; an outline that crosses itself or has fewer than three corners is `invalid_area`, an empty name `invalid_name`, a repeated one `zone_name_taken`. `setZoneActive` activates or deactivates one. Active zones never overlap (owner decision 2026-11-06): a zone whose inside meets an active zone's stays inactive (`zones_overlap`); sharing an edge is allowed. Changes of a city's zones take turns on an advisory lock. Polygons are written and read through PostGIS SQL only, never as JavaScript values.
- **Drawing and editing a zone (AP §9.12.2; slice 2.37c):** `saveZone(cityId, zoneId, { name, area, active }, change)` saves a zone under the id the panel chose: a new id creates it (`zone.create`, as `createZone`), a known one sets its name, outline and state together, writing and auditing only what changed (`zone.update` with the corners before and after); another city's id is `unknown_zone`. The checks of `createZone` apply, and an active zone (or one activated) is checked for overlap with its new outline. `zoneOutlines(cityId)` reads every zone with its corners in order (`ST_AsGeoJSON` with 15 decimals, the ring left open). Routes: `GET /v1/admin/cities/{cityId}/zones` (`zones.read`: the zones and the vote map) and `PUT …/zones/{zoneId}` (`zones.manage`; it sets the value sent, so no `Idempotency-Key`; `400 invalid_name`, `invalid_area`, `409 zone_name_taken`, `zones_overlap`, `404 unknown_city`, `unknown_zone`). The names are typed on the screen as people in Azaz call their neighbourhoods (CU §18 item 6, owner decision 2026-11-19: no seeded list).
- **The vote map (AP §9.12.2; slice 2.37c):** `voteMap(cityId)` counts the city's votes by cells of a thousandth of a degree (about 100 m; the point rounded in SQL), the most voted first, so the map guides expansion without placing any one device's vote. Votes with no city (beyond 50 km) are on no city's map.
- **The zone check (CU §7.1):** `zoneAt(cityId, point)` returns the active zone that covers the point, its edge included, or null outside every active zone; a point on an edge two zones share gets the older one. Whether a restaurant covers the point is the merchants module's coverage (`MerchantsService.covers`), which asks `zoneAt` first. `cityTimeZone(cityId)` is the clock of a city's restaurant hours.
- **«المدن» (AP §9.12.1; slice 2.37c, owner decisions 2026-11-19: the existing city only, adding one waits for a second city; the clock may change at any time):** `cities()` lists every city, active or not, `city(id)` one, and `updateCity(id, { name, code, timeZone, status }, change)` sets them under the city row's lock: an empty name `invalid_name`, a code other than two or three capitals or a time zone PostgreSQL or `Intl` does not know `invalid_city`, another city's code `city_code_taken`, an unknown city `unknown_city`; only what changed is written and audited (`city.update`), so the same values again record nothing. A new code numbers the receipts from then on (those issued keep theirs). Routes (`admin-cities.controller.ts`): `GET /v1/admin/cities/all` and `GET …/{cityId}` (`zones.read`; the city, its currencies in display order with their names, the currencies in use it lacks, and `baseLocked`), `PUT …/{cityId}` (`zones.manage`, the reason optional), and fx's own commands for the currencies: `PUT …/{cityId}/currencies/{currency}` (`setCityCurrency`: add one, its order, accepted or not) and `PUT …/{cityId}/base-currency` (`setBaseCurrency`, D25), each answering the city; the rates and rounding stay on «أسعار الصرف». The cutoff (`finance.business_day_cutoff`) and the platform hours (`platform.hours`) are settings, changed through the settings routes. They set values, so no `Idempotency-Key`. Refusals: `400` for a malformed value and fx's `not_city_currency`, `inactive_currency`, `base_disabled`, `unknown_currency`; `409` for `city_code_taken`, `base_locked`, `rate_missing`, `amounts_in_other_currency`; `404 unknown_city`.
- **Cities (AP §7.2):** `GET /v1/admin/cities` (`@SignedIn()`, `admin-cities.controller.ts`) lists the active cities by name with their code and time zone, for the panel's city selector; every city read of the panel takes the chosen id. Every signed-in user sees every active city until panel users have their cities.
- **The office (AP §9.12.1, CA C13, §7.5; slice 2.17c, owner decision 2026-11-12):** `geo.offices` (the point as latitude and longitude until the map, slice 2.29) and its `office_periods` are versions, never changed (the application role may only insert); the newest is in force. `setOffice(cityId, { location, hours }, change)` takes the city row's lock, refuses a point off the map or hours that break the week's rules (`invalid_office`: one desk period per weekday at most, then `checkWeeklySchedule`) and an unknown city (`unknown_city`), and audits `city.office` with the version before. `office(cityId, now)` answers the point, the hours, the city's clock and the desk's state now: `open` with `closesAt` (CA §7.5 «المكتب مفتوح حتى {الساعة}»), or closed with `opensAt` (null when no day is open); null until one is saved. Routes (`admin-cities.controller.ts`): `GET /v1/admin/cities/{cityId}/office` (`zones.read`) and `PUT` (`zones.manage`, OWN; no key: it sets the value sent).
- **Weekly hours (`weekly-hours.ts`):** the pure rules of a week of opening periods (RA §10): `checkWeeklySchedule`, `currentPeriod`, `nextOpening` and `inDailyWindow`, shared by the restaurants' hours, the platform's hours (ordering) and the office.
- **Points in a zone (AP §9.11.4; slice 2.27d):** `covered(zoneId, points)` answers the ids of the points the zone covers, its edge included, in one query (the arrays bound as single parameters with `sql.param`), so the polygon never leaves SQL.
- **Locating a point (CU §7.1):** `locate(point)` returns the active zone of an active city that covers the point (as `zoneAt` picks it), or, outside every one, the city whose active zones are nearest with no zone; null while no city has an active zone. The customer app knows no city, so its routes find it here.
- **Votes (CU §7.2, AP §9.12.2; owner decisions 2026-11-06):** `zone_votes` keeps a device's vote for a point outside every active zone, for the vote map and the lost-demand report (AP §9.17). `castVote` refuses a point inside a zone (`inside_zone`); a device votes once within 500 m of its earlier votes (`VOTE_RADIUS_METRES`, checked under the device's advisory lock), and a repeat records nothing (`already_voted`). The city is the one whose active zones are nearest, or none (null) beyond 50 km of every active zone (`VOTE_CITY_METRES`).
- **The delivery fee (AP §9.12.3; owner decisions 2026-11-06):** geo owns the fee rule. `geo.fee_rules` and `geo.fee_bands` are versions, never updated: `setDeliveryFeeRule` adds one in effect at once, and one that charges the same as the current rule adds nothing. A rule is «ثابت» (`flat`) or «شرائح مسافة» (`distance_bands`); a distance on a band's edge takes that band, the lower fee, and the last band has no edge, so every distance has a fee. Then the minimum fee raises a lower fee, and an items value at or above the free-delivery threshold pays 0, whatever the minimum. The items value is after the restaurant's item discounts, the value the commission is on (docs/domain/04). The pure rules are in `delivery-fee.ts`.
- **A restaurant's own rule (AP §9.12.3; slice 2.37a, owner decision 2026-11-19):** a whole rule of the city rule's shape that replaces it for that restaurant alone: `geo.restaurant_fee_rules` and `restaurant_fee_bands` (the merchants module's id a plain column), versions as the city's, in tables of their own so a server of an earlier version reading `fee_rules` never takes one for the city's rule. `setRestaurantFeeRule(cityId, restaurantId, rule | null, change)` adds one (null adds a `city_rule` version, no currency, that returns the restaurant to the city's rule; following the city already, or the same rule, adds nothing), with the city rule's checks and lock, audited `restaurant_fee_rule.set` on the target `restaurant` (so the restaurant's «سجل التغييرات» lists it); the caller checks the restaurant is the city's. `restaurantFeeRule` reads one in effect, `restaurantFeeRules(cityId)` every restaurant's in effect, `deliveryFeeVersions(cityId)` the city's versions newest first (the latest 50) with editor, reason and time. fx sees each one's currency (`useCityAmounts`), so a base change waits for them too.
- **Fee distance:** `deliveryFee(cityId, { restaurantId, from, to, itemsValue })` prices by the restaurant's own rule, or else the city's (`scope` says which; `restaurantId` null for the city's), and measures a distance-band fee by the motorbike's distance (`GeoService.distance`, so `pricing.distance_method` and the fallback apply), before any captain is known; a flat rule measures nothing. It returns the rule version it priced with, for the quote.
- **Money:** amounts are `bigint` minor units, charged in the city's base currency (AP §8.6). Until the first order locks the base, a rule may be entered in any of the city's currencies, so the owner can enter it in a new base and then change the base; once locked, only in the base. `deliveryFee` refuses a rule outside the current base (`fee_not_in_base_currency`). `setDeliveryFeeRule` checks the currency under `lockCityCurrencies`, and geo registers its rule with `FxService.useCityAmounts`, so fx refuses a base change while the rule is in another currency (`amounts_in_other_currency`). The audit log keeps the amounts as strings.
- **Every change** of a zone or a fee rule is audited in its transaction (`zone.create` with its corners, `zone.update`, `delivery_fee_rule.set`, `restaurant_fee_rule.set`).
- **The delivery estimate (`delivery-estimate.ts`, docs/domain/03, CU §8.4):** `estimateRange` (default prep + busy-mode minutes + the trip + `order.eta_buffer_min`, from a multiple of 5 to 10 minutes later) and `tripMinutes` (a distance at `order.eta_speed_kmh`), pure functions for the quote and the customers' browsing.
- **Business days (`business-day.ts`):** `nextCutoff` and `lastCutoff` around a moment, and `cutoffOn(day, cutoffMinutes, timeZone)`, the cutoff that starts a day written YYYY-MM-DD (a restaurant's statement for any period, slice 2.19g), each with the offset in force then.
- **Locally:** `docker compose run --rm osrm-build`, then `docker compose --profile osrm up --detach` (root `compose.yaml`). On staging one shared OSRM project serves every environment and rebuilds weekly (`infra/CLAUDE.md`).
