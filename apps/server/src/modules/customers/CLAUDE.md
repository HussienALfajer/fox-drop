# customers module

The customers' profiles and saved addresses (architecture §4.2; CU §5.3, §7), and their browsing of the restaurants that deliver to a point (CU §8.2, §8.4, §9.1–9.4). The late-cancellation strikes and review stop (docs/domain/08), the blocks and the dues read (AP §8.8, §9.8; slice 2.25a), and the panel's customers module (AP §9.8).

## Rules

- **The account is identity's:** `customer_accounts` and sign-in live in `identity`; this module keys its rows by the account id, a plain column, and reads `AppSession.accountId` in its routes.
- **Details (CU §5.3):** `profiles`, one row per account, written by `PUT /v1/customer/profile` («أكمل بياناتك», and later edits in «حسابي»). The first save needs `acceptTerms` (`400 terms_required`), records `terms_accepted_at` and, in the same transaction, accepts the customer terms in force (`TermsService.acceptCurrent`, AP §9.13); a later version is accepted through `POST /v1/customer/terms/accept` (the content module). Names are 2 to 30 Arabic or Latin letters with spaces and «-», two letters at least, Arabic marks such as the shadda allowed; the birthday is optional and before today on Syria's clock (`400 invalid_birthday`). `GET /v1/customer/profile` answers `completed: false` until the first save, so an app that stopped before the details shows them again whatever `newAccount` said.
- **The zone check (CU §7.1):** `GET /v1/customer/location` (public, for guests too, CU §5.1) answers `served`, `no_restaurants` or `outside_zones`, with the zone. The app knows no city: `GeoService.locate` finds it from the point, then `MerchantsService.coveringRestaurants` tells the first two apart.
- **Votes (CU §7.2):** `POST /v1/customer/zone-votes` (public) records the point through `GeoService.castVote`; geo owns the votes and their rules.
- **Saved addresses (CU §7.3):** `addresses`, never deleted (`deleted_at`), up to 10 (`MAX_ADDRESSES`, `409 address_limit`), one default (a partial unique index): the first address is the default, `isDefault` or `PUT …/default` moves it (never to one outside every active zone, `409 outside_zones`), and deleting the default passes it to the newest remaining address inside a zone, or else the newest. The pin must be inside an active zone when saved or edited (`409 outside_zones`); the city comes from `GeoService.locate`. «الحي» is not stored: each read names the active zone at the pin, and null is «خارج منطقة التوصيل حالياً» (Q6). Changes of one customer's addresses take turns on an advisory lock.
- **Contact number (U5, Q5):** null is the account's own number (the app sends null when the customer keeps it); another Syrian or Turkish mobile is kept encrypted with identity's key (`PhoneCipher`, architecture §15.3) and needs «اسم المستلم» (a `CHECK` and the route's schema).
- **Browsing (CU §8.2, §9; owner decision 2026-11-06: one plain list in Phase 1):** `BrowsingService` and `customer-browsing.controller.ts`, public for guests (CU §5.1). `GET /v1/customer/restaurants` lists the cards of `coveringRestaurants` for the point: open first, then busy and paused, then closed (holiday closes included), each group in the restaurants' own order; empty outside every zone. A card has the estimate range, the fee to the point before any item (`deliveryFee` with `itemsValue` 0, null while the city has no fee rule), and the state as customers see it (`MerchantsService.storefrontState`: the holiday or operations message, the next opening while closed by the schedule). `GET /v1/customer/restaurants/{id}` adds the description, minimum order, free-delivery threshold, weekly schedule, `deliversHere` and the menu; a restaurant customers do not see (not `active` or not visible) answers `404`. With `similarTo` (CU §11.9, slice 2.7b), the list is the similar restaurants under a rejected or unanswered order, at its pin (`BrowsingService.similar`): the open ones (busy mode included) of that restaurant's category, without it, in the list's own order, up to 5; empty when it has no category or the server does not know it.
- **The menu customers see (CU §9.3):** read through `MerchantsService.menu(id, now)` only. Hidden categories and items are left out; a category keeps its daily `window` (minutes on the city clock). An unavailable item keeps its place with `unavailableReason`: `unavailable` («غير متوفر حالياً») when any switch holding it back (its own, its category's, the options of a required group with none left) is off until turned back on; else `tomorrow` («يتوفر غداً») when some are off until the next cutoff; else `window` («متوفر من {الساعة}»), its category's window. `fromPrice` («من {أقل سعر}») is set when a required group has an option with a price delta: the price with each required group's cheapest available choices, and none when an option is in another currency. The restaurant's `logoKey` and `coverKey` and the item's `photoKey` are keys in `public-media` (null without an image), set from the panel (slice 2.5); the app shows them through imgproxy at a width it picks (architecture §5.8, CU §4.5).
- **The estimate (CU §8.4, docs/domain/03):** `estimateRange` and `tripMinutes` from `geo`: default prep + busy-mode minutes + the trip at `order.eta_speed_kmh` + `order.eta_buffer_min`, from a multiple of 5 to 10 minutes later. The trip is the motorbike's distance (`GeoService.distance`, so `pricing.distance_method` applies), as the fee's is. Ratings are not built: `rating` and `ratingCount` are always null, «جديد». A card measures the trip once: a distance-band fee's distance, or else `GeoService.distance`. A restaurant suspended between the list and its card is left out; its page answers `404`.
- **Snapshots (Q6):** an order keeps its own copy of the address, so editing or deleting one changes no order. `orderAddress(accountId, { addressId } | { fields })` gives it: a saved address of this customer (`unknown_address` otherwise) or the fields of an unsaved one (CU §7.4, another contact number encrypted here), with the active zone at the pin now as «الحي» (`outside_zones` outside every one). The route schemas of the address fields are in `address-fields.ts`; `unsavedAddressFields` and `withRecipient` are exported for the order command.
- **Late-cancellation strikes and the review stop (docs/domain/08; slice 2.8b, owner decision 2026-11-09):** `late_cancel_strikes` (one per order, never changed) and `review_stops` (one open per account, a partial unique index; the application role may change only `ended_at`, `ended_by` and, since 2.25b, `end_note`, the first two together). `recordLateCancel(tx, …)` runs in operations' cancellation's transaction (after the order's lock): it takes the customer's ordering lock (`customerOrderLock(accountId)`, exported for placing, which placing takes first, so a stop and a placing take turns; placing never takes an order's lock), records the strike, counts the customer's strikes within `customer.late_cancel_window_days` from the latest clear (`strikesSince`) and opens a stop at `customer.late_cancel_strikes` (both read by the caller with the order's city). `reviewStopped(accountId, executor?)` is what the quote shows as `review_stop` («حسابك موقوف حتى مراجعة فريق الدعم») and what placing reads again under the customer's lock.
- **Trust marks, strike clears and ending a review stop (AP §9.8, §8.8, D23, docs/domain/08; slice 2.25b, owner decisions 2026-11-17; `trust.ts`):** `trust_marks` and `strike_clears`, append-only (the application role cannot update them), each with the admin and the required note. `trustBasis` is the pure rule ordering's `ActiveOrders.customer` reads with `trustMark`: the deliveries count after the later of the latest fake outcome and the latest mark, and a «توثيق» newer than any fake outcome trusts the account whatever the count; «إزالة الثقة» restarts the count. `strikesSince` counts the strikes within the window and after the latest clear (`strikesClearedAt`), for `recordLateCancel` and the profile. `POST /v1/admin/customers/{customerId}/trust` (`trusted`, `note`; `customers.manage`: OWN, OPS) sets a value, so it takes no key; `.../clear-strikes` (`note`; `customers.clear_strikes`: OWN, OPS, SUP) records a clear and ends an open review stop with it, `409 nothing_to_clear` without a strike since the latest clear nor a stop; `.../end-review-stop` (`stopId` the stop the panel showed, `note`; same permission) ends that stop only and leaves the strikes counted, `409 not_stopped` when it is no longer open. Each runs under the customer's ordering lock and is audited (`customer.trust_mark`, `customer.clear_strikes`, `customer.end_review_stop`) and answers the profile, which adds `trustMark`, `lateCancels.cleared` and `reviewStop.id`.
- **Blocks (AP §9.8, CU §5.5, docs/domain/08; slice 2.25a, owner decisions 2026-11-17; `blocks.ts`, `customer-admin.service.ts`):** `customers.blocks`, one row per «حظر»: `until` (a duration, ending after now and within `BLOCK_MAX_DAYS`), `indefinite` («حتى إشعار آخر») or `dues` («حتى دفع مستحق»); the reason a code of Content's `customer_block` list with its label and customer text copied (CU Appendix C), the admin's required note, the start and the admin, and the end (`ended_at`, `ended_by`, `end_note`; the application role may change only those). `blockInForce` is the pure rule: an open `until` or `indefinite` row until its time; a `dues` row never stops ordering by itself: it records the `dues.charge` of its amount in the city's base against `platform.adjustments` (`city_id`, `dues_amount`, `dues_currency`, `dues_journal_id`; never ends, `blocks_dues`), so the dues balance is the block and the desk's payment lifts it. One open `until` or `indefinite` per account (`blocks_one_open`, `409 already_blocked`); one whose time passed is closed at its `until` with no admin when the next block starts. `POST /v1/admin/customers/{customerId}/block` (`customers.block`: OWN, OPS; `Idempotency-Key` kept on the row for good with the request's hash, `blocks_key`, so a dues block never charges twice; `422 idempotency_key_reused`) and `.../unblock` (`blockId`, the block in force the panel showed, and the note; no key: it names its target, so a repeat, or a block replaced since, is `409 not_blocked`; it ends only a duration or «حتى إشعار آخر»: dues go through the desk or «القيود التصحيحية», owner decision 2026-11-17) run under the customer's ordering lock (`customerOrderLock`), so placing reads them; audited `customer.block` and `customer.unblock`. `activeBlock(accountId, now, executor?)` is what the quote shows as `account_blocked` (the customer text as its `message`, `until`) and placing reads again under the lock, with the dues (a dues block charged since the quote refuses the order); `dues(cityId, accountId, executor?)` is what it shows as `dues_unpaid` (ordering's `customer-dues.ts` moved here). **The panel (`admin-customers.controller.ts`, `customers.read`: OWN, OPS, SUP, FIN; `404 unknown_city` for a city that is not one):** `POST /v1/admin/customers/search` (`{ cityId, q }`: a number in the desk's forms, `phoneOf`, through identity's `customerByPhone`, else a part of the first or last name, the latest 20) answers each customer's name, `maskedPhone` (`maskedPhone`: the country code and the last three digits, decided by us; the number is never answered) and `status` (`accountStatus`: `blocked`, `dues_unpaid`, `review_stop` or `active`, the first that applies); `GET /v1/admin/customers/{customerId}?cityId=` the profile: the details, the status, the block in force, the open review stop, the trust (D23: `trusted`, `delivered`, `trustedAfter`) and the fake orders from ordering's port (`useOrderFacts`, registered by `OrdersService` at start, since this module cannot import ordering), the late cancellations within `customer.late_cancel_window_days` and in all, the dues balance in the city (+ owes, − a credit) and the latest 20 blocks with the admins' names. **The app (`customer-account-state.controller.ts`):** `GET /v1/customer/account-state?latitude=&longitude=` (`@AppSignedIn()`) answers CU §5.5's banners: `reviewStop`, `block` (`until`, the reason's customer text), `dues` in the point's city and, with dues, the city's `office` for the sheet (AP §9.12.1); outside every city, no dues.

## Not yet

- Idempotency keys on the address commands (CU §15, §4.5): take the header with `@Idempotent()` and `@IdempotencyKey()` from `src/http/idempotency.ts` and keep the key with the address in its transaction, as `ordering.idempotency_keys` does; until then a lost answer retried adds a second address.
- A request limit on the public routes: the deploy slice's per-IP limits in nginx (architecture §15.5) must cover `/v1/customer/zone-votes` and `/v1/customer/restaurants`, whose cards each read the database and OSRM.
- Recognising the account's own number typed as another contact number (it then asks for a recipient).
- Home sections, banners, categories, search, favourites (CU §8.1, §8.3, §9.6) and the price report (§9.5): Phase 2 (owner decision 2026-11-06).
- One road distance per card: the OSRM `table` matrix replaces it when lists grow.
- Closing the account (§5.6, slice 2.25c); the profile's orders, tickets and codes, notes, the test account, «تغيير الرقم» (slice 2.25b); the device block (2.25d). A block reaches the app at its next read of the account state (on opening and on returning to it), not by real time (CU §5.4 «applies at once»: the server refuses at once). The name search scans `profiles` with `ilike` and reads each hit's status (up to 20): a trigram index (`pg_trgm`, a new extension in `db/bootstrap.sql`) and batched reads when the customers grow.
