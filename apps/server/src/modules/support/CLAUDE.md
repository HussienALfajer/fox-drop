# support module

Tickets «الدعم» (architecture §4.2; AP §9.9). Compensation and its approvals live in `marketing`. Slice 2.23a built manual tickets, their list, page, notes, assignee, status, SLA timers and «تذكرة عاجلة»; 2.23b resolving, closing, the resolution message, «سعر مختلف عن المحل» and the pickup dispute's ticket with «تصحيح في الحسابات»; 2.23c the money of a problem after delivery and «كود تعويض»; 2.23d «إعادة توصيل مجانية»; the apps' sources come with 2.24.

## Rules

- **What this module owns:** `support.tickets`, `support.ticket_numbers`, `support.ticket_notes` and `support.note_keys` (migration 0088; 0089 adds the price difference's restaurant, the pickup dispute's row, the resolution and the closing, with `CHECK`s tying them to «محلولة» and «مغلقة»; 0091 the problem's money, `tickets_money`; the application role writes them only through their column grants). Orders, parties and accounts are plain ids (architecture §4.3).
- **A ticket** names its party (`customer`, `restaurant` or `captain`; owner decision 2026-11-17), a type from that party's list in Content (`TICKET_TYPE_LISTS`: `customer_problem`, `merchant_ticket_type`, `captain_ticket_type`; AP Appendix B), kept as its code and label as chosen, what the party said (`description`; a type that asks for its text, «أخرى», takes it), and optionally an order with its number. Its number is sequential per city (`ticket_numbers`, taken in the opening's transaction), shown `T-…` (AP §4.8). `source` is `manual` or `pickup_dispute` until the apps' sources. Two types are fixed, outside the Content lists (`FIXED_TYPE_LABELS`): «سعر مختلف عن المحل» (`price_difference`, a customer's ticket naming `restaurant_id` and no order; `400 invalid_restaurant` otherwise; offered at the end of the customer's `types` and opened by hand until the app's report, owner decision 2026-11-17) and «خلاف على دفعة الاستلام» (`pickup_dispute`, opened only by ordering). `ticketKind` reads `general`, `price_difference` or `pickup_dispute` from the type.
- **The pure rules are `tickets.ts`** (`tickets.test.ts`): «عاجلة» while open on an active order, and a pickup dispute while open whatever its order (`isUrgent`); the resolutions each kind takes (`resolutionsOf`) and `resolutionProblem`: the responsible party required for every ticket but a price difference, which takes none (AP §9.9.3), the lines received with «تصحيح في الحسابات» only; `canClose` an open ticket; the SLA timer is chosen once at the opening and runs from it (owner decisions 2026-11-17): the first response (`support.first_response_active_min`) for a ticket opened on an active order, the resolution (`support.resolution_post_delivery_hours`) for every other; amber at 80%, breached at its end (`slaState`); the first response stops when the ticket leaves «جديدة», the resolution runs through «بانتظار رد» (`slaStop`); «جديدة» turns «قيد المعالجة» at the first assignment or note (`afterFirstHandling`); by hand only «بانتظار رد» and back (`canSetStatus`), «محلولة» only by resolving. The panel's `tickets.ts` repeats `slaState` on the server's clock.
- **Opening:** `POST /v1/admin/tickets` (no order: the party found by `parties/search`, a POST so a number stays out of the URL) and ordering's `POST /v1/admin/orders/{orderId}/tickets` (the party's id taken from the order, under the order's lock, so `order_active` is exactly the order's state then; `409 no_captain` for a captain's ticket without one), both `support.manage` and `Idempotency-Key`, kept on the ticket for good (`tickets_opened_by_key`) with the request's hash (`422 idempotency_key_reused`). `prepare` reads the type and the settings through the pool before the transaction; `openIn` runs in the caller's.
- **Changes:** a note (`Idempotency-Key`, `note_keys`; the application role can neither change nor delete a note), the assignee (a member holding `support.manage`, `AdminSessionsService.teamWith`; no key: it sets a value) and the status (no key: a repeat is `409 invalid_status_change`), each under the ticket's row lock with its audit line (`ticket.open`, `.note`, `.assign`, `.status`). A closed or resolved ticket takes no change (`409 ticket_not_open`).
- **What follows a change (`ticket-sync.ts`, E4):** in the change's transaction, `TicketSync.changed` syncs «تذكرة عاجلة» (`urgent_ticket`, critical, OWN, OPS and SUP, subject `ticket` with `ticketNumber` and `orderNumber`) and writes `ticket.changed` through `PlatformBroadcasts` to `ops:city.{id}` only (architecture §6.2). Ordering's `OrderAlerts.sync`, which runs under the order's lock after every transition, calls `TicketSync.orderEnded` once the order is no longer active: its tickets turn «عادية» (`order_active` false) and their alert closes (owner decision 2026-11-17). The worker gets `TicketSync` from `SupportWorkerModule`.
- **Resolving (AP §9.9.3; owner decisions 2026-11-17):** `POST /v1/admin/tickets/{ticketId}/resolve` (`resolution`, `responsible`, `message` up to 500 characters, `receivedLines` for «تصحيح في الحسابات»): a pickup dispute needs `support.resolve_pickup_dispute` (OWN, OPS, FIN), every other ticket `support.manage` (`managingPermission`; `403 forbidden`; the route takes either). In one transaction under the ticket's lock: «محلولة» with the resolution's columns, the timer stopped, the entries of a correction, the message's push to the ticket's party (`recipientOf`: the customer's account, the restaurant's owners, RA §16 #29, or the captain; templates `*.ticket_resolved`, data `{ target: 'ticket', ticketId }`, kept 24 hours) and the audit `ticket.resolve` with the message as its reason. `POST .../close` (`reason`; the same permission as resolving, so SUP cannot close a pickup dispute): «مغلقة» with an internal reason, no message and no entries, audited `ticket.close`. Neither takes a key: a repeat is `409 ticket_not_open`.
- **The pickup dispute (RA §8.10 item 8, AP §9.9.1):** ordering's dispute after the pickup calls `openPickupDisputeIn` in its transaction under the order's lock (timers read first with `preparePickupDispute`): the restaurant the party, the restaurant's user `opened_by`, the key `pickup_dispute:<dispute id>`, audited with the actor `merchant`; its `openedByName` is the restaurant's. Its alert is `urgent_dispute_ticket` («تذكرة عاجلة» for FIN too), the others' `urgent_ticket` (`TicketSync.changed`). Support cannot read orders, so ordering registers a `PickupDisputes` port at start (`usePickupDisputes`, `pickup-disputes.ts`; `PickupCorrections` in ordering): `facts` for the ticket page's `pickupDispute`, `preview` for `POST .../correction-preview` (`support.resolve_pickup_dispute`, writes nothing; `400 invalid_lines`, `no_difference`; `409 not_pickup_dispute`) and `correct`, called in the resolution's transaction, which posts the journals and the captain's push.
- **A problem after delivery (AP §8.10, §9.9.3, §6.4, docs/domain/10; slice 2.23c, owner decisions 2026-11-17):** «كود تعويض» (`compensation_code`) joins the general resolutions. `moneyProblem` (`tickets.ts`) is the pure rule: money (affected lines, `M`, a code) only on a customer's general ticket (`invalid_resolution`) with an order ordering knows (`order_required`); the affected items and `M` only once the order was delivered with the outcome normal (`not_delivered`); «مشكلة في المبلغ أو الباقي» (`WRONG_CHANGE_TYPE`, `amount_or_change`) takes `M` (`shortAmount`, required unless the customer is responsible, who takes none: `amount_required`; at most what the captain recorded as received, `receivedTotal`, else `invalid_value`, decided by us), never the restaurant as the party (docs/domain/10) and no items, its code is `M` itself (no value); every other type takes items and no `M`, its code a value and a reason from Content's `compensation` list (`value_required`); never a code with the customer responsible. Support cannot read orders, so ordering registers a `TicketOrders` port at start (`useTicketOrders`, `ticket-orders.ts`; `TicketMoney` in ordering): `facts` (null for an order it does not know) for the page's `order` (a customer's general ticket on an order: its lines by position, money, `deliveredNormally`, the captain's record at the door), `preview` for `POST .../money-preview` (`support.manage`, writes nothing; `400 invalid_affected` for a line not the order's, named twice or beyond its quantity) and `post`, in the resolution's transaction. Resolving checks everything before the transaction (the lines through `preview`, the code through marketing's `prepare`; any «كود تعويض» needs `operations.compensate`, `403 forbidden`), then under the ticket's lock: the order's money lock (`support.ticket_money:<orderId>`) with the earlier resolved tickets on the order, so one problem is paid once: the affected quantities of every ticket stay within each line (`invalid_affected`) and the wrong change is resolved with `M` once (`409 money_already_resolved`); the entries and their pushes (`post`), the code (`CompensationsService.requestIn` with the key `ticket:<id>`: issued within the resolver's cap, the wrong change's uncapped, else waiting for OWN while the ticket is resolved all the same, owner decision 2026-11-17; `409 no_base_currency`, `currency_changed` roll it all back), «محلولة» with `affected_lines` (position, quantity, name, value), `affected_value` (V), `short_amount` (M), `money_currency` and `compensation_id`, the message's push and the audit with them. The entries follow docs/domain/10 whatever the remedy (owner decision 2026-11-17), with the wrong change charged to the captain only with its code (in person: nothing).
- **«إعادة توصيل مجانية» (AP §9.9.3, docs/domain/10; slice 2.23d, owner decisions 2026-11-17):** `free_redelivery` joins the general resolutions (migration 0092 widens `tickets_resolution_values`). `moneyProblem` takes it on a customer's general ticket with an order delivered normally (`order_required`, `not_delivered`), the affected items (`affected_required` without any), never the wrong change, M, a code value or the customer responsible (`invalid_resolution`); `chargePay` (its captain's pay charged to the party) only with the restaurant or the captain responsible, and only by `operations.manage` (AP §9.9.3 «unless OPS charges it»; `403 forbidden`). Resolving reads `TicketOrders.prepareRedelivery` before the transaction (`409 captain_pay_unset`; `409 restaurant_closed` while the restaurant takes no orders, so it never rings no one and counts as unanswered; `409 emergency_stop`, checked again in the transaction; `409 no_base_currency` without the city's rates), then under the ticket's lock and the order's money lock (the affected quantities count with the earlier tickets' as for V) calls `redeliver`, which places the linked order through ordering (nothing is posted now: its money comes with its delivery), and keeps the affected lines and V on the ticket; the audit names `redeliveryOrderId`. `POST .../money-preview` takes `redelivery` and answers `redelivery` (the pickup payment, the commission and the merchant net its party bears) with no entries. The page's `redeliveries` lists the orders placed for the ticket (`TicketOrders.redeliveries`). **A re-delivery rejected, cancelled or failed** (owner decision 2026-11-17): ordering's `OrderAlerts.sync` calls `TicketSync.redeliveryEnded` under the order's lock once it ended so, only for the ticket's newest re-delivery (a late or repeated sync of one it replaced changes nothing): a ticket still resolved by `free_redelivery` returns to «قيد المعالجة» with its resolution, message and affected items cleared (so another remedy can take them), its alert synced and the audit `ticket.reopen` by the system; the SLA timer stays stopped and the customer is not told (both ours, to confirm with the owner).
- **Reads:** the list (`support.read`: OWN, OPS, SUP, FIN), newest first, the latest 200, filtered by status, type, assignee, source and a breached timer (`coalesce(sla_stopped_at, now) >= sla_due_at`); a ticket with its notes; `forOrder` for the order's detail and `ordersWithOpenTickets` for the board's «لها تذكرة», both called by ordering.
