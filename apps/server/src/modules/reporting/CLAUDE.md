# reporting module

Statements, reports and exports (architecture §4.2, §5.9). Slice 2.19g: «كشوف المطاعم» (AP §9.10.5) and the first Excel export; slice 2.20d: «التقرير اليومي» (AP §9.10.1); slice 2.22d: «كلفة التعويضات والخصومات» (AP §9.10.6). It reads other modules through their public services and owns only `reporting.exports`.

## Rules

- **A restaurant's statement for any period** (`admin-restaurant-statements.controller.ts`, `restaurants.statements`: OWN, FIN, AP §6.2 «Statements and settlement»): `GET /v1/admin/restaurant-statements/restaurants?cityId=` lists the city's restaurants by name; `GET /v1/admin/restaurant-statements?restaurantId=&from=&to=` answers the statement of the business days `from` to `to` (YYYY-MM-DD on the city's clock, both included: from the `finance.business_day_cutoff` of the first day to the one of the day after the last; decided by us, as the weekly statements count days), `404 unknown_restaurant`, `400 invalid_period` (the last day before the first, a day that does not exist, or longer than `PERIOD_MAX_DAYS`, 366). The statement is cashdesk's `MerchantPeriodService` (its `CashDeskPeriodModule`): the opening balance (the account before `from`) and the period's `merchant.account` entries read in one repeatable-read snapshot, through `buildStatement`, so the closing is their sum; each payment and payout carries its receipt. Money travels as strings of minor units, + the restaurant owes.
- **Excel exports (architecture §5.9):** `POST /v1/admin/restaurant-statements/exports` (`restaurants.statements`, `Idempotency-Key`) checks the period and the restaurant, then in one transaction records `reporting.exports` (pending), sends `QUEUES.exportBuild` and audits `export.create` (AP §5.4); `202` with the export. The key is unique per admin account (`exports_requester_key`): a repeat answers the same export, another body with the key is `422 idempotency_key_reused`. The worker's `ExportsWorker` (`ReportingWorkerModule`) writes it with ExcelJS (`statementWorkbook`: one right-to-left sheet, the header, the summary from the opening to the closing balance, every line; amounts as numbers in the base's major units, written from their digits by `majorOf`; times on the city's clock) to `private-docs` under `exports/<id>.xlsx` (`FilesService.putPrivate`), then marks it ready with `expires_at` 24 hours on (`EXPORT_KEEP_MS`) and sends `QUEUES.exportExpiry` for then, which removes the file (`deletePrivate`) and marks it `expired` whenever it runs (pg-boss starts it by the database's clock, so a worker clock behind never lets it complete without removing the file; the state reads `expired` from `expires_at` on anyway). Both jobs re-read the export and act only on its state, so a repeat changes nothing. `GET /v1/admin/exports/{exportId}` answers its state to its requester only (`404 unknown_export` for anyone else); asked with `download=true` while ready (the panel's click on «تنزيل»), it signs a GET on the panel's origin valid `EXPORT_URL_SECONDS` (5 minutes, `FilesService.privateDownloadUrl`, saved under the export's file name) and audits `export.download` (architecture §5.8: every view of a private file). Each kind is read with its own permission (`exportPermissions`).
- **«التقرير اليومي» (AP §9.10.1, §8.11; slice 2.20d; `daily-figures.ts`, `daily-report.service.ts`, `admin-daily-report.controller.ts`):** `GET /v1/admin/daily-report?cityId=&day=` (`finance.read`: OWN, FIN; `day` YYYY-MM-DD on the city's clock, today's business day when left out) answers the day from its `finance.business_day_cutoff` to the next day's, with the `COMPARED_DAYS` (6) days before (`previous`, the nearest first); `400 invalid_day` (a day that does not exist or has not started), `404 unknown_city`, `409 no_base_currency` (`DailyReportService.check`). Each day is `dailyFigures` (pure, `daily-figures.test.ts`) over the ledger's `cityTotals` of the day (platform side, by purpose, type, currency and rates), the order pay by order (`sourceTotals`, its distance part from `OrdersService.distancePay`, capped at the pay, as the captain's weekly statement), ordering's `deliveredTotals` (orders delivered in the day, test orders left out: count, GMV the customer totals, items value) and every custody and restaurant account at the day's end (`cityBalancesAt`). In the base: the delivery fees (`platform.revenue_delivery`), the discounts by funder (`platform.discount_cost`; `merchant.discount_share` on the restaurants), the commission earned (`platform.revenue_commission`), the captain pay by part from `captain.earnings` whoever bears it (base, distance, waiting, peak, bonuses), the compensation, operating and test costs, rounding and FX and the manual corrections (+ a gain), and the margin, minus the sum of every platform account of AP §8.11 but the cash and the order clearing; an amount in another currency converts at its journal's rate version, else at the rate in force at the day's end (`FxService.valueAt`). Per currency, in that currency: the custody the day's orders created (collect, change, pickup payment, pickup correction), handed in, reimbursed, open at the end (what captains hold, and what the office owes them), the earnings paid out and the restaurants' payments at the desk. The restaurants: the commission and restaurant-funded discount posted (a failed order's commission included), and what they owe and are owed at the end. Today's report adds `now`: the captains blocked (any block of `CaptainsService.balances`) and the overdue restaurants (`MerchantStatementsService.restaurants`, through `CashDeskPeriodModule`); null for a past day (decided by us: no ledger entry dates them). Codes and compensation slices not built show zero. `POST /v1/admin/daily-report/exports` (`finance.read`, `Idempotency-Key`, `{ cityId, day }`) records the `daily_report` export as the statement's (`ExportsService.record`); the worker writes `dailyReportWorkbook` (one right-to-left sheet: the earned figures with the days before as columns, the cash per currency, today's state) as the report stands then. Reading an export needs its kind's permission (`exportPermissions`), so `GET /v1/admin/exports/{exportId}` takes either and a user who lost it gets `404 unknown_export`.
- **«كلفة التعويضات والخصومات» (AP §9.10.6, §8.11; slice 2.22d; `cost-report.ts`, `cost-report.service.ts`, `admin-cost-report.controller.ts`):** `GET /v1/admin/cost-report?cityId=&from=&to=` (`finance.read`: OWN, FIN) answers the business days `from` to `to` as a statement's period counts them (both or neither; the last `DEFAULT_PERIOD_DAYS`, 7, ending today when left out; decided by us); `400 invalid_period` (one day without the other, a day that does not exist, the last before the first, longer than 366 days, or ending after today), `404 unknown_city`, `409 no_base_currency`, `rate_missing`. `costReport` (pure, `cost-report.test.ts`) splits the ledger's `sourceCosts` of the period on `platform.discount_cost`, `platform.compensation_cost` and `platform.operating_cost` (`costAccounts`; a test order's `platform.test_cost` stays out), in the base (an amount of an order placed in an earlier base at the rate in force at the period's end, `FxService.valueAt`), with ordering's `costOrders` and marketing's `codeOrigins`: the totals; the compensation by line (`kind` `reason` with the code of «تعويض الزبون» and the label as chosen, `late_delivery` for the late codes, `other` for a cost with no personal code the report can read behind it; what a ticket's remedy posts there is slice 2.23's to place); the operating cost by its cause and by the party operations named (ordering's `operatingCostCause`); the discounts by code (the codes of «أكواد الخصم»; a personal code is no line there); and by restaurant (a discount and an operating cost under the order's restaurant, a compensation under the restaurant of the order compensated; null for an entry without an order the report can read). Each split's `orders` counts the orders behind its entries, and a line that cancels out is left out. An outcome's cost counts under the outcome and the party that posted its journal, so «تغيير النتيجة» in a later period shows there as a negative under the outcome replaced and leaves the earlier period's report as it was. A compensation costs when its code is used (docs/domain/11), never when it is issued (to confirm with the owner). `POST /v1/admin/cost-report/exports` (`finance.read`, `Idempotency-Key`, `{ cityId, from, to }`) records the `cost_report` export (migration 0087 widens `exports_kind_values`); the worker writes `costReportWorkbook` (one right-to-left sheet: the header, the totals, then each split) as the report stands then.
- **The file's words** are `restaurantStatementLabels` in the content module (`export-labels.ts`), the panel's words; its test keeps the exceptions' labels equal to the panel's `restaurantSettlement.cause`, `dailyReportLabels.line` equal to the panel's `dailyReport.line`, and every word of `costReportLabels` the panel shows equal to its `costReport`.

## Not yet

- A failed export stays pending while pg-boss retries it (`QUEUE_OPTIONS`); the panel gives up after 10 minutes. No `failed` state yet.
- An order with no commission and no restaurant-funded discount in the period (a restaurant at 0%, a test order) has no line and no items value, as on the weekly statement: the lines come from the restaurant's account, not from the pickups (to confirm with the owner).
- `private-docs` is not encrypted at rest (Garage offers only SSE-C, which a signed browser download cannot carry). The exports live 24 hours; the captain documents (2.34) decide the encryption.
- A personal code used on an order placed before slice 2.22b was booked to `platform.discount_cost`, so it shows among the discounts by code (staging only). The cost report's reads are not one snapshot. The cost report reads the ledger's entries of the whole period and each order behind them at every read (fine for Azaz); it has no end-to-end test with real orders (the split is tested pure, each module's read against its database, the route with entries no order stands behind); the restaurant's part of a code's discount and the codes issued but not used are not in it. AP §9.10.6 says «printable» only of the statements, so the report has no print layout.
- The other exports of AP §9.17, and exports with phone numbers (`exports.phone_numbers`, AP §5.4).
