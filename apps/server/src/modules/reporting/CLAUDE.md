# reporting module

Statements and exports (architecture §4.2, §5.9). Slice 2.19g: «كشوف المطاعم» (AP §9.10.5) and the first Excel export. It reads other modules through their public services and owns only `reporting.exports`.

## Rules

- **A restaurant's statement for any period** (`admin-restaurant-statements.controller.ts`, `restaurants.statements`: OWN, FIN, AP §6.2 «Statements and settlement»): `GET /v1/admin/restaurant-statements/restaurants?cityId=` lists the city's restaurants by name; `GET /v1/admin/restaurant-statements?restaurantId=&from=&to=` answers the statement of the business days `from` to `to` (YYYY-MM-DD on the city's clock, both included: from the `finance.business_day_cutoff` of the first day to the one of the day after the last; decided by us, as the weekly statements count days), `404 unknown_restaurant`, `400 invalid_period` (the last day before the first, a day that does not exist, or longer than `PERIOD_MAX_DAYS`, 366). The statement is cashdesk's `MerchantPeriodService` (its `CashDeskPeriodModule`): the opening balance (the account before `from`) and the period's `merchant.account` entries read in one repeatable-read snapshot, through `buildStatement`, so the closing is their sum; each payment and payout carries its receipt. Money travels as strings of minor units, + the restaurant owes.
- **Excel exports (architecture §5.9):** `POST /v1/admin/restaurant-statements/exports` (`restaurants.statements`, `Idempotency-Key`) checks the period and the restaurant, then in one transaction records `reporting.exports` (pending), sends `QUEUES.exportBuild` and audits `export.create` (AP §5.4); `202` with the export. The key is unique per admin account (`exports_requester_key`): a repeat answers the same export, another body with the key is `422 idempotency_key_reused`. The worker's `ExportsWorker` (`ReportingWorkerModule`) writes it with ExcelJS (`statementWorkbook`: one right-to-left sheet, the header, the summary from the opening to the closing balance, every line; amounts as numbers in the base's major units, written from their digits by `majorOf`; times on the city's clock) to `private-docs` under `exports/<id>.xlsx` (`FilesService.putPrivate`), then marks it ready with `expires_at` 24 hours on (`EXPORT_KEEP_MS`) and sends `QUEUES.exportExpiry` for then, which removes the file (`deletePrivate`) and marks it `expired`. Both jobs re-read the export and act only on its state, so a repeat changes nothing. `GET /v1/admin/exports/{exportId}` answers its state to its requester only (`404 unknown_export` for anyone else); while ready it signs a GET on the panel's origin valid `EXPORT_URL_SECONDS` (5 minutes, `FilesService.privateDownloadUrl`, saved under the export's file name) and audits `export.download` (architecture §5.8: every view of a private file). The only kind so far is the restaurant statement, so the route asks its permission; a new kind checks its own.
- **The file's words** are `restaurantStatementLabels` in the content module (`export-labels.ts`), the panel's words; its test keeps the exceptions' labels equal to the panel's `restaurantSettlement.cause`.
