# dispatch module

Eligibility, ranking, offers, assignments and handovers (architecture §4.2). So far manual dispatch (D6, the launch mode): operations' offers and direct assignments, the captain's answer and the offer timer (AP §9.3.2, §9.12.6, §8.3; CA §8, §15; owner decisions 2026-11-06).

## Rules

- **What this module owns:** the offers (`dispatch.offers`) and the keys of its commands (`dispatch.idempotency_keys`). The order, its captain and its captain track (AP §8.2) are `ordering`'s: every change goes through `OrderAssignments` from `ordering/index.ts` (`find`, `lock`, `assignable`, `change`), which writes the order, its event and its outbox row in this module's transaction. Captains' state, eligibility and pay come from `CaptainsService`; the restaurant from `MerchantsService`; distances from `GeoService`.
- **Locks:** a command locks the order first (`OrderAssignments.lock`), then the offer (`FOR UPDATE`), then the captain (`CaptainsService.assignmentLock`, which reads the city's rules before the transaction and returns the function that locks the row in it). Going offline locks the captain too and is refused with an active order, so an assignment and going offline never cross. Slow reads (the candidates, the road distance, the settings) run before the transaction; pg-boss connects (`jobs.ready()`) before any lock.
- **Assign (AP §9.3.2 «تعيين كابتن»):** `POST /v1/admin/orders/{orderId}/assign` (`{ captainId }`, `operations.manage`: OWN and OPS, with `Idempotency-Key`). The order must be `accepted` or `ready` with no captain and no open offer (`409 order_not_assignable`; an order still `placed` waits for the restaurant, CA §8.1). The captain must be eligible now (`CaptainsService.candidates`): `409 captain_not_eligible` with its reasons (`busy`, `cash_limit`, `closed_due`, `simulated_location`, `out_of_range`, `vehicle_not_allowed`, or `offline` when not listed), checked again under the captain's lock, and with no other open offer (`409 captain_has_offer`, CA §8.1, also a unique partial index). With `dispatch.manual_assign_requires_accept` (true) the captain gets an offer (`order.offered`); without it the order is the captain's at once (`order.assigned`, actor the admin). Audited as `order.assign_captain`. The answer is the order's dispatch state: its status, version, captain track, captain and open offer.
- **The offer (CA §8.2):** kept with what it showed when it opened: the road distance from the captain's live position to the restaurant for its vehicle (`GeoService.distance`, owner decision 2026-11-06) and the order pay of the pay table the order kept for the trip (`CaptainsService.orderPay`, before any waiting). `GET /v1/captain/offers/current` answers the open offer still in time, or null: the pay, the restaurant (name, location, distance), «الحي» and the trip, «تدفع للمطعم» (the items value, D26), «تقبض من الزبون» (the total), the preparation («جاهز», or the time it should be) and `fromOperations` («تعيين من العمليات»). Never the customer's name, phone or exact address before acceptance (C2).
- **The answer (CA §8.3, §15):** `POST /v1/captain/offers/{offerId}/accept` and `/decline` (`@AppSignedIn()` with the captain token, `Idempotency-Key`). Another captain's or an unknown offer is `404 unknown_offer`; one no longer open or past `expires_at` is `409 offer_unavailable` («لم يعد العرض متاحاً»). Accepting needs the order still waiting for a captain and the captain still online without an active order; it closes the offer and assigns the order (`order.assigned`, actor the captain), so the captain is busy. Declining needs no reason (`order.offer_closed`, reason `declined`).
- **The timer (AP §8.3):** each offer sends its `QUEUES.offerExpiry` job in its transaction for `dispatch.offer_timeout_sec` (45 s) later; the worker's `OfferTimersWorker` runs `DispatchService.expire`, which acts only on an offer still open: `expired` («انتهت المهلة»), `order.offer_closed` with reason `expired`, actor `system`. A closed offer returns the order to «بحاجة لتعيين» (track `searching`) once its search has started, or to no track before (docs/domain/03).
- **C3 (AP §9.7.6):** after an expiry, when the captain's last `captain.offer_expiry_offline_count` (3) answered offers all expired, the captain goes offline (`CaptainsService.offlineForUnansweredOffers`, end reason `offers_expired`), never with an active order. A decline or an acceptance breaks the run.
- **Keys (architecture §5.3):** `idempotency_keys` per account (the admin's, or the captain's app account), with a hash of the command, the order and the offer, written in the command's transaction; a repeat within 24 hours answers the order's state or the offer, another command with the key `422 idempotency_key_reused`.
- **Processes:** the API serves the routes (`DispatchModule`); the worker runs the offer timers (`DispatchWorkerModule`). The `dispatcher` role's loop comes with automatic dispatch.
