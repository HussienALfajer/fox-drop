# dispatch module

Eligibility, ranking, offers, assignments and handovers (architecture §4.2). So far manual dispatch (D6, the launch mode): operations' offers and direct assignments, the captain's answer and the offer timer (AP §9.3.2, §9.12.6, §8.3; CA §8, §15; owner decisions 2026-11-06).

## Rules

- **What this module owns:** the offers (`dispatch.offers`) and the keys of its commands (`dispatch.idempotency_keys`). The order, its captain and its captain track (AP §8.2) are `ordering`'s: every change goes through `OrderAssignments` from `ordering/index.ts` (`find`, `lock`, `assignable`, `change`), which writes the order, its event and its outbox row in this module's transaction. Captains' state, eligibility and pay come from `CaptainsService`; the restaurant from `MerchantsService`; distances from `GeoService`.
- **Locks:** a command locks the order first (`OrderAssignments.lock`), then the offer (`FOR UPDATE`), then the captain (`CaptainsService.assignmentLock`, which reads the city's rules before the transaction and returns the function that locks the row in it). Going offline locks the captain too and is refused with an active order, so an assignment and going offline never cross. Slow reads (the candidates, the road distance, the settings) run before the transaction; pg-boss connects (`jobs.ready()`) before any lock.
- **Assign (AP §9.3.2 «تعيين كابتن»):** `POST /v1/admin/orders/{orderId}/assign` (`{ captainId }`, `operations.manage`: OWN and OPS, with `Idempotency-Key`). The order must be `accepted` or `ready` with no captain and no open offer (`409 order_not_assignable`; an order still `placed` waits for the restaurant, CA §8.1). The captain must be eligible now (`CaptainsService.candidates`): `409 captain_not_eligible` with its reasons (`busy`, `cash_limit`, `closed_due`, `simulated_location`, `out_of_range`, `vehicle_not_allowed`, or `offline` when not listed), checked again under the captain's lock, and with no other open offer (`409 captain_has_offer`, CA §8.1, also a unique partial index). With `dispatch.manual_assign_requires_accept` (true) the captain gets an offer (`order.offered`); without it the order is the captain's at once (`order.assigned`, actor the admin). Audited as `order.assign_captain`. The answer is the order's dispatch state: its status, version, captain track, captain and open offer.
- **The assignment picker (AP §9.3.2):** `GET /v1/admin/orders/{orderId}/captains` (`operations.manage`) lists every online captain of the city nearest the restaurant first (`CaptainsService.candidates`), eligible or greyed out with its reasons, each with the distance and ETA to the restaurant (by road for its vehicle at `order.eta_speed_kmh` within the search radius, the straight line and no ETA beyond it), its orders assigned since the business day began (`finance.business_day_cutoff`), its cash-limit usage and its acceptance rate over its last `captain.acceptance_window_offers` answered offers (CA §11.1, null without one).
- **The offer (CA §8.2):** kept with what it showed when it opened: the road distance from the captain's live position to the restaurant for its vehicle (`GeoService.distance`, owner decision 2026-11-06) and the order pay of the pay table the order kept for the trip (`CaptainsService.orderPay`, before any waiting). `GET /v1/captain/offers/current` answers the open offer still in time, or null: the pay, the restaurant (name, location, distance), «الحي» and the trip, «تدفع للمطعم» (the items value, D26), «تقبض من الزبون» (the total), the preparation («جاهز», or the time it should be) and `fromOperations` («تعيين من العمليات»). Never the customer's name, phone or exact address before acceptance (C2).
- **The answer (CA §8.3, §15):** `POST /v1/captain/offers/{offerId}/accept` and `/decline` (`@AppSignedIn()` with the captain token, `Idempotency-Key`). Another captain's or an unknown offer is `404 unknown_offer`; one no longer open or past `expires_at` is `409 offer_unavailable` («لم يعد العرض متاحاً»). Accepting needs the order still waiting for a captain and the captain still online without an active order; it closes the offer and assigns the order (`order.assigned`, actor the captain), so the captain is busy. Declining needs no reason (`order.offer_closed`, reason `declined`).
- **An offer past its time:** the order's next assignment expires it first, under the order's lock (through the same `close` as the timer), so a late worker never holds the order; the captain can no longer accept it, and the dispatch state shows it no more. A captain's own late offer still refuses a new one (`captain_has_offer`) until the timer runs, since closing it would lock another order.
- **Withdrawn (CA §7.5):** when the captain's online period ends (going offline, a suspension, a termination), `CaptainsService.onWentOffline` calls `withdraw`: the open offer becomes `withdrawn` and the order returns to «بحاجة لتعيين» at once.
- **Operations' cancellation (AP §9.3.2, CA §9.10; slice 2.8b):** `onModuleInit` registers `OrderAssignments.onCancel`: in the cancellation's transaction, under the order's lock and before its own transition, the order's open offer is locked and closed as `withdrawn` (`order.offer_closed`, actor the admin), silently; the app shows «لم يعد العرض متاحاً». An offered order cancelled where no dispatch registered the hook fails rather than leave its offer open.
- **The timer (AP §8.3):** each offer sends its `QUEUES.offerExpiry` job in its transaction for `dispatch.offer_timeout_sec` (45 s) later; the worker's `OfferTimersWorker` runs `DispatchService.expire`, which acts only on an offer still open: `expired` («انتهت المهلة»), `order.offer_closed` with reason `expired`, actor `system`. A closed offer returns the order to «بحاجة لتعيين» (track `searching`) once its search has started, or to no track before (docs/domain/03). A job that starts before `expires_at` by the database's clock throws, so the queue tries again and the captain keeps the whole time.
- **C3 (AP §9.7.6, CA §7.4):** after an expiry, when the captain's last `captain.offer_expiry_offline_count` (3) answered offers of the current online period all expired, the captain goes offline (`CaptainsService.offlineForUnansweredOffers`, end reason `offers_expired`), never with an active order. A decline, an acceptance or going offline starts the run again. A retried job checks C3 again for an offer already expired, so a failure after the expiry never loses it.
- **Keys (architecture §5.3):** `idempotency_keys` per account (the admin's, or the captain's app account), with a hash of the command, the order and the offer, written in the command's transaction; a repeat within 24 hours answers the order's state or the offer, another command with the key `422 idempotency_key_reused`, also when two commands with one key run at once (the second's insert finds the key and rolls back). The captain's answers record its app installation on the order's event (`actor_device`, CA §15).
- **Grants:** the application role may change only `status` and `responded_at` of an offer (AP §9.7.4: every offer and its answer stay as logged).
- **Processes:** the API serves the routes (`DispatchModule`); the worker runs the offer timers (`DispatchWorkerModule`). The `dispatcher` role's loop comes with automatic dispatch.

- The captain's location on the offer answers (CA §15; the order commands in `ordering` record it since slice 1.12), the C3 message «لم ترد على 3 عروض متتالية» on the captain's channel (CA §7.4, with push), the open offer and the captain's phone, live position and cash in the order detail (AP §9.3.1), reading `dispatch.no_captain_alert_min` when the search starts rather than at acceptance.
- `offers` is not partitioned; partition it by month with the orders before its volume matters.
