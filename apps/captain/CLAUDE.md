# apps/captain

The captain app «تطبيق الكابتن» (Android, iOS; architecture §11, §14). It follows the rules of `apps/customer/CLAUDE.md`.

## The work cycle (slice 1.14g; CA §4.3–4.6, §7–§9, §14–§15)

- **Layout:** `lib/src/work/`. `work.dart` holds the state and its pure rules (the step, the open and refused pickup requests) and the feed; `location.dart` the location; `commands.dart` the commands and their refusals; `door_payment.dart` the door payment's pure rules; `alerts.dart` the ring and the chimes; `texts.dart` every string mapping; `offer_sheet.dart`, `order_view.dart`, `payment_form.dart` and `reason_sheet.dart` only show that state and call it (the design stop). `home_screen.dart` shows the availability or the active order, under `WorkLayer`: the offline bar, the offer, the alerts, the hand-in reminder and the summary.
- **State:** `workFeedProvider` is a `LiveFeed` (`packages/core_realtime/CLAUDE.md`) over `captain:{captain_id}` (the id from `GET /v1/captain/auth/session`) whose fetch reads the availability, the open offer and the active order together, so the screens never show one newer than the others; the versions are the active order's. The screens read `workProvider` and never patch it; every command's answer refreshes it.
- **Location (CA §4.3; owner decision 2026-11-07):** `geolocator` for Phase 1, not `flutter_background_geolocation`, whose Android licence waits for the launch. `LocationTracker` runs while the state is online in any way (available, busy, no offers), started and stopped by `WorkLayer` from the state, never while offline. On Android its position stream runs geolocator's foreground service of type `location` with the permanent notification «أنت متصل — FOX DROP يتابع موقعك لتصلك العروض». Points carry the server clock (`ServerClock.offset`), queue in memory (a day at most) and go every 10 seconds, at most 360 per call, oldest first; no answer or a 5xx keeps them, `409 not_online` or a `400` drops the batch. The latest point feeds the arrival radii and every command's fix, taken again at each retry (a fresh position when it is older than 20 seconds). Going online asks for the location permission and a position first, then sends its mock flag (`isMocked`); that position is kept as the latest, never queued, since the captain is still offline. `start` asks for the location access itself before tracking, since a phone signed in while the captain is online (a released device, CA §4.9) never went online on it; without access, or when the stream fails, it stays stopped and the next online state tries again. A session that ends (sign-out, another device, a released device) resets the tracker: no more points, the queue dropped, the foreground service stopped. `WorkLayer` ignores the loading states, which still carry the last online state.
- **Commands (CA §4.6, §15):** `Commands` runs one command at a time per subject (`availability`, the offer's id, the order's id), shown as «جارٍ الإرسال…». As in the restaurant app, the `Idempotency-Key` belongs to the command and its body (the fix is left out, as the server's hash does), and a call without an answer, a 5xx, a 408 or a 429 is sent again with the same key after a backoff. `refusal` maps every refusal of CA §15 to a `Notice`, which `texts.dart` explains. The answers of going offline (the hand-in) and delivery (the summary) are kept in `handInProvider` and `summaryProvider` until closed.
- **The offer (CA §8; owner decision 2026-11-07):** it shows and rings while it is open, before its `expiresAt` on the server clock and not answered on this phone (`ringingOffer`), checked every second; past its time the feed is fetched again, so a lost event never leaves it on. The ring is the restaurant app's `PhoneRinger` (the phone's alarm sound on the alarm stream, started again every 10 seconds), and the screen stays on while online (`wakelock_plus`). Back does not leave the app while an offer shows.
- **Time:** every countdown and «منذ» watches `tickProvider` (once a second) and reads `serverClockProvider`; never `DateTime.now()`.
- **Alerts (CA §14):** `alertStep` compares two states the server answered: the offer gone before its time («لم يعد العرض متاحاً», silent) or after it («انتهى العرض»), an order not accepted on this phone («عيّنتك العمليات على طلب»), ready, the pickup confirmed (by operations or not), refused, and disputed after pickup, each with a chime unless silent. Thirty seconds without the channel chime once.
- **Cancelled by operations (slice 2.8b; CA §9.10, §14 #11):** the active read's `cancelled` (the latest order of this captain operations cancelled, not yet seen, within 12 hours) is `Work.cancelled`. `alertStep` raises `OrderCancelled` when a record appears that the previous state did not have, which plays `Ringer.cancellation()` (the notification sound in `PhoneRinger` until the bundled cancellation sound, the design stop) and no message; a record already waiting when the app opens shows silently. `_CancelledCard` covers home with «أُلغيت المهمة: {السبب}» (the reason's label), the cash line (`cashText`: `returned` «استرجع المبلغ من المطعم», `not_returned` «لا تسترجع المبلغ، يُخصم من المطعم في التسوية», otherwise none), «تعويض: {المبلغ}» when compensated, and «حسناً», which posts `acknowledge-cancellation` (no `Idempotency-Key` and no location: it only records once that the captain saw it); the availability shows under it, and an offer over it.
- **Taken from the captain (slice 2.12a; CA §9.10, §14 #11, AP §9.3.2):** the active read's `withdrawn` (the latest withdrawal from this captain, not yet seen, within 12 hours) is `Work.withdrawn`. `alertStep` raises `OrderWithdrawn` when a withdrawal appears that the previous state did not have (another order, or the same order taken again), which plays `Ringer.cancellation()` and no message; `_WithdrawnCard` covers home with «سُحب منك الطلب: {السبب}» (the reason's label), the cash line (`withdrawnCashText`, the cancellation's two texts) and «حسناً», which posts `acknowledge-withdrawal` (no key, no location).
- **An outcome set by operations (slice 2.11a; CA §9.10, §9.12):** the active read's `ended` (the latest order of this captain operations ended with an outcome, not yet seen, within 12 hours) is `Work.ended`. `alertStep` raises `OutcomeSet` when a record appears, which chimes with no message; `_EndedCard` covers home with «أنهت العمليات الطلب: {النتيجة}.» (`outcomeText`, AP Appendix C), where the food goes (`foodText`: «الطعام يبقى معك», «أعِد الطعام إلى المطعم», «أتلف الطعام»), «أجرك من الطلب: {المبلغ}» or, with the captain responsible, «خُصم من مستحقاتك {المبلغ}: {النتيجة}», and «حسناً», which posts `acknowledge-outcome` (no key, no location). **An amount to collect (slice 2.11b, K9):** while the active order's `outcomeToCollect` is set, `OrderView` replaces the step with «بانتظار تسجيل التحصيل», «أنهت العمليات الطلب: {النتيجة}. اقبض {المبلغ}.» and `PaymentForm` with `outcomeAmount` and `outcomeEquivalents` (the amount in the other accepted currencies, which the server computes from the order's snapshot), whose «تم التسليم» posts `Commands.collectOutcome` (`outcome-payment` with the latest fix when there is one, no radius) and opens the summary; `alertStep` chimes `OutcomeToCollect` when it appears. A payment operations record instead ends the order with the outcome card.
- **The handover after the pickup (slice 2.12b; CA §9.10.1, §14 #11, K17):** an offer whose `handover` is set shows «استلام من كابتن», «من الكابتن {الاسم} — {المسافة}» in place of the restaurant and «لا تدفع شيئاً» in place of the pickup payment. The active order's `handover` replaces the steps: `receiving` (the second captain, `receiving()`) is step 1 «استلام من كابتن» to the first captain's latest point, with «ابدأ الملاحة», «اتصال بالكابتن», «لا تدفع شيئاً», the amount to collect and «الحي», and «استلمت الطلب من الكابتن {الاسم}» enabled within `radii.customerMetres` of that point (`Commands.receiveHandover`, `receive-handover` with the fix; `too_far_from_captain` is `TooFarFromCaptain`); `giving` (the order's captain, `giving()`) is the waiting screen «سيستلم الكابتن {الاسم} الطلب منك. انتظره في موقعك.» with the second captain's distance and «اتصال بالكابتن» once assigned (while still offered: «تبحث العمليات عن كابتن يستلم الطلب منك. انتظر في موقعك.»), which `alertStep` chimes once (`HandoverStarted`). While a handover is open both screens read the order again every 15 seconds, so the other captain's position stays live. After the receipt the active read's `handedOver` is `Work.handedOver`: `OrderHandedOver` plays `Ringer.cancellation()` and `_HandedOverCard` shows «سلّمت الطلب للكابتن {الاسم}» with «حسناً» (`acknowledge-handover`, no key, no location); the first captain is offline then (K17).
- **The steps (CA §9.1–9.8):** the arrival buttons use the radii the active order sends (`radii`, the settings `captain.merchant_arrival_radius_m` and `captain.customer_radius_m`) against the latest point by the straight line, as the server measures; worse than 100 m the restaurant arrival waits for operations (K3). The item check resets when the restaurant answers «الطلب غير مكتمل». The pickup sends the proposed lines (the base currency only in Phase 1). «وصلت» and «تم التسليم» ask for the away reason first when the phone is beyond the radius, and again when the server measures it so; a reason given at the arrival covers the delivery (K4). The door payment keeps the amount due it showed, and says «تغيّر المبلغ المطلوب إلى …» when the order's changes. It proposes the change only for money received in the base currency, which needs no rate (CA §9.7 item 3: the change is in the base currency); the server checks every other line with the order's rates. A shortfall is the server's to find (a gap within half the largest rounding unit is rounding, AP §8.6): its `shortfall_reason_required` brings the reason list, and «تم التسليم» waits for a reason.
- **Tests:** `work_test.dart` proves the pure rules (the door payment, the refusals, the alerts, the ring, the location queue); `home_test.dart` drives the cycle from going online to the summary with `TestApp` (`test/fixtures.dart`: the JSON as the server sends it, `FakeServer`, `FakeTransport`, `FakeLocation`, `FakeRinger`, a server clock the test moves with `advance`). Widget tests wait with `settle` (the home's one-second tick never lets `pumpAndSettle` finish), tap below the fold with `tapVisible` and end with `done`.

## Last-order mode (slice 2.13a; CA §7.3, §7.5, §7.6, K13, K18)

- During an active order `_LastOrderBar` above the order replaces «متصل» with «لا أريد طلبات جديدة» (`Commands.setLastOrder`, `POST /v1/captain/availability/last-order`), «ستصبح غير متصل بعد هذا الطلب.» while on; a mode operations, a suspension or a termination set shows its reason instead (`lastOrderText`), with no switch, and `last_order_not_yours` says so. `workFeedProvider` reads the state again on `captain.availability_changed` (a publication with no order version, which `LiveFeed` ignores), and `handInAfterLastOrder` opens the hand-in card when a last order took the captain offline holding more than `custody.remitTolerance`. The force offline's push (CA §14 #26) shows as a notification.

## «مشكلة» and «لا أستطيع إكمال الطلب» (slice 2.13b; CA §9.11, C5, K17)

- `ProblemBar` (`work/problem_sheet.dart`) sits above every step of the active order, under `_LastOrderBar`: «مشكلة» opens a sheet with «اتصال بالعمليات» and «واتساب العمليات» (the support numbers, `captainAuth.contact`; a missing one hides its button) and, while no request is open, «لا أستطيع إكمال الطلب», which picks a reason of Content's `captain_cannot_complete` list (`CaptainContent.cannotComplete`, «أخرى» with its text) and posts `Commands.cannotComplete` (`cannot-complete` with the fix). While the active order's `cannotComplete` is set the bar shows «بانتظار العمليات», the reason's label and «تراجع» (`Commands.withdrawCannotComplete`); every step stays open (owner decision 2026-11-11). When operations take the order the app hears the withdrawal or the handover as before, and `captain.availability_changed` reads the state: the captain is offline (K17). The report with a quick reason waits for slice 2.24.

## «طوارئ» (slice 2.13c; CA §9.11, C15, K12, K17, CA §4.5 check 8)

- `EmergencyButton` (`work/emergency_button.dart`) sits under `ProblemBar` on every step of the active order: red (the spec's colour, otherwise unstyled), held `emergencyHold` (2 seconds, K12) with its progress ring; a shorter press does nothing. Held, it calls operations (`captainAuth.contact`'s phone) through `operationsCallerProvider` (`work/call.dart`) and, for the order's own captain while none is open, posts `Commands.emergency` (`emergency` with the latest fix whatever its age, a new one within 3 seconds, or null: the server never refuses it), on its own subject so a step being sent never holds it back. The operations number is kept on the phone (`captainAuth.store`, `operationsPhoneKey`) at every fetch, so a press without signal still calls; with no number at all it says «لم يصل رقم العمليات إلى هاتفك بعد…» (`NoOperationsNumber`) and still sends. While the active order's `emergency` is set it shows «أُبلغت العمليات بالطوارئ وموقعك. ابقَ في مكان آمن.» (or, when `located` is false, «…ولم يصل موقعك…») and another press only calls: it cannot be withdrawn (owner decision 2026-11-11). When operations take the order the app hears it as before and the captain is offline (K17).
- **The call:** `PhoneCaller` calls through `MainActivity`'s `com.foxdrop.captain/call` channel: `ACTION_CALL` with `CALL_PHONE`, else `ACTION_DIAL` with the number; off Android (and when the channel fails) it opens `tel:`, where iOS asks to confirm. `Commands.goOnline` asks for the call permission after the notification permission (`askCallPermission`, permission_handler's `Permission.phone`), never blocking, as check 8 of the device check until it exists. Tests give `FakeCaller` (`caller`: `prepared`, `calls`).

## The customer not answering (slice 2.13d; CA §9.9, §14 #13, #13a; owner decisions 2026-11-11)

- `DoorPanel` (`work/door.dart`) sits in step 4 between the customer block and the payment, while the active order's `noAnswer` is set (the server gives it at the door only): «تنبيه الزبون» enabled once the server's `nextPingAt` passed (`Commands.pingCustomer`, `ping-customer` with the fix), «التنبيهات المتبقية: {العدد}», «يمكنك التنبيه مرة أخرى الساعة {الساعة}» until then; the customer's latest answer («الزبون رأى التنبيه وهو قادم» or «الزبون يتواصل مع المستلم», `pingAnswerText`); from `reportableAt` «الزبون لا يستجيب» (`Commands.reportNoAnswer`, `customer-not-answering`), before it «يظهر «الزبون لا يستجيب» الساعة {الساعة}»; then «العمليات تراجع الطلب — انتظر تعليماتهم» until operations' «انتظر», whose message shows as a card «العمليات: انتظر الزبون. {الرسالة}» while the button comes back at the next `reportableAt`. Every time is the server's and the panel watches `tickProvider`. `alertStep` chimes `CustomerAnswered` when a newer answer appears and `WaitForCustomer` when a wait does, each with its message. The payment stays below: the customer may appear meanwhile (CA §9.9 item 5); the outcome is operations' «تحديد النتيجة», which the app shows as before.

## Pickup currencies (slice 2.14a; CA §10.5, C8, RA §12.7; owner decisions 2026-11-11)

- **«الإعدادات»:** `lib/src/settings/`, reached from the home's app bar (`/settings`); for now «أقصى مبلغ أحمله» and «العملات التي أحملها», which grow with the captain's settings (CA §12). `carriedCurrenciesProvider` reads and sets `GET`/`PUT /v1/captain/settings/carried-currencies`; a switch per currency of the city with its Arabic name from the server, the base marked «العملة الأساسية», and the last one carried cannot be switched off (`CarriedCurrencies.switched`).
- **«أقصى مبلغ أحمله» (slice 2.15a; CA §10.5, C4, K2):** `maxPickupProvider` (`settings/max_pickup.dart`) reads and sets `GET`/`PUT /v1/captain/settings/max-pickup-amount`. `MaxPickupForm` (`settings/max_pickup_form.dart`) shows the limit («الحد الآن: {المبلغ}» or «بلا حد الآن»), a field in the base currency (`minorOf`, «اكتب مبلغاً أكبر من صفر.»), «حفظ» and «بلا حد»; it sits on «الإعدادات» (apart from the currencies, so one failed read never hides the other) and in the question `askMaxPickupUnanswered` asks at «متصل» while the server says the captain never answered (K2, once: «حفظ» or «بلا حد» answers it; a failed read never holds going online back). The server refuses a change during an order (`409 active_order`, CA §15). An offer with `aboveMaxPickup` shows «المبلغ أكبر من الحد الذي حددته — اقبل فقط إذا كان معك ما يكفي» (CA §8.4). The activation's ask comes with 2.35. Tests: `max_pickup_test.dart`.
- **The pickup's payment:** step 2 pays the server's one proposed line (`pickup.proposedLines`). With more than one currency the restaurant accepts (`pickup.currencies`, each with the whole pickup payment in it), «تقسيم المبلغ أو تعديله» opens one field per currency, the proposal filling its own; the typed lines go with «دفعت للمطعم واستلمت الطلب» and the server checks them (`amount_mismatch`, `currency_not_accepted`). Tests: `pickup_currencies_test.dart`.

## The item-unavailable report (slice 2.9; CA §9 step 2, RA §8.12, §8.10)

- While the active order's `itemReportOpen` (`pickupBlocked`), the at-restaurant step keeps the item check but shows «بانتظار تعديل الطلب من العمليات» in place of the payment and «دفعت للمطعم واستلمت الطلب»; a pickup request refused with `409 item_report_open` (`ItemReportOpen`) says the same. The order events of slice 2.9 (`order.item_reported`, `order.item_report_withdrawn`, `order.item_report_closed`, `order.time_changed`) carry a newer version, so the feed reads the active order again like any event; `alertStep` compares states only, so they add no chime. Slice 2.10a: while `editUnacknowledged` (operations added items and the restaurant has not acknowledged), `pickupBlocked` holds the pickup with «بانتظار تأكيد المطعم للتعديل» (also for `409 edit_unacknowledged`, `EditUnacknowledged`), and an edit before the pickup that changes the amounts or the lines chimes «تم تعديل الطلب — المبلغ الجديد للمطعم {المبلغ}، للتحصيل {المبلغ}» (`OrderEdited`); the item check shows the new lines. Slice 2.10b: after the pickup an edit that changes the lines chimes «حُذفت أصناف من الطلب — المبلغ الجديد للتحصيل {المبلغ}» (`ItemsRemovedAfterPickup`) and one that only lowers the amount to collect «المبلغ الجديد للتحصيل: {المبلغ}» (`CollectLowered`); the door payment then says «تغيّر المبلغ المطلوب إلى …» as before.

## Push (slice 2.3c; CA §14, CA §4.4–4.5; `packages/core_push/CLAUDE.md`)

- `lib/src/push.dart`: `pushTokensProvider` keeps the installation's token registered while signed in (`PUT /v1/captain/push-token`) and `pushTapsProvider` opens a tapped push's target, both watched by `CaptainApp`. `pushTargetPath` maps an offer's or an order's push to home, which shows the open offer or the active order; the other targets come with their slices.
- **The permission (owner decision 2026-11-08):** `Commands.goOnline` asks for it first (`askNotifications`) while the phone does not allow it, on Android only; the answer never blocks going online (CA §4.5: the check only warns). It stands for check 1 of the device check until that exists, and lets the location service's notification show.
- **Channels:** `MainActivity` creates `offer` (the phone's alarm sound on the alarm stream, as the app's own offer ring), `chime` (the notification sound once), `cancellation` (the notification sound until the bundled cancellation sound) and `silent` (low importance, no sound); their names are in `res/values/strings.xml`. A push of an unknown channel shows on `chime`.
- **Tests:** `TestApp` gives `FakePush` (`push`) and `FakeNotificationPermission` (`permission`, allowing unless a test says otherwise); `push_test.dart` proves the token, the permission at «متصل» and the taps.

## Terms (slice 2.4b; AP §9.13, CA §4.8, §7.2, K7)

- `termsCheckProvider` (`lib/src/terms.dart`) follows `apps/customer/CLAUDE.md` «Terms». A pending version shows «وافق على الشروط الجديدة لتتمكن من الاتصال» on home while offline, with a link to `/terms`; «متصل» refused with `terms_not_accepted` («The terms screen», CA §7.2) reads the terms again and opens `/terms`, beside the other blocks' message. A version published while the captain is online or on an order blocks nothing until the next time they go online. ## Emergency stop (slice 2.4d; CA §6.3, §14 #31, §15)

- `emergencyStopProvider` (`lib/src/platform.dart`) is an `EmergencyStopCheck` (`packages/core_platform/CLAUDE.md`) on `GET /v1/captain/emergency-stop` while signed in; `platformBroadcastsProvider` follows `captain:{captain_id}` for it and for `platform.app_changed`, and reads both again whenever the channel connects. `WorkLayer` shows `EmergencyStopBanner` right after the offline bar (CA §6.3 item 2). Each start or end seen while the app runs chimes once (`ringerProvider`). A current order goes on; the server sends no offers meanwhile.
