# apps/captain

The captain app «تطبيق الكابتن» (Android, iOS; architecture §11, §14). It follows the rules of `apps/customer/CLAUDE.md`.

## The work cycle (slice 1.14g; CA §4.3–4.6, §7–§9, §14–§15)

- **Layout:** `lib/src/work/`. `work.dart` holds the state and its pure rules (the step, the open and refused pickup requests) and the feed; `location.dart` the location; `commands.dart` the commands and their refusals; `door_payment.dart` the door payment's pure rules; `alerts.dart` the ring and the chimes; `texts.dart` every string mapping; `offer_sheet.dart`, `order_view.dart`, `payment_form.dart` and `reason_sheet.dart` only show that state and call it (the design stop). `home_screen.dart` shows the availability or the active order, under `WorkLayer`: the offline bar, the offer, the alerts, the hand-in reminder and the summary.
- **State:** `workFeedProvider` is a `LiveFeed` (`packages/core_realtime/CLAUDE.md`) over `captain:{captain_id}` (the id from `GET /v1/captain/auth/session`) whose fetch reads the availability, the open offer and the active order together, so the screens never show one newer than the others; the versions are the active order's. The screens read `workProvider` and never patch it; every command's answer refreshes it.
- **Location (CA §4.3; owner decision 2026-11-07):** `geolocator` for Phase 1, not `flutter_background_geolocation`, whose Android licence waits for the launch. `LocationTracker` runs while the state is online in any way (available, busy, no offers), started and stopped by `WorkLayer` from the state, never while offline. On Android its position stream runs geolocator's foreground service of type `location` with the permanent notification «أنت متصل — FOX DROP يتابع موقعك لتصلك العروض». Points carry the server clock (`ServerClock.offset`), queue in memory (a day at most) and go every 10 seconds, at most 360 per call, oldest first; no answer or a 5xx keeps them, `409 not_online` or a `400` drops the batch. The latest point feeds the arrival radii and every command's fix, taken again at each retry (a fresh position when it is older than 20 seconds). Going online asks for the location permission and a position first, then sends its mock flag (`isMocked`); that position is kept as the latest, never queued, since the captain is still offline. A session that ends (sign-out, another device, a released device) resets the tracker: no more points, the queue dropped, the foreground service stopped. `WorkLayer` ignores the loading states, which still carry the last online state.
- **Commands (CA §4.6, §15):** `Commands` runs one command at a time per subject (`availability`, the offer's id, the order's id), shown as «جارٍ الإرسال…». As in the restaurant app, the `Idempotency-Key` belongs to the command and its body (the fix is left out, as the server's hash does), and a call without an answer, a 5xx, a 408 or a 429 is sent again with the same key after a backoff. `refusal` maps every refusal of CA §15 to a `Notice`, which `texts.dart` explains. The answers of going offline (the hand-in) and delivery (the summary) are kept in `handInProvider` and `summaryProvider` until closed.
- **The offer (CA §8; owner decision 2026-11-07):** it shows and rings while it is open, before its `expiresAt` on the server clock and not answered on this phone (`ringingOffer`), checked every second; past its time the feed is fetched again, so a lost event never leaves it on. The ring is the restaurant app's `PhoneRinger` (the phone's alarm sound on the alarm stream, started again every 10 seconds), and the screen stays on while online (`wakelock_plus`). Back does not leave the app while an offer shows.
- **Time:** every countdown and «منذ» watches `tickProvider` (once a second) and reads `serverClockProvider`; never `DateTime.now()`.
- **Alerts (CA §14):** `alertStep` compares two states the server answered: the offer gone before its time («لم يعد العرض متاحاً», silent) or after it («انتهى العرض»), an order not accepted on this phone («عيّنتك العمليات على طلب»), ready, the pickup confirmed (by operations or not), refused, and disputed after pickup, each with a chime unless silent. Thirty seconds without the channel chime once.
- **The steps (CA §9.1–9.8):** the arrival buttons use the radii the active order sends (`radii`, the settings `captain.merchant_arrival_radius_m` and `captain.customer_radius_m`) against the latest point by the straight line, as the server measures; worse than 100 m the restaurant arrival waits for operations (K3). The item check resets when the restaurant answers «الطلب غير مكتمل». The pickup sends the proposed lines (the base currency only in Phase 1). «وصلت» and «تم التسليم» ask for the away reason first when the phone is beyond the radius, and again when the server measures it so; a reason given at the arrival covers the delivery (K4). The door payment keeps the amount due it showed, and says «تغيّر المبلغ المطلوب إلى …» when the order's changes. It proposes the change only for money received in the base currency, which needs no rate (CA §9.7 item 3: the change is in the base currency); the server checks every other line with the order's rates. A shortfall is the server's to find (a gap within half the largest rounding unit is rounding, AP §8.6): its `shortfall_reason_required` brings the reason list, and «تم التسليم» waits for a reason.
- **Tests:** `work_test.dart` proves the pure rules (the door payment, the refusals, the alerts, the ring, the location queue); `home_test.dart` drives the cycle from going online to the summary with `TestApp` (`test/fixtures.dart`: the JSON as the server sends it, `FakeServer`, `FakeTransport`, `FakeLocation`, `FakeRinger`, a server clock the test moves with `advance`). Widget tests wait with `settle` (the home's one-second tick never lets `pumpAndSettle` finish), tap below the fold with `tapVisible` and end with `done`.
