# apps/merchant

The restaurant app «تطبيق المطعم» (Android, Windows; architecture §11, §12). It follows the rules of `apps/customer/CLAUDE.md`.

## The board (slice 1.14e; RA §4.3–4.7, §8.1–8.11)

- **Layout:** `lib/src/board/`. `board.dart` holds the rules as pure functions (columns, sorting, lateness, the captain line, what rings) and the feed; `commands.dart` the commands; `alerts.dart` the ring and the chimes; `texts.dart` every string mapping; the `*_screen.dart`, `prompts.dart` and `dispute_sheet.dart` files only show that state and call it (the design stop).
- **State:** `boardFeedProvider` is a `LiveFeed` (`packages/core_realtime/CLAUDE.md`) of `GET /v1/merchant/orders` over `merchant:{restaurant_id}`; the screens read `boardProvider` and never patch it. The order detail (`/orders/:id`) reads the same board, so it never shows another version of an order.
- **Time:** every countdown and «منذ» runs on `serverClockProvider` (`packages/core_time/CLAUDE.md`), through `tickProvider` once a second; never `DateTime.now()`.
- **Commands** (`BoardCommands`, RA §4.7): one at a time per order, shown as «جارٍ الإرسال…»; the `Idempotency-Key` belongs to the command and its body, and a call without an answer (no response, a 5xx, a 408 or a 429) is sent again with the same key after a backoff with jitter until the server answers. Any answer refreshes the board. A `409 order_state_changed` is explained from the order it carries (`commandText`). Commands live in memory: the queue that survives a restart (`core_commands`, architecture §11.2) comes later.
- **Ring and chimes** (RA §4.4; owner decisions 2026-11-07): what rings is derived from the board, never from an event: every `placed` order and every open pickup request, oldest first, one sound for all, the prompts stacked. So the ring stops on every device once any device (or the server) handles it. `alertStep` is the pure rule for the chimes and their notices (automatic cancellation from the event's reason, the prep late at its time and every 5 minutes, the captain's arrival and waiting, a prompt closed by another device, 30 seconds offline). `PhoneRinger` plays the phone's alarm sound looping on the alarm stream and its notification sound, through `flutter_ringtone_player`, until the design's bundled sounds; it starts the alarm again every 10 seconds while ringing, since the plugin loops only on Android 9 and later and the system can stop a sound; Windows plays nothing yet. Events wait for the board fetched after them, so a cancellation's chime plays after its prompt closed; a ringing item past its deadline (`autoCancelAt`, `confirmBy`) fetches the board again, so a lost event never leaves a ring on. `BoardAlertsLayer` puts the offline bar and the prompts over the board and the detail alike; Back does not leave the app while something rings, and the ring stops when the Flutter engine detaches, since the plugin's sound would outlive it. The app rings only while it runs, with the screen kept on while the board shows (`wakelock_plus`); the foreground service, full-screen intents and the Windows tray (`core_alerts`, architecture §12.2–12.3) come later.
- **Tests:** `board_test.dart` proves the pure rules; `board_screen_test.dart` drives the screens with `FakeServer`, `FakeTransport` and `FakeRinger` (`test/board_fixtures.dart`, the board's JSON as the server sends it). Widget tests end with `done(tester)`, which disposes the container inside the test so the board's timers stop before Flutter checks for pending timers.

## Reject and operations' decisions (slice 2.7a; RA §8.6, §8.15, §16 #5–6)

- **«رفض»** on the new-order prompt opens `showRejectSheet` (`reject_sheet.dart`): the three reasons of R3 (`RejectReason`), offered while active in Content with Content's labels (`rejectionReasonsProvider` in `lib/src/content.dart`, `GET /v1/merchant/content`), else the built-in labels of `rejectReasonText`; «صنف غير متوفر» picks the order's items (`lines[].itemId`), each «حتى الغد» (the default) or «حتى أعيده»; «المطعم مغلق» shows its note; «ضغط طلبات» offers «الرفض فقط», busy mode with each of the status's `busyExtraOptionsMin`, or «إيقاف 30 د». «تأكيد الرفض» stays off until the choice is complete (`Rejection.complete`). `BoardCommands.reject` sends it like any command; its key belongs to `Rejection.body`. `400 unknown_reason`/`invalid_rejection` is `RejectRefused`, a `409` on a rejected order `RejectedFirst`, on one operations accepted `AcceptedByOperationsFirst`.
- **Operations' decisions:** every order event carries `byOperations`. `alertStep` chimes «رفضت العمليات الطلب #{رقم}: {السبب}» (`RejectedByOperations`, the event's reason code) and «قبلت العمليات الطلب #{رقم} نيابة عنكم — وقت التحضير {د} د» (`AcceptedByOperations`, the prep time from the board fetched after the event); the restaurant's own decision on another device only closes the prompt. The card and the detail show «قبلته العمليات» while `acceptance.byOperations`.
- **Not yet:** the reports' rejection counts (RA §11), history's rejected orders (RA §8.18).

## Push (slice 2.3c; RA §16, RA §4.4–4.5; `packages/core_push/CLAUDE.md`)

- `lib/src/push.dart`: `pushTokensProvider` keeps the installation's token registered while signed in (`PUT /v1/merchant/push-token`), `notificationAskProvider` asks for the permission, and `pushTapsProvider` opens a tapped push's target, all watched by `MerchantApp`. `pushTargetPath` maps an order's push to `/orders/:id`; the other targets (statements, tickets, the menu) come with their slices. A tapped target waits for the session's details (signed in, the device named), so the push that started the app is not lost to the router's start. `main` gives `startPush()`, which is `NoPush` on Windows.
- **The permission (owner decision 2026-11-08):** asked once per installation on Android, at the first sign-in (`notifications_asked` in the device store), with the system's dialog; a phone that already allows notifications is not asked. It stands for check 1 of the device check (RA §4.5) until that exists.
- **Channels:** `MainActivity` creates `ring` (the phone's alarm sound on the alarm stream, as the app's own ring), `chime` (the notification sound once) and `silent` (low importance, no sound); their names are in `res/values/strings.xml`. A push of an unknown channel shows on `chime`.
- **Tests:** `app_test.dart` gives `FakePush`, `FakeNotificationPermission` and `MemoryDeviceStore`; every test container must override `notificationPermissionProvider` and `deviceStoreProvider`, since the real ones call the platform.
- **Not yet:** owner devices with «الرنين على هذا الجهاز» off getting Ring events silently (RA §4.4, with that setting), the owner-only rows (RA §16 #19, #21, #22, #27, #29–33) with their events, the bundled sounds. A push only shows a notification with its channel's sound: it does not wake the app to ring (RA §4.3), which comes with the foreground service (`core_alerts`). A tapped push of an order that left the board (cancelled, rejected) opens a detail with nothing to show until history (RA §8.18).

## Terms (slice 2.4b; AP §9.13, RA §4.10, §5.2 item 3, S13)

- `termsCheckProvider` (`lib/src/terms.dart`) follows `apps/customer/CLAUDE.md` «Terms»; the server answers `pending: false` to a cashier, so a cashier's device is never affected. An owner with a pending version gets `/terms` pushed over the board once per version, on the next open or resume, after the device is named (`termsPromptProvider`, watched by `MerchantApp`), may go back to Orders, and sees «وافق على الشروط الجديدة لتستخدم شاشات المطعم الأخرى. الطلبات تصل كالمعتاد.» on the board; the router sends every other screen to `/terms` while pending (`ownerScreenWaits`: only `/`, `/orders/:id` and `/terms` stay), so each owner screen added later is covered. The board keeps ringing meanwhile. Not yet: the owner web portal's accept screen (RA §4.10 «in the app or the portal», with the portal's sign-in) and the server refusing owner-only routes while pending (none exists besides naming the device, which comes first).

## Emergency stop (slice 2.4d; RA §8.17, §16 #34, §18)

- `emergencyStopProvider` (`lib/src/platform.dart`) is an `EmergencyStopCheck` (`packages/core_platform/CLAUDE.md`) on `GET /v1/merchant/emergency-stop` while signed in; `platformBroadcastsProvider` follows `merchant:{restaurant_id}` for it and for `platform.app_changed`, and reads both again whenever the channel connects. `BoardAlertsLayer` shows `EmergencyStopBanner` right after the offline bar on the board and the detail (RA §6.3 item 2), and the other signed-in screens (the terms, naming the device) get it above them from their route (`WithStopBanner`), so it is on every screen (RA §8.17); signed out there is no stop to show. Each start or end seen while the app runs chimes once on every device (`ringerProvider`). Active orders and the restaurant's own state are untouched.

## Status and hours (slice 2.6a; RA §6.2–6.3, §7, §10, §16 #3, #24, #25)

- **Layout:** `lib/src/status/`. `status.dart` holds the pure rules (the pill, the banners of RA §6.3 items 3 and 6, what the sheet allows, the next change, the notices) and the feed and commands; `hours.dart` the weekly schedule being edited (`WeekDraft`) and the owners' commands; `texts.dart` every string mapping; `status_sheet.dart` and `hours_screen.dart` only show that state and call it (the design stop).
- **State:** `statusFeedProvider` is a `StatusFeed` of `GET /v1/merchant/restaurant/status` over `merchant:{restaurant_id}`, built as `LiveFeed` is but without order versions: it reads at start, after a subscription that did not recover, on every `restaurant.state_changed`, on order events while a maximum counts the active orders, a second after the next moment the status changes on its own (`nextChange`, on the server's clock), and every 30 s while down or failed. A command's answer is shown at once (`answered`). Screens read `statusProvider`.
- **Where it shows:** the pill in the board's top bar opens the status sheet (owners and cashiers); `StatusBanners` sits in `BoardAlertsLayer` after the offline bar and the stop; `/status-and-hours` (the clock button, until the navigation of RA §6.2) shows the status to everyone and the schedule, holiday close, default prep time, maximum active orders and the admin-set fields to owners. Its date and time pickers use the city's clock (`cityUtcOffset`).
- **Chimes:** `statusChimesProvider` (watched by `MerchantApp`) chimes once per auto-pause, pause ended on its own (only when the restaurant takes orders again), busy-mode reminder, and operations' pause (with its reason), resume and end of busy mode (RA §16 #23, slice 2.6b), and the board shows its notice; the push covers a closed app.
- **Tests:** `status_test.dart` proves the pure rules over `status_fixtures.dart`; `status_screen_test.dart` drives the pill, sheet, banners, chime and the hours screen.
- **Not yet:** the sheet's call button to operations (RA §7.2: the restaurant app has no operations number yet), «طلب تعديل» (RA §10, with support tickets), the bottom navigation and the connection dot (RA §6.2), up to two banners with a counter (RA §6.3), the banners' colours (the design).
