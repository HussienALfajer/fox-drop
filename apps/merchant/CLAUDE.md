# apps/merchant

The restaurant app «تطبيق المطعم» (Android, Windows; architecture §11, §12). It follows the rules of `apps/customer/CLAUDE.md`.

## The board (slice 1.14e; RA §4.3–4.7, §8.1–8.11)

- **Layout:** `lib/src/board/`. `board.dart` holds the rules as pure functions (columns, sorting, lateness, the captain line, what rings) and the feed; `commands.dart` the commands; `alerts.dart` the ring and the chimes; `texts.dart` every string mapping; the `*_screen.dart`, `prompts.dart` and `dispute_sheet.dart` files only show that state and call it (the design stop).
- **State:** `boardFeedProvider` is a `LiveFeed` (`packages/core_realtime/CLAUDE.md`) of `GET /v1/merchant/orders` over `merchant:{restaurant_id}`; the screens read `boardProvider` and never patch it. The order detail (`/orders/:id`) reads the same board, so it never shows another version of an order.
- **Time:** every countdown and «منذ» runs on `serverClockProvider` (`packages/core_time/CLAUDE.md`), through `tickProvider` once a second; never `DateTime.now()`.
- **Commands** (`BoardCommands`, RA §4.7): one at a time per order, shown as «جارٍ الإرسال…»; the `Idempotency-Key` belongs to the command and its body, and a call without an answer (no response, a 5xx, a 408 or a 429) is sent again with the same key after a backoff with jitter until the server answers. Any answer refreshes the board. A `409 order_state_changed` is explained from the order it carries (`commandText`). Commands live in memory: the queue that survives a restart (`core_commands`, architecture §11.2) comes later.
- **Ring and chimes** (RA §4.4; owner decisions 2026-11-07): what rings is derived from the board, never from an event: every `placed` order, every open pickup request and every operations' cancellation after acceptance not yet acknowledged, oldest first, one sound for all, the prompts stacked. So the ring stops on every device once any device (or the server) handles it. `alertStep` is the pure rule for the chimes and their notices (automatic cancellation from the event's reason, the prep late at its time and every 5 minutes, the captain's arrival and waiting, a prompt closed by another device, 30 seconds offline). `PhoneRinger` plays the phone's alarm sound looping on the alarm stream and its notification sound, through `flutter_ringtone_player`, until the design's bundled sounds; it starts the alarm again every 10 seconds while ringing, since the plugin loops only on Android 9 and later and the system can stop a sound; Windows plays nothing yet. Events wait for the board fetched after them, so a cancellation's chime plays after its prompt closed; a ringing item past its deadline (`autoCancelAt`, `confirmBy`) fetches the board again, so a lost event never leaves a ring on. `BoardAlertsLayer` puts the offline bar and the prompts over the board and the detail alike; Back does not leave the app while something rings, and the ring stops when the Flutter engine detaches, since the plugin's sound would outlive it. The app rings only while it runs, with the screen kept on while the board shows (`wakelock_plus`); the foreground service, full-screen intents and the Windows tray (`core_alerts`, architecture §12.2–12.3) come later.
- **Tests:** `board_test.dart` proves the pure rules; `board_screen_test.dart` drives the screens with `FakeServer`, `FakeTransport` and `FakeRinger` (`test/board_fixtures.dart`, the board's JSON as the server sends it). Widget tests end with `done(tester)`, which disposes the container inside the test so the board's timers stop before Flutter checks for pending timers.

## Reject and operations' decisions (slice 2.7a; RA §8.6, §8.15, §16 #5–6)

- **«رفض»** on the new-order prompt opens `showRejectSheet` (`reject_sheet.dart`): the three reasons of R3 (`RejectReason`), offered while active in Content with Content's labels (`rejectionReasonsProvider` in `lib/src/content.dart`, `GET /v1/merchant/content` through `merchantContentProvider`, which a failed read invalidates), else the built-in labels of `rejectReasonText`; «صنف غير متوفر» picks the order's items (`lines[].itemId`), each «حتى الغد» (the default) or «حتى أعيده»; «المطعم مغلق» shows its note; «ضغط طلبات» offers «الرفض فقط», busy mode with each of the status's `busyExtraOptionsMin`, or «إيقاف 30 د». «تأكيد الرفض» stays off until the choice is complete (`Rejection.complete`). `BoardCommands.reject` sends it like any command; its key belongs to `Rejection.body`. `400 unknown_reason`/`invalid_rejection` is `RejectRefused`, a `409` on a rejected order `RejectedFirst`, on one operations accepted `AcceptedByOperationsFirst`.
- **Operations' decisions:** every order event carries `byOperations`. `alertStep` chimes «رفضت العمليات الطلب #{رقم}: {السبب}» (`RejectedByOperations`, the event's reason code) and «قبلت العمليات الطلب #{رقم} نيابة عنكم — وقت التحضير {د} د» (`AcceptedByOperations`, the prep time from the board fetched after the event); the restaurant's own decision on another device only closes the prompt. The card and the detail show «قبلته العمليات» while `acceptance.byOperations`.
- **Not yet:** the reports' rejection counts (RA §11), history's rejected orders (RA §8.18).

## Operations' cancellation (slice 2.8b; RA §8.14, §18)

- An order operations cancelled after acceptance stays on the board with `cancellation` (`at`, the reason's code and label, `byOperations`) until «حسناً» on any device; the server lists it for 24 hours at most and never gives the note, the responsible party or the money. `ringing` turns it into `CancelledRinging`, so it rings with the others and stops on every device once the board drops it; `CancelledPrompt` shows «أوقف التحضير — أُلغي الطلب #{رقم}: {السبب}» (the label) and «حسناً», which sends `BoardCommands.acknowledgeCancellation` (`POST /v1/merchant/orders/{orderId}/acknowledge-cancellation`, with a key; a `409` says nothing, since the board fetched after it drops the order). It has no deadline: only «حسناً» ends it.
- `alertStep`: an `order.cancelled` with `byOperations` whose order rings on the fetched board adds no «أُلغي الطلب #{رقم}» notice (the prompt says it); a placed order operations cancelled keeps that chime. The pickup request the cancellation voids closes without `PickupClosed`. A cancelled order never chimes «وصل الكابتن» or «الكابتن ينتظر الطلب…», even when a board carries its `captainArrival` (the server sends it only for an accepted or ready order). `order.cancellation_acknowledged` only refreshes the board.

## The item-unavailable report and operations' time change (slice 2.9; RA §8.7, §8.10, §8.12, §16 #12, #37)

- **«صنف غير متوفر — اتصلوا بالزبون»** is on the detail while `canReportItems` (an accepted or ready order, no pickup request open, no report open). It opens `showItemReportSheet` (`item_report_sheet.dart`), which shares the reject sheet's item picker (`UnavailableItemsPicker`, `items_picker.dart`: each item «حتى الغد» by default or «حتى أعيده») and adds the optional note from Content's `item_unavailable_note` list (`itemReportNotesProvider` in `lib/src/content.dart`, which shares one read of `GET /v1/merchant/content` with `rejectionReasonsProvider` through `merchantContentProvider`), sending its code; when the list could not be read, the built-in labels of its seeded codes (`itemReportNoteLabels`: `similar_alternative` «يوجد بديل مشابه», `insufficient_quantity` «الكمية غير كافية»), and the failed read is read again on the next report. The detail shows the note's `label` as the server kept it. «إرسال البلاغ» stays off until an item is picked (`ItemReport.complete`); `BoardCommands.reportItems` sends `POST /v1/merchant/orders/{orderId}/item-report` with a key bound to `ItemReport.body`.
- While the board's `itemReport` is set, the card and the detail show «صنف نفد — بانتظار العمليات» and «العمليات تتواصل مع الزبون» (the detail also names the items), and the detail offers «تراجع — الصنف متوفر» (`canWithdrawItemReport`, `BoardCommands.withdrawItemReport`, `POST .../item-report/withdraw`). Refusals: `400 invalid_item_report` is `ItemReportRefused`, and so is `400 unknown_reason` (a note removed from Content), which also reads Content again; a report's `409` with the pickup request open is `ReportWhilePickupOpen`, with another device's report open `ItemReportOpenFirst`; a withdrawal's `409` without a report is `ItemReportClosedFirst`.
- **Chimes (`alertStep`):** `order.time_changed` with `prep_time_changed` chimes «عدّلت العمليات وقت تحضير #{رقم} إلى {د} د» (`PrepTimeChanged`, the prep time from the board fetched after the event) and starts the late-prep chime again from the new prep time; `promised_time_changed` says nothing to the restaurant. `order.item_report_closed` chimes «أغلقت العمليات بلاغ الصنف في الطلب #{رقم}: {السبب}» (`ItemReportClosed`, operations' text from the event). `order.item_reported` and `order.item_report_withdrawn` only refresh the board.

## Edits by operations (slice 2.10a; RA §8.13, §8.10, §16 #16–17)

- The board's `edits` are operations' edits not yet acknowledged, oldest first, each with its changes («أُضيف: …», «حُذف: …», «{الصنف}: {قبل} ← {بعد}», `editChangeText`), the new items value and the extra minutes of a ready order sent back to preparing. `editRings` (one added items) makes `ringing` give `EditRinging`, before the new-order prompt of a placed order, so it rings until acknowledged with `EditPrompt` («تم تعديل الطلب #{رقم} — أُضيفت أصناف»); an edit that added nothing chimes «تم تعديل الطلب #{رقم}» on its `order.edited` (reason `changed`, `OrderEdited`) and stays on the card and the detail with the same prompt. «اطلعت على التعديل» sends `BoardCommands.acknowledgeEdit` (`POST /v1/merchant/orders/{orderId}/acknowledge-edit` with `through`, the newest edit's `at` the prompt showed, so an edit that arrived since keeps ringing; a `409` says nothing, since the board fetched after it shows what is left). It has no deadline.

## Push (slice 2.3c; RA §16, RA §4.4–4.5; `packages/core_push/CLAUDE.md`)

- `lib/src/push.dart`: `pushTokensProvider` keeps the installation's token registered while signed in (`PUT /v1/merchant/push-token`), `notificationAskProvider` asks for the permission, and `pushTapsProvider` opens a tapped push's target, all watched by `MerchantApp`. `pushTargetPath` maps an order's push to `/orders/:id` a statement's or receipt's to its screen under «الحساب» (slice 2.19e) and the overdue reminder to «الحساب» (2.19f); a ticket's opens `/support` (slice 2.24b); the menu's comes with its slice. A tapped target waits for the session's details (signed in, the device named), so the push that started the app is not lost to the router's start. `main` gives `startPush()`, which is `NoPush` on Windows.
- **The permission (owner decision 2026-11-08):** asked once per installation on Android, at the first sign-in (`notifications_asked` in the device store), with the system's dialog; a phone that already allows notifications is not asked. It stands for check 1 of the device check (RA §4.5) until that exists.
- **Channels:** `MainActivity` creates `ring` (the phone's alarm sound on the alarm stream, as the app's own ring), `chime` (the notification sound once) and `silent` (low importance, no sound); their names are in `res/values/strings.xml`. A push of an unknown channel shows on `chime`.
- **Tests:** `app_test.dart` gives `FakePush`, `FakeNotificationPermission` and `MemoryDeviceStore`; every test container must override `notificationPermissionProvider` and `deviceStoreProvider`, since the real ones call the platform.
- **Not yet:** owner devices with «الرنين على هذا الجهاز» off getting Ring events silently (RA §4.4, with that setting), the owner-only rows (RA §16 #19, #21, #22, #27, #33) with their events, the bundled sounds. A push only shows a notification with its channel's sound: it does not wake the app to ring (RA §4.3), which comes with the foreground service (`core_alerts`). A tapped push of an order that left the board (cancelled, rejected) opens a detail with nothing to show until history (RA §8.18).

## Terms (slice 2.4b; AP §9.13, RA §4.10, §5.2 item 3, S13)

- `termsCheckProvider` (`lib/src/terms.dart`) follows `apps/customer/CLAUDE.md` «Terms»; the server answers `pending: false` to a cashier, so a cashier's device is never affected. An owner with a pending version gets `/terms` pushed over the board once per version, on the next open or resume, after the device is named (`termsPromptProvider`, watched by `MerchantApp`), may go back to Orders, and sees «وافق على الشروط الجديدة لتستخدم شاشات المطعم الأخرى. الطلبات تصل كالمعتاد.» on the board; the router sends every other screen to `/terms` while pending (`ownerScreenWaits`: only `/`, `/orders/:id` and `/terms` stay), so each owner screen added later is covered. The board keeps ringing meanwhile. Not yet: the owner web portal's accept screen (RA §4.10 «in the app or the portal», with the portal's sign-in) and the server refusing owner-only routes while pending (none exists besides naming the device, which comes first).

## Emergency stop (slice 2.4d; RA §8.17, §16 #34, §18)

- `emergencyStopProvider` (`lib/src/platform.dart`) is an `EmergencyStopCheck` (`packages/core_platform/CLAUDE.md`) on `GET /v1/merchant/emergency-stop` while signed in; `platformBroadcastsProvider` follows `merchant:{restaurant_id}` for it and for `platform.app_changed`, and reads both again whenever the channel connects. `BoardAlertsLayer` shows `EmergencyStopBanner` right after the offline bar on the board and the detail (RA §6.3 item 2), and the other signed-in screens (the terms, naming the device) get it above them from their route (`WithStopBanner`), so it is on every screen (RA §8.17); signed out there is no stop to show. Each start or end seen while the app runs chimes once on every device (`ringerProvider`). Active orders and the restaurant's own state are untouched.

## Status and hours (slice 2.6a; RA §6.2–6.3, §7, §10, §16 #3, #24, #25)

- **Layout:** `lib/src/status/`. `status.dart` holds the pure rules (the pill, the banners of RA §6.3 items 3 and 6, what the sheet allows, the next change, the notices) and the feed and commands; `hours.dart` the weekly schedule being edited (`WeekDraft`) and the owners' commands; `texts.dart` every string mapping; `status_sheet.dart` and `hours_screen.dart` only show that state and call it (the design stop).
- **State:** `statusFeedProvider` is a `StatusFeed` of `GET /v1/merchant/restaurant/status` over `merchant:{restaurant_id}`, built as `LiveFeed` is but without order versions: it reads at start, after a subscription that did not recover, on every `restaurant.state_changed`, on order events while a maximum counts the active orders, a second after the next moment the status changes on its own (`nextChange`, on the server's clock), and every 30 s while down or failed. A command's answer is shown at once (`answered`). Screens read `statusProvider`.
- **Where it shows:** the pill in the board's top bar opens the status sheet (owners and cashiers); `StatusBanners` sits in `BoardAlertsLayer` after the offline bar and the stop; `/status-and-hours` (the clock button, until the navigation of RA §6.2) shows the status to everyone and the schedule, holiday close, default prep time, maximum active orders and the admin-set fields to owners. Its date and time pickers use the city's clock (`cityUtcOffset`).
- **Chimes:** `statusChimesProvider` (watched by `MerchantApp`) chimes once per auto-pause, pause ended on its own (only when the restaurant takes orders again), busy-mode reminder, and operations' pause (with its reason), resume and end of busy mode (RA §16 #23, slice 2.6b), and the board shows its notice; the push covers a closed app.
- **Tests:** `status_test.dart` proves the pure rules over `status_fixtures.dart`; `status_screen_test.dart` drives the pill, sheet, banners, chime and the hours screen.
- **Not yet:** the sheet's call button to operations (RA §7.2: the restaurant app has no operations number yet), «طلب تعديل» (RA §10, with support tickets), the bottom navigation and the connection dot (RA §6.2), up to two banners with a counter (RA §6.3), the banners' colours (the design).

## «الحساب» and the order's owner lines (slice 2.19d; RA §8.4, §12.1–12.2, §12.5, R10)

- `lib/src/account/`, owners only: the board's wallet button (`open-account`, beside the gear, shown only to an owner, until the navigation of RA §6.2) opens `/account`, which the terms' wait covers. `accountProvider` reads `GET /v1/merchant/account` on opening and on «تحديث» (auto-disposed); a `403` says «الحساب للمالكين فقط.» with no retry, any other failure «تعذّر تحميل الحساب.» with «إعادة المحاولة». `account.dart` holds the pure parts (`daysOf` groups the week's flat lines by their day in the server's order, `percentOf` shows basis points as «15» or «12.5», `causeLabel` gives each exception's label of RA §12.5, the correction as «تسوية يدوية: {السبب}», `balanceText`, `settlementText`); `account_screen.dart` only shows them: the balance card («المستحق عليك حتى الآن», «لك عند FOX DROP» below zero, «لا مستحقات عليك الآن.» at zero) with «موعد التسوية: {اليوم} {التاريخ}», the current rate and a scheduled change («تتغيّر عمولتك إلى…»), «قبضت اليوم من الكباتن» with each pickup (order, time, captain, the currency lines), and «هذا الأسبوع» by day: each order's commission and restaurant-funded discount, each exception with its order and whether it raises or lowers what is owed, each payment.
- **The detail (RA §8.4):** the board's `owner` adds «العمولة {النسبة}% — تُسدَّد يوم السبت» and «صافيك من الطلب», or «طلب تجريبي — لا عمولة», under the items value; a cashier's board carries none. Every user sees «طلب تجريبي» on a test order's card and detail (`testOrder`, RA §8.16; slice 2.30b).
- **«الكشوف» and «الإيصالات» (slice 2.19e; RA §12.3–12.5, §16 #30–#31):** «الحساب» links to `/account/statements` and `/account/receipts`, each detail nested under its list (so Back goes to the list, then «الحساب»), all covered by the terms' wait. `records.dart` holds the providers (each read while its screen shows, auto-disposed), the pure parts (`statusOf` by the status's name, `linesOf` splits a statement's flat lines into orders, exceptions and payments, `failureOf`: a `403` says «الكشوف والإيصالات للمالكين فقط.» and a `404`/`400` «لم يُعثر على …» with no retry, anything else «تعذّر التحميل.» with «إعادة المحاولة») and the texts; `records_screen.dart` only shows them. The statement shows the panel's printed labels: each order with the currency lines the captain paid, its commission and restaurant-funded discount, the exceptions with their order, label (`causeNameLabel`, shared with «الحساب»), direction and «من قرّر: {الموظف}» (or «تلقائياً» for the system), the payments, the totals down to «المجموع المستحق», the status and what is unpaid. «مشاركة كصورة» shares a PNG of the statement through `share_plus` (`imageSharerProvider`, as the captain's; an image only, owner decision 2026-11-16; PDF and Excel with the portal, 2.45), rendered at the screen's pixel ratio lowered so its longest side stays within 8192 pixels (`imageRatio`), so a busy week's statement still renders on Android. «الحساب»'s week shows «من قرّر» on its exceptions too (`deciderNameText`). The receipts list sits under the safety note of RA §12.4; a receipt shows its number, time, method, staff member, each currency line with its base value, the total and the balance before and after. `pushTargetPath` opens `statement` (`statementId`) and `receipt` (`receiptId`) pushes there.
- **The overdue settlement (slice 2.19f; RA §6.3 item 7, §12.2, §12.6, §16 #32):** «الحساب»'s `overdue` shows «متأخر: {المبلغ} من كشف {التاريخ}» under the balance (`account-overdue`; red with the design). `overdueProvider` (`account/overdue.dart`) reads `GET /v1/merchant/account/overdue` for an owner only (null for a cashier or signed out, with no request), at start, on a resume a minute or more after the last read (`overdueResumeGap`: Windows resumes on every focus change) and every `overdueRefresh` (15 minutes), since a payment at the desk or a statement past its days sends no event the app follows; «تحديث» on «الحساب» reads it again too. `OverdueBanner` shows «لديك مبلغ متأخر: {المبلغ}. يرجى التسوية في أقرب وقت.» in `BoardAlertsLayer` after `StatusBanners` (item 7 after items 3 and 6; amber with the design); a failed read keeps the last answer. The reminder push (`target: account`) opens «الحساب». The restaurant is never paused for it.

## Support (slice 2.24b; RA §15, §8.19, §16 #29; owner decisions 2026-11-17)

- `lib/src/support/`: `support.dart` holds the providers and the commands, `report_photo.dart` the photo (as the customer app's: picked scaled to 1600 pixels, its type by its signature, `POST /v1/merchant/uploads/report-photo` then the PUT to the storage, the camera on Android only), and the screens only show them. The board's `open-support` button (everyone, until the navigation of RA §6.2) opens `/support`: «اتصال بالعمليات» and «واتساب العمليات» (`contactTiles`, the numbers of `merchantAuth.contact`; a missing one hides its button), «إبلاغ عن مشكلة», «بلاغاتي» (`myReportsProvider`, `GET /v1/merchant/reports`, read while the screen shows and again after a report: each ticket's number and type, the data change's subject, the order, the note, «من مستخدم آخر» or «من العمليات» on an owner's list (`openedBy`), the status of AP Appendix C and «الرد: …») and the FAQ (`merchantFaqProvider`, from `GET /v1/merchant/content`, which `merchantContentProvider` now answers whole). `/support/report` takes a type of `merchant_ticket_type`, the subject for «طلب تعديل بيانات» (`DataChangeSubject`; «إرسال البلاغ» stays off until it is chosen), optionally a board order, a note and one photo. The order detail's «مشكلة في الطلب» (`showOrderProblem`, every order) offers the call, WhatsApp with «بخصوص الطلب #{رقم}» and the quick reasons of `merchant_problem`; one tap sends it, after asking the text of a reason Content requires it for (`Reason.noteRequired`, as the report's «إرسال البلاغ» waits for the note). `MerchantReports` keeps a key per body until the server answers, so sending the same report again after a lost answer replays it; `unknown_reason` and the subject's refusals read Content again, a photo's refusal uploads it again on the next send. A `ticket` push («تم حل بلاغك», to the owners and the reporter) opens `/support`.

## «القائمة» (slice 2.32a; RA §6.1 #3, §9.1–9.4, §9.6–9.7, §18)

- `lib/src/menu/`: `menu.dart` holds the state, the commands and the pure parts (`quickRows`, `moved`, `typedOf`, `deltaOfTyped`, `basisPointsOf`, `changesMenu`); `texts.dart` the string mappings; the `*_screen.dart` files only show them (the design stop). The board's floating button (`open-menu`, everyone, until the navigation of RA §6.2: the top bar's actions already fill a phone) opens `/menu`, with `quick-edit`, `bulk-price`, `price-log` and `preview` under it; the terms' wait covers them.
- **State:** `menuProvider` (`MenuNotifier`, auto-disposed) reads `GET /v1/merchant/menu` on opening and again after a subscription that did not recover and on every `restaurant.state_changed` of kind `menu_changed`, `price_change` or `price_change_bulk` on `merchant:{restaurant_id}`, so every device shows what the last one published; each command's answer (the menu) is shown at once, the availability switch's (only the switch) by a read.
- **Everyone (RA §6):** each category, item and option with its own switch («متوفر», «متوقف حتى الغد», «متوقف حتى أعيده»), «غير متوفر للزبائن» for an item off through its category, window or a required group, the window and «مخفي عن الزبائن»; switching off asks «حتى الغد» or «حتى أعيده». «تعديل سريع» (search by name, filter by category; an option shared by several items shows once) has the switch for everyone.
- **Owners:** «تعديل السعر» on each item and option (the field in major units, `minorOfTyped`; a delta may be 0), always with «تذكير: سعر التطبيق يجب ألا يزيد على سعر المحل»; «إخفاء»/«إظهار»; up and down for categories and items (until the design's drag); «فترة التوفر» with the system's time pickers on the minutes of the city's day, or «طوال اليوم»; «تعديل جماعي للأسعار» (category or the whole menu, percentage or amount, up or down, «معاينة قبل وبعد» then «تأكيد التعديل», off while a price would reach 0; its key belongs to the body until the server answers); «سجل الأسعار» (the user's name or «FOX DROP»); «معاينة كما يراها الزبون» (unavailable items faded with «يتوفر غداً», «متوفر من {الساعة}» or «غير متوفر حالياً»). The cashier sees none of these (the server answers them `403 owner_only`).
- **Tests:** `menu_screen_test.dart`.
- **Change requests (slice 2.32b; RA §9.2 «After admin review», §9.5, §18):** `request_forms.dart` holds the forms and `PendingLines`. The menu's answer carries each row's `pending` (a request «بانتظار المراجعة»: `pendingText` says what it asks: «الاسم المطلوب: …», «الوصف المطلوب: …», «صورة جديدة بانتظار المراجعة؛ تبقى الصورة الحالية حتى الموافقة», a group's choices and items) and the new rows waiting for review (`isNew`: «جديد — لا يراه الزبائن قبل الموافقة», no switch, price, hiding, order or window; `publishedIds` keeps them out of the reorder and `quickRows` out of «تعديل سريع»), and `optionGroups`, shown by `OptionGroupsSection` («مجموعات الخيارات»). Everyone sees the badge; owners get «سحب الطلب» on each request and the forms, each answering the menu: «إضافة تصنيف» (the app bar) and «تعديل الاسم» on a category, «إضافة صنف» (name, description, price with the parity reminder, a photo) and «تعديل الطلب» on a new item, «تعديل الاسم والوصف» and «تغيير الصورة» on a published one, «إضافة مجموعة خيارات» and «تعديل المجموعة» (name, minimum and maximum, the items ticked, the options; a published option's delta is read only, since it changes through «تعديل السعر»). Each form starts from the pending values (`pendingOf`), since sending a published value again withdraws its request. A new row's id comes from `newMenuIdProvider` (`uuidV4`; tests give their own), so a request sent again changes the same row. A photo is picked as the report's (`photoPickerProvider`, the camera or the gallery on Android, a file on Windows), uploaded once (`ItemPhoto`, `POST /v1/merchant/uploads/item-photo`, then the PUT through `photoPutProvider` to `/public-media/incoming/` on the API's host) and sent by its upload id. A refusal says «قُرّر هذا الطلب أو سُحب.», «تعذّر رفع الصورة، حاول مرة أخرى.» or «تحقق من الحقول وحاول مرة أخرى.» (`refusalText(…, request: true)`). A review's decision (`menu_change_approved`, `menu_change_rejected`) reads the menu again; its silent push reaches the owners.
- **Not yet:** the menu's push target (`pushTargetPath`), the drag, showing the photos themselves (the design), the photo crop before review (the design), a menu without any price yet (onboarding never leaves one) has no «إضافة صنف» since the base currency comes from the menu's prices.
